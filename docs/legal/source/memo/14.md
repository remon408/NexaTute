# 14. Takedowns, DMCA, Repeat Infringers and Law-Enforcement Requests

Covers complaints about content (copyright, likeness, defamation, brands, child safety), the US DMCA safe harbour, the repeat-infringer policy, and requests from police, regulators and courts. Applies to Motion Studio, NexaTute user and institution uploads, and NexaLogicDev sites.

## 14.1 Facts

- Report buttons everywhere; verified complaints taken down within 24 hours, child abuse and phishing immediately; an unauthorised clone or face complaint suspends the account during review.
- Ladder: warning, suspension, ban; logged reasons; appeal; a person decides. Repeat infringers are suspended, then closed (record period to confirm).

## 14.3 Our proposed position

1. One complaint form per product (copyright, likeness, defamation, trademark, privacy, child safety, other) in Bangla and English, plus email [to insert]; a valid notice states the complainant, content, right claimed, good faith and accuracy.
2. Content disabled within 24 hours of a valid notice (immediately for child safety, phishing and intimate images); the uploader told why and how to respond unless the law or a child-safety report forbids it. Counter-notices restore content after the DMCA waiting period (US copyright) or after review (other complaints), unless legal action is shown. Repeated bad-faith complainants blocked after review.
3. DMCA agent registered in RemonLogic Private Limited's name with a role address and a Bangladesh street address or a third-party agent [to insert]; same details on both websites; 3-year renewal in the compliance calendar.
4. Repeat infringers (default, editable): 1 valid notice in 12 months = removal and warning; 2 = 7-day suspension of uploads and publishing; 3 = account closed, with unused prepaid amounts handled as the refund policy says [lawyer to confirm whether they may be withheld]. A successful counter-notice removes a strike. Unauthorised cloning, child sexual content or fraud = immediate closure. Takedown records kept 3 years after the last strike (default, editable).
5. Child sexual abuse material: automatic block without human viewing; account closed; evidence preserved in a restricted store; report to the authority the lawyer names, and to NCMEC or the IWF where available; ask NCMEC in writing whether a Bangladeshi company can register.
6. Law-enforcement requests: one legal-requests address and log; disclosure only on a written request citing a legal power from a verified authority; minimum data; lawyer review for non-emergencies; verified emergencies involving danger to life or a child answered at once, with documents afterwards; users told unless forbidden or unsafe; blocking orders applied in Bangladesh by geo-blocking where content is lawful elsewhere; foreign requests handled through the data holder or mutual legal assistance after review; a yearly counts-only transparency report after launch (default, editable).
