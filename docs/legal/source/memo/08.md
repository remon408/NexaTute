# 8. Children

Rules on children in NexaTute, PlayBoom and Motion Studio (data-law points in chapters 6 and 7).

## 8.1 Facts

- Many NexaTute learners are under 18; PlayBoom serves young children. Owner decision (30 October 2026): the NexaTute Free Beta starts adult-only; children join only after incorporation and the Free Beta items (chapter 15). Age check: date of birth plus guardian OTP; the guardian decides every consent switch.
- PlayBoom: guardian account with child profiles and PIN; no face templates; only the guardian buys; the AI Play Buddy says it is an AI.
- NexaTute minors: face verification with guardian consent; no private adult–child chat; no ads or trackers; batch recordings (when one member asks) include other learners' question audio; app-store purchases only with guardian approval.
- Live Quran teachers, mentors and institution teachers may meet children.
- Motion Studio content: child-character voices only from adult artists or synthetic voices, never a real child's; child characters trigger kids-safe filters and "Made for kids"; zero tolerance for child sexual content.
- Motion Studio users (owner, 30 October 2026): NexaTute's age rules. Under-18s use a profile under a guardian's account; the guardian pays and approves each purchase; kids-safe forced on; no clone, twin, face acting, bulk sends, API, selling or publishing without the guardian; US under-13s blocked; cross-product offers 18+ only.
- Protected settings (owner, 30 October 2026): child-safety, consent and never-list settings change only with 3 approvals and a 24-hour delay, logged, with rollback; the AI Co-Admin never approves.

## 8.3 Our proposed position

1. The guardian is the contracting party and buyer for every under-18 user; the child is a profile with a child-friendly explanation (assent, not consent).
2. Guardian consent per feature, recorded with version and time; OTP plus an adult declaration unless the lawyer requires more. Institutions confirm in their agreement that they hold guardian consent.
3. No profiling, engagement intelligence or personalised or cross-product offers for under-18s in either product; face verification off for them (chapter 6). In the shared marketplace, under-18 profiles see only NexaTute learning items, each bought with guardian approval.
4. Batch recordings: learners whose guardians have not consented are not captured (audio muted, name hidden) (default, editable).
5. Staff and teachers with access to children: safeguarding code, identity and reference checks, police verification where lawful [lawyer to confirm], logged sessions, no off-platform contact.
6. Showcases and leaderboards show first name and initial only unless the guardian agrees (default, editable).
7. Motion Studio under-18 mode as in 8.1; minimum age 13; Co-Director minor mode (no "any subject" chat, crisis alerts to the guardian); before the first clone or twin, an age-assurance check beyond a typed date of birth (ID check with the image deleted, or an adult payment-instrument check) (all default, editable).
8. Real children in Motion Studio uploads: only in the client's own real footage, with the client's written warranty of guardian consent; real children's faces or voices are never animated, cloned, twinned or altered by AI (default, editable) [lawyer to confirm].
9. Child sexual abuse material: blocked by hash matching and classifiers; account closed; evidence preserved without staff viewing; reported to the authority the lawyer names; no notice to the account holder.
10. A protected setting quoted in a legal page takes effect only after the page is updated and the notice period has run; the urgent path may only tighten.

## 8.4 Risks if wrong

- Void contracts with minors (refunds, unenforceable terms); children's-data offences.
- Harm to a child through staff or users; AI sexual content involving a child if controls fail; family claims over a real child's likeness.
