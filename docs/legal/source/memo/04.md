# 4. Tax and VAT

Covers VAT on Bangladeshi sales, Mushak forms, VAT deducted at source (VDS), reverse-charge VAT on foreign bills, the income-tax exemption for IT-enabled services, withholding tax on payments abroad and Paddle as merchant of record. For the lawyer and the chartered accountant together; the owner has ruled that VAT rates are set by the chartered accountant.

## 4.1 Facts

- Bangladesh prices include VAT. Working position in both blueprints: 15/115 on aamarPay sales; Mushak invoices for business clients; 0% Bangladesh VAT on Paddle sales as an export of services (accountant to confirm). The rate is chosen per product tag in the admin panel.
- Foreign costs: servers, Cloudflare, cloud GPU, APIs, certificates, domains, messaging, stock media, app-store and Paddle fees. Business buyers include schools, agencies and limited companies. The company pays affiliates, resellers, marketplace sellers, counsellors, artists and reviewers.

## 4.2 Applicable law

| Point | What the research found | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| VAT and SD Act, 2012 — rates (as amended by the Finance Act 2026; Act number unverified) | Standard 15%. IT-enabled services (software, hosting, digital content, animation, data processing) 5% with 5% VDS. "Television and online program producer" 15% VDS (may affect Motion Studio). Online education rate not confirmed (15%, or 5% if ITES). Proposed 0% VAT for qualifying startups to 30 June 2035 not confirmed | High / medium / low |
| Registration | Mandatory above Tk 50 lakh turnover; turnover tax 4% between Tk 30 and 50 lakh; exporters must register whatever their turnover; exports of services are zero-rated | High |
| Reverse charge | 15% VAT on imported services (servers, cloud); the bank deposits it by a-challan when remitting; input credit where allowed; s.20 rewritten from 1 July 2026. Banks also collect 15% VAT on some card payments for online services | High / medium |
| Mushak forms; VDS | 6.3 invoice at supply; 6.6 VDS certificate; 6.7/6.8 credit and debit notes; 9.1 monthly return. VDS applies when the buyer is a limited company, bank or educational institution | High / medium |
| Income Tax Act, 2023 | ITES income, including "Software as a service (SaaS)" and "E-learning platform and e-publication", exempt 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2027, with returns, a yearly certificate and possibly BASIS membership (cashless condition not confirmed); about one tax year of benefit here. Corporate rate 27.5%, or 25% if all transactions go through banks | High / medium |
| Withholding on payments abroad (s.163) | Technical services 10%; royalties and software licences 20%; digital marketing 10%; other payments (possibly cloud) 20%; reducible under the US and Netherlands treaties | Medium |

## 4.3 Our proposed position

1. Register for VAT (BIN) right after incorporation, before any sale or export receipt.
2. Keep 15% VAT-inclusive pricing until the accountant confirms a lower rate in writing (a lower rate later raises margin; it creates no exposure).
3. Mushak-6.3 per sale, 6.7 per refund, monthly return, VDS certificates stored. Time of supply for prepaid amounts follows chapter 2: under Option A each purchase is a supply; for any prepaid plan or pack, VAT at payment unless the accountant confirms otherwise.
4. Foreign bills paid through one AD bank deducting reverse-charge VAT and withholding; budget assumes no gross-up; treaty rates where possible.
5. Apply for the ITES exemption (and BASIS membership if required); bank channels only, for the 25% rate; plan for the exemption ending 30 June 2027.
6. Paddle: Paddle sells to the foreign customer as merchant of record; RemonLogic's supply to Paddle is treated as a zero-rated export, repatriated through the bank (chapter 5) [lawyer to confirm].
7. Before Paddle, aamarPay card sales make RemonLogic the seller to foreign consumers; EU and UK consumer sales may create VAT duties there from the first sale (general knowledge) [international lawyer to confirm]. EU and UK billing addresses are blocked until Paddle (default, editable).

## 4.4 Risks if wrong

- Underpaid VAT with interest and penalties on every sale; lost zero rating without a BIN; refused remittances and disallowed costs; 25–27.5% tax if the ITES exemption is lost; foreign VAT on direct card sales.
