# 3. Consumer Protection and Digital Commerce

Covers delivery, refund, renewal, price-change and marketing promises to customers in Bangladesh, measured against the Consumers' Right Protection Act 2009 and the Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines 2021 (DCOG). Applies to both products.

## 3.1 Facts

- NexaTute: monthly plans, prepay packages, Daily Pass, add-ons, learning-guarantee terms; paid from 1 January 2027. Motion Studio: packages, pre-production fee, per-minute rates at storyboard approval; Express (+20%, shown ETA), Normal (12 hours), Slow (24 hours); Islamic review up to 7 days, uncharged until approval.
- One-click cancel, proration, 7 days' notice of price rises (default, editable); app-store purchases +30% on a separate line.
- Refunds: fresh purchase 0–30 days, 100% of the unused portion (Motion Studio package fee only if no video was made that cycle); 31+ days none; technical failures, outages, wrong or duplicate charges refunded or credited; Motion Studio shorter, failed or late videos refunded to the wallet automatically; refund channel "original method or wallet" open.
- Adopted marketing rules: no fake reviews or numbers, no scarcity wording, no "No. 1" claims, no AI testimonials.
- Legal pages: NexaTute in six languages, Motion Studio in English with a summary; English prevails in both.

## 3.2 Applicable law

| Law | Status and key point | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Consumers' Right Protection Act, 2009 (Act No. 26 of 2009) | In force; no 2025–26 amendment found. Offences: misleading advertisement (reported s.44), not supplying promised services (s.45), negligent service harming safety (s.53). DNCRP complaints within 30 days (s.60); fines partly paid to complainants | Medium; low on sections |
| DCOG 2021 (Ministry of Commerce, 4 July 2021, amended 2021) | Operative as far as found; covers goods and services sold online. Display trade licence, VAT number, TIN, DBID; refund, return and delivery terms in Bangla; checkbox consent before collecting data | Medium; SaaS applicability not confirmed |
| DCOG — delivery and refunds | Hand-over within 48 hours; delivery within 5 or 10 days. For delay after advance payment, full refund within 10 days through the same payment channel. If the seller cannot supply: notice within 48 hours, refund within 72 hours of the notice | Medium |
| DCOG — other | 100% advance only for ready-to-ship goods or with an approved escrow; complaint reply within 72 hours; a compliance officer liaising with the DNCRP; reviews not deleted; records kept 6 years | Medium |

## 3.3 Our proposed position

1. Treat the DCOG as applying to both products until the lawyer says otherwise; services are expressly covered and compliance is cheap.
2. Before paid launch, show on both websites the company name, address, trade licence, BIN, TIN, DBID (if required), support contacts and compliance officer [to insert].
3. Publish refund, cancellation, delivery and complaint terms in Bangla and English; for consumers, both versions are official and the one more favourable to the consumer applies (default, editable) [lawyer to confirm]. English may prevail for business terms.
4. Refund channel: any money refund goes to the original payment method within 10 days (default, editable), unless the customer chooses a credit (only if a lawful credit exists after chapter 2).
5. Delivery promises stay, with automatic refunds when missed; late Normal and Slow videos get a full refund of that video's charge (default, editable).
6. Advance payment: a plan paid at the start of a service that starts immediately is not, in Claude's reading, the restricted "advance for goods not ready to ship" [lawyer to confirm].
7. Complaints: acknowledgement at once, resolution message within 72 hours, named compliance officer, records kept 6 years.
8. A written basis for every public claim; public reviews never deleted except unlawful content.

## 3.4 Risks if wrong

- DNCRP fines (shared with complainants); a wallet-only refund counted as no refund; English-only terms unenforceable or in breach of the DCOG; chargebacks and settlement holds.

## 3.5 Questions for the lawyer

1. Does the DCOG bind an online-education and AI-video SaaS provider, and the free Free Beta?
2. Must every refund go back through the same channel within 10 days, or is a credit acceptable if the customer agrees in advance?
3. Do the 48-hour and 5/10-day delivery rules apply to instant digital services, and do the Motion Studio promises satisfy them?
4. Is a monthly plan paid at the start a restricted "advance payment"?
5. Must consumer terms be in Bangla, and may English prevail? Is the "more favourable version" rule acceptable?
6. Is DBID registration required? Does the draft Cross-Border Digital Commerce Policy 2026 affect Paddle sales?
7. Are the refund rules in 3.1 lawful, in particular "no refund after 30 days" and "package fee refunded only if no video was made"?
8. Which AI marketing statements are risky ("AI teacher", "learning guarantee", "usually much sooner", "Quality Checked")?
9. Must the compliance officer be a named employee notified to the DNCRP, and how long must complaint records be kept?

## 3.6 Documents and actions needed

- Refund and subscription terms in Bangla and English; Free Beta terms.
- Seller-identity block; DBID if required; complaint procedure, compliance officer, claims file.
