# 9. AI-Generated Content, Likeness and Transparency

Covers deepfakes, voice and face cloning, digital twins, stock presenters, AI labels, and the offences and duties attaching to AI output in Bangladesh, the USA, the EU and on platforms. Mainly Motion Studio; NexaTute for AI-interaction notices.

## 9.1 Facts

- Voice Clone Studio (in app only): live consent sentence (date, account name, "I consent") plus OTP, about 3 minutes of reading, speaker verification against the consent voice; no cloning from WhatsApp or Messenger voice notes.
- Whose voice: the client's own, or another adult's with direct consent (OTP on their own phone). Not allowed: under-18s; politicians, celebrities or religious figures without written-permission proof; deceased persons without the heirs' permission. Use only in the account that made it; never for political speech, money requests, fraud or obscene content.
- Withdrawal: no new renders at once; model deleted within 72 hours; consent records kept 3 years after deletion; already delivered videos remain the creator's responsibility.
- Digital-twin presenter: own face only, about 2 minutes of video (never one selfie), live consent and OTP, 18+, watermark. Also webcam acting transfer (own face), live avatar streaming for Enterprise (AI-labelled), diaspora video messages. Any real person's face needs that person's consent and OTP; photo-real public figures blocked without proof of permission.
- Stock presenters from paid, consenting actors with releases; stock clips sold with model releases. Re-check (passkey) before clone, twin, publish or large spend.
- Clone, twin and face acting reach users only after the lawyer's check (owner, 30 October 2026). Not offered: talking photos from one picture; AI testimonial videos.
- Labels: C2PA on every export; AudioSeal on cloned-voice audio; invisible re-upload watermark; visible watermark on free output; automatic platform AI-disclosure ticks; "Made with AI" / "AI voice" labels. Verification tool checks only the company's own marks.
- AI Co-Director: general information only on medical, legal and financial topics; chats kept 180 days, never used for training; crisis protocol (helpline information, possible safety review) and prompt-injection protection (text from links and documents is content, not instructions) (owner, 30 October 2026). NexaTute's AI tutor, Play Buddy and Co-Admin always say they are AI.

## 9.3 Our proposed position

1. Keep the adopted consent framework; it is the core control under every law above.
2. At Free Beta and paid launch, offer only own-voice and own-face cloning. Third-party cloning switches on only after the lawyer approves a third-party consent form and a licence template (scope: purpose, duration, channels, languages), plus an identity and age check of the third party with the ID image deleted after checking (default, editable) [lawyer to confirm].
3. Deceased persons: off until the lawyer defines the evidence of heirs' permission (for example all legal heirs in writing or a succession certificate) (default, editable).
4. Public figures, politicians and religious figures: blocked unless written permission is uploaded and a reviewer approves; never during election periods set in admin (default, editable).
5. On withdrawal, the client must stop further publishing of affected videos within 7 days of the owner's request (default, editable) [lawyer to confirm how far published videos must come down].
6. Terms forbid removing watermarks or C2PA data; breach means immediate suspension.
7. EU AI Act: C2PA and watermarks on every export from the first public output, including Free Beta; a visible "AI-generated" label option, on by default for realistic human likeness (default, editable); platform disclosure set automatically when Motion Studio publishes; clients told at export of their own deployer duty to label deepfakes.
8. AI text exported for publication (scripts, captions, faceless autopilot) carries an AI-assisted marker where technically possible [international lawyer to confirm what 50(2) requires for text].
9. Every AI chat and tutor states it is an AI on its first screen, in the user's language.
10. Voice-artist and presenter releases expressly cover AI synthesis, emotions, languages, derived voices and sale to clients, with independent advice offered (chapter 13).

## 9.4 Risks if wrong

- Bangladesh: officers' criminal exposure if clones are misused and controls failed; defamation claims. USA: ELVIS Act "tool" liability; unenforceable performer contracts. EU: up to €15 million or 3%. Platforms: forced labels, demonetisation, lost API access. One viral misuse can end the product.
