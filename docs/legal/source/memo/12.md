# 12. Islamic Content Verification and Religious Sensitivity

Covers Motion Studio's Islamic content programme (a 25% discount tied to verification), NexaTute's religious subjects, and the wider risk of content that offends any religion in Bangladesh.

## 12.1 Facts

- Islamic content gets 25% off each per-minute rate (not the package fee), after common safety verification and Islamic verification.
- The client picks the Islamic or General plan per project; a mismatch guard offers a switch but never switches silently. The AI checks whether content is Islamic and whether Quran and hadith quotations match a verified text, with a confidence score. Confident results are discounted automatically; unsure results or mismatches go to human reviewers.
- Two named reviewers before the Free Beta; more can be added; queue alert. Review in 3–7 days; nothing charged before approval. Audit trail of score, reviewer, time, decision and reason; appeal available.
- Production rules: Arabic text only as an overlay; prayer scenes always human-reviewed; unverified quotations never labelled verified.
- The content policy blocks religious hatred and communal incitement. Bulk Islamic content for monetising clients is a stated use (platform risk, chapter 11). A Middle East pack with Gulf and Egyptian Arabic voices is approved.
- NexaTute teaches religion in Class III (NCTB) and offers live Quran lessons with a human teacher.

## 12.3 Our proposed position

1. Wording: "Islamic content review" and "reviewed by our Islamic content reviewers"; never "certified", "halal-certified", "fatwa" or "approved by" any religious authority (default, editable).
2. Scope: accuracy of quotations against a named verified text, respectful depiction, policy compliance. Reviewers do not issue rulings; content taking a side on questions disputed between schools of thought is reviewed for quotation accuracy only and is not discounted (default, editable).
3. Depiction rules (default, editable): no visual depiction of prophets, their family or companions, or angels; sacred places and objects shown respectfully; Quran text only as an overlay from the verified source; recitation only from licensed or own recordings, not AI-generated, unless reviewers approve that category.
4. Text sources (Quran text, translations, hadith collections) recorded in the licence register; modern translations are copyright works [to insert sources].
5. Reviewers sign contracts with confidentiality, conflict-of-interest and data clauses; a written review guideline; second review on appeal; names not shown publicly unless they agree.
6. The same respect rules apply to all religions; mockery of any religion and communal incitement are blocked; unclear cases go to a person.
7. If a discounted video is later found not to be Islamic content, the difference is charged only after notice and appeal; never for the company's own review error.
8. NexaTute Quran teachers follow the child safeguarding rules (chapter 8) and the curriculum, without sectarian content.

## 12.4 Risks if wrong

- A wrong quotation or offensive depiction can cause public outrage, police complaints, platform reports and threats to staff, whatever the legal outcome.
- Criminal complaints under the Penal Code or a revived cyber offence.
- Consumer complaints about "verified" errors or refused discounts; review delays as unsupplied services.
