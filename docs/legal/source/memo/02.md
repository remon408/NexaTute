# 2. Prepaid Wallet and Payment-Services Law

Covers the stored-balance designs planned until 29 October 2026 (the shared Nexa Wallet, credits, minute packs, gift cards, reseller packs and NexaLogicDev credits), measured against the Payment and Settlement Systems Act 2024 and the Bangladesh Bank prepaid-instrument guidelines of 2024. The owner chose P1 (pay per order / per plan, no stored balance) on 30 October 2026; this chapter asks the lawyer to confirm P1 and lists every product that must change.

## 2.1 Facts

- Earlier design: one Nexa Wallet in Taka across products, booked as a company liability and described as "non-cash-out, non-transferable prepaid credit". NexaTute top-ups of ৳200, ৳700 and ৳1,200 (15, 45, 75 days), gift top-ups by others, a shared sibling wallet and a 30-day refund of unused balance; Motion Studio charged the wallet at "approve & pay" on the storyboard and paid automatic refunds back into it; USD top-ups through Paddle.
- Owner decision, 30 October 2026: no stored-money wallet anywhere. Every purchase is paid per order or per plan, and no stored-value product is sold in Taka: each becomes a named plan code or a per-order price. **P1 decided by the owner on 30 Oct 2026.**
- Payment partners: aamarPay now (account currently in Vitlous Technology's name, chapter 1), Paddle later for international sales, and a second payment gateway that the owner is adding on his own initiative as risk mitigation [provider to insert].

## 2.2 Applicable law

| Law or rule | Status and key point | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2024 | In force. Bangladesh Bank approval needed to issue, buy or sell a prepaid payment instrument; up to 5 years and/or Tk 50 lakh | High |
| Bangladesh Bank Guidelines for issuing Prepaid Instrument (PI) by non-payment system entities, 2024 (v1.0, June 2024) | In force; no replacement found. Closed PI = stored value in BDT, bought with money, usable only for the issuer's own goods or services (vouchers, gift cards, wallets). Issuer "shall take approval". No exemption for non-cash-out store credit found | High (text); medium (SaaS credit is a PI) |
| Same — eligibility | 5 years of business in Bangladesh, 5 years of audited accounts, paid-up capital Tk 20 crore | High |
| Same — limits | Face value up to Tk 10,000; holding up to Tk 1 lakh; expiry up to 1 year plus one 1-year extension; discount up to 20%; no cash-out or transfer; expired balances held 1 year, then deposited with Bangladesh Bank | High |
| Draft E-Money Issuer regulations (November 2025) | Final status not confirmed; non-bank issuers would need Tk 50 crore capital | Low |
| Bangladesh Bank e-commerce settlement circular (30 June 2021) | For vouchers, payment is withheld until the service is provided; refunds go back through the same medium | Medium |

## 2.3 The finding

> On the sources found, a wallet bought with Taka, holding a stored value and usable only for RemonLogic's services, looks like a closed Prepaid Instrument. Closed instruments are non-cash-out by definition, so "non-cash-out" does not take the wallet outside the guidelines. A new company cannot meet the eligibility criteria. Whether P2–P4 below fall outside "PI" is not confirmed [lawyer to confirm].

The earlier design also had features that point further towards a PI: transferable gift cards and gift top-ups, a shared sibling wallet, a ৳30,000 reseller pack (above the Tk 10,000 face value) at 22% discount (above 20%), and a 30-day cash refund of unused balance (a form of cash-out).

**2.4.2 Payment-flow alternatives for automated and small charges.**

P1 turns every API order, autopilot, event-triggered or bulk video, hook variant, remake and ৳5 "shorter video" refund into a gateway transaction with a fee. Four alternatives keep P1 workable; each is a question for aamarPay and the lawyer before it is built:

1. Authorise then capture: authorise the estimated price at "approve & pay" and capture the actual amount after render (card pre-authorisation, bKash authorise-and-capture), so most shorter-video refunds disappear.
2. Tokenised mandate: a tokenised bKash agreement or card on file, with explicit consent and a spending cap, for API and autopilot charges.
3. Monthly business invoice: post-paid monthly invoicing only for KYB-verified businesses, with a credit limit and finance approval (the Enterprise kit pattern).
4. Batched small refunds: refunds below ৳20 (default, editable) added together and paid to the original method once a month.

## 2.5 Risks if wrong

- Offence with up to 5 years and/or Tk 50 lakh; officers exposed.
- Order to close a stored-value product and refund all balances; payment partners suspend the merchant account.
- Settlement withheld until delivery if any product is treated as a voucher.
- Dependence on one gateway: a freeze or a missing partial-refund feature stops all sales; mitigated by the second gateway.
- Rebuilding payments after launch; VAT timing changes (chapter 4).
