# 2. Prepaid Wallet and Payment-Services Law

Covers the shared Nexa Wallet, credits, gift cards and reseller packs in both products, measured against the Payment and Settlement Systems Act 2024 and the Bangladesh Bank prepaid-instrument guidelines of 2024. Ends with four redesign options for the lawyer to choose from.

## 2.1 Facts

- NexaTute is sold as monthly plans; the wallet is for extra 1:1 minutes, add-ons, extra images, Group Study, the phone tutor and counsellor calls. Top-ups: ৳200 (15 days), ৳700 (45 days), ৳1,200 (75 days); balances carry forward on the next deposit.
- NexaTute balances also come from gift top-ups by others, referral and reward minutes, outage credits and Sponsor-a-Learner purchases; siblings share one guardian wallet. Unused wallet balance is refundable within 30 days (11.6). The Shikkha Salami gift card is valid 12 months, not cash-refundable and "can be given to someone else".
- Motion Studio takes the video charge from the wallet at "approve & pay" on the storyboard (bKash top-up if short); automatic refunds go back to the wallet. Purchased credits are valid 30 days. Minute packs and pay-per-video are approved. Reseller packs: ৳10,000 (180 days) and ৳30,000 (360 days) at 15% and 22% discount.
- One wallet across products; NexaLogicDev credits (৳5 = 1 credit) buy Motion Studio videos; international top-ups in USD through Paddle.
- Top-ups are booked as a company liability until spent. Everywhere the wallet is described as "non-cash-out, non-transferable prepaid credit (lawyer check)".

## 2.2 Applicable law

| Law or rule | Status and key point | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2024 | In force. Bangladesh Bank approval needed to issue, buy or sell a prepaid payment instrument; up to 5 years and/or Tk 50 lakh | High |
| Bangladesh Bank Guidelines for issuing Prepaid Instrument (PI) by non-payment system entities, 2024 (v1.0, June 2024) | In force; no replacement found. Closed PI = stored value in BDT, bought with money, usable only for the issuer's own goods or services (vouchers, gift cards, wallets). Issuer "shall take approval". No exemption for non-cash-out store credit found | High (text); medium (SaaS credit is a PI) |
| Same — eligibility | 5 years of business in Bangladesh, 5 years of audited accounts, paid-up capital Tk 20 crore | High |
| Same — limits | Face value up to Tk 10,000; holding up to Tk 1 lakh; expiry up to 1 year plus one 1-year extension; discount up to 20%; no cash-out or transfer; expired balances held 1 year, then deposited with Bangladesh Bank | High |
| Draft E-Money Issuer regulations (November 2025) | Final status not confirmed; non-bank issuers would need Tk 50 crore capital | Low |
| Bangladesh Bank e-commerce settlement circular (30 June 2021) | For vouchers, payment is withheld until the service is provided; refunds go back through the same medium | Medium |

## 2.3 The finding

> On the sources found, a wallet bought with Taka, holding a stored value and usable only for RemonLogic's services, looks like a closed Prepaid Instrument. Closed instruments are non-cash-out by definition, so "non-cash-out" does not take the wallet outside the guidelines. A new company cannot meet the eligibility criteria. Whether any redesign below falls outside "PI" is not confirmed [lawyer to confirm].

Features that make the design look more like a PI: balances shown in Taka and booked as a liability; gift top-ups, a transferable gift card and a shared sibling wallet; a ৳30,000 reseller pack (above the Tk 10,000 face value) at 22% discount (above 20%). The 30-day cash refund of unused balance is a form of cash-out.

Claude's recommendation for the lawyer to test:

1. Motion Studio: Option A (it already charges at one moment). Credits, minute packs and reseller packs stay off until the lawyer decides.
2. NexaTute: Option A for add-ons and calls; Option B for extra-minute packs only if the lawyer confirms they are not a PI.
3. Long term: Option D; Option C if a licensed partner offers it.
4. Non-purchased allowances (referral, rewards, Free Beta, outage credits) shown in minutes, not Taka [lawyer to confirm they fall outside the guidelines].
5. Gift cards, vouchers, third-party gift top-ups and reseller packs off until a lawful form is confirmed; reseller sales can become invoiced wholesale purchases of plan codes (default, editable).
6. The admin panel keeps "wallet" as a switch, off for Bangladesh by default (default, editable). Sponsor-a-Learner becomes a direct plan purchase.
7. USD balances through Paddle are reviewed by the international firm; until then, international customers also pay per order or plan.

## 2.5 Risks if wrong

- Offence with up to 5 years and/or Tk 50 lakh; officers exposed.
- Order to close the wallet and refund all balances; payment partners suspend the merchant account.
- Settlement withheld until delivery if balances are treated as vouchers.
- Rebuilding payments after launch; VAT timing changes (chapter 4).
