## 1.3 Our proposed position

1. Objects clause: long and specific now, because later changes need the High Court. The draft memorandum in this pack covers: education for all ages and institutions; software, SaaS, AI, cloud IDE, hosting and APIs; AI and human media production, voice and likeness services with consent, stock media; marketing and CRM; marketplaces for a commission, reseller and affiliate programmes; data processing and content review (including religious content); export of IT-enabled services and import of software and cloud services; IP licensing; payments-related activity, stored value, IVR, recruitment and charitable funds only "with any approval or licence required by law"; subsidiaries at home and abroad subject to Bangladesh Bank approval; incidental acts.
2. Before incorporation: nothing is signed or applied for in the company's name. Personal data is not collected for the NexaTute Free Beta (20 November 2026) until the company exists, unless the lawyer confirms who the controller is in the gap. After incorporation, the owner and every contributor assign pre-incorporation work (code, logos, content, domains) to the company in writing.
3. Vitlous account: no RemonLogic sale is collected through it. Pilot test payments use the sandbox or fully refunded test amounts (default, editable). RemonLogic applies for its own merchant account as soon as it has the certificate, trade licence, TIN, BIN and bank account; if not ready, the paid launch moves. A collection-agent fallback only with written approval from the lawyer, accountant and aamarPay.
4. USA LLC: not formed now. Apply to Paddle as RemonLogic Private Limited, disclosing the AI voice and avatar features; register the DMCA agent in the company's name; ask NCMEC about non-US registration (else the paid IWF list). An LLC only if Paddle or NCMEC refuses, after the lawyer's FX opinion.

## 1.4 Risks if wrong

- Narrow objects: ultra vires acts and a High Court petition to fix. Pre-incorporation data and contracts: unclear, possibly personal liability; consents collected again.
- Vitlous account: possible unlicensed aggregation (up to 5 years and/or Tk 50 lakh), account freeze, revenue and VAT in the wrong company. USA LLC: possible FX breach and transfer-pricing exposure.
