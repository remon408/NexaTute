# 11. Platform and Messaging Rules

Covers the rules of YouTube, Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger), TikTok, imo and the app stores. These are contracts and policies, not laws, but breaking them can stop the business faster than a regulator. They change without notice and are re-checked at every feature switch-on.

## 11.1 Facts

- Motion Studio publishes to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok through official APIs, ticks AI-disclosure options automatically and sets "Made for kids" in kids-safe mode. It also uses YouTube trend data, platform analytics, one-click Meta boosts from the creator's ad account and a pre-publish ad-policy check.
- Bulk or templated output: personalised bulk videos, one ad per catalogue product, auto-publish calendar, faceless autopilot (daily cap), bulk Islamic and story content. Clients are warned about "inauthentic content" rules.
- Ordering by WhatsApp or Messenger voice note with a payment link (subject to platform approval); personalised videos sent by WhatsApp or SMS link.
- NexaTute study bots on WhatsApp and Messenger. Nexa Co-Admin for staff on WhatsApp, Messenger and imo: off until the lawyer and DPO check; read-only by default; data masked; logged.
- NexaTute in the three app stores; in-app purchases through store billing at +30%.

## 11.3 Our proposed position

1. Official APIs only; no scraping, personal-account automation or unofficial WhatsApp tools.
2. Motion Studio sets each platform's AI-disclosure option for realistic AI content and cloned voices (other than the client's own voiceover) and keeps C2PA in the file; the client cannot switch disclosure off for realistic human likeness.
3. Before auto-publish, a similarity check warns when a video differs only superficially from the client's recent uploads (default, editable); autopilot keeps its daily cap; marketing never says content is "monetisation-safe".
4. "Made for kids" set automatically but remains the channel owner's responsibility; realistic AI political ads blocked in election periods (default, editable).
5. One-click boost: the creator pays Meta directly; RemonLogic never holds ad spend.
6. WhatsApp and Messenger ordering only through the WhatsApp Business Platform with an approved account in the company's name; approved templates; opt-in recorded before business-initiated messages; voice notes used only for the order, never for cloning; payment by link to the company checkout.
7. Personalised bulk videos are sent only from the client's own business accounts or SMS sender, with the client confirming opt-in for each recipient; RemonLogic does not send marketing for clients from its own accounts (default, editable).
8. Co-Admin on chat apps stays off until approved; no imo integration until an official business API exists; app-store wording follows each store's rules.

## 11.4 Risks if wrong

- Revoked API access stops publishing, chat ordering and analytics for all clients at once; client channels demonetised; a banned WhatsApp number; app removal for steering; staff data exposed in chat apps.
