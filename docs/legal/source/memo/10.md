# 10. Copyright, Licences and Trademarks

Covers outputs, inputs, images, music, training data, model licences and trademarks.

## 10.1 Facts

- Motion Studio terms (draft): the client receives all rights RemonLogic holds in the output; paid videos have full commercial use; free videos are watermarked, personal use; the client is responsible for inputs; no promise of copyright protection.
- Upload controls: ownership declaration with file hash, music replacement, Chromaprint check, logo detection. Marketplace (owner, 30 October 2026): one for the three products, including third-party goods; sellers 80%; personal voice clones and twins never listed; under-18s see only NexaTute items.
- NexaTute: AI lessons, institution-uploaded past papers, NCTB textbooks chapter by chapter.
- Images: Wikimedia (CC0 or CC BY, attributed); Pexels and web images for reference only; own photographer shoots for the library and the "Bangladesh scenes" and people LoRA; client uploads only in that client's videos; a paid stock API. LoRA training only on own photos until a lawyer confirms. Music: own AI music (ACE-Step, to verify) or Content-ID-free tracks.

## 10.3 Our proposed position

1. Terms grant "all rights RemonLogic holds in the output" and never say the client "owns the copyright".
2. Client warranties that it owns or has licensed every input and has consent from every person shown or heard, with an indemnity (cap as the lawyer advises). RemonLogic processes inputs only to provide the service and never trains on client content except the client's own LoRA, unless the client opts in.
3. Photographers assign copyright in writing and every identifiable person signs a release covering AI training and derivatives before any photo enters the library or LoRA. Wikimedia: CC0 or CC BY only, attributed; identifiable people not animated without consent (default, editable).
4. Music only from verified own AI music, own library or written licences allowing use in client videos. No copying of full textbooks or past papers without a licence.
5. FLUX.1-dev is not used anywhere, including the Free Beta, without a commercial licence. No model or library goes live until its licence is verified and recorded; model-card prohibited uses go into the Content Policy; Wan 2.2 pinned to the open-weight version; no GPL or AGPL in shipped server components without written review.
6. Marketplace: one refund rule (7 days if not downloaded or opened; any time if not as described; default, editable); items sold under their stated licence; sellers identity- and sanctions-checked before payout; chapter 14 takedowns; licensed payment providers only.
7. Trademarks: file the main names and logos in Bangladesh (Claude suggests classes 9, 35, 41, 42, default, editable), and in the US, EU and UK before marketing there; logo maker assigns rights in writing.

## 10.4 Risks if wrong

- Misleading promises of copyright; claims from photographers, models, musicians or publishers; Content ID claims; licence breach forcing removal of a model and its LoRAs; rebranding after a trademark conflict.

## 10.5 Questions for the lawyer

1. Can AI-assisted output with human creative input be protected under the Copyright Act 2023, and who is the author?
2. Is LoRA training on photos the company does not own (for example CC BY images) lawful in Bangladesh?
3. Do photos of identifiable people in public places need consent for commercial AI training and animation?
4. May NexaTute teach from NCTB textbooks chapter by chapter, and what may it quote? Are past papers usable under any licence?
5. Which trademark classes; can a Bangladeshi applicant file internationally through one system; who owns an AI-assisted logo?
6. Marketplace: is RemonLogic seller or agent; must settlement use a licensed PSP or escrow; VAT on commission or gross; withholding on seller payouts?
