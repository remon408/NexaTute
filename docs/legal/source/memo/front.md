# How to Use This Memo

## Purpose and structure

The other drafts in this legal pack (memorandum and articles, NexaTute and Motion Studio legal pages) follow this memo's positions; when the lawyer changes a position here, those drafts change with it. Each of chapters 1–14 covers one legal area in six parts:

1. Facts — what the company plans to do, from the blueprints and the owner's decisions.
2. Applicable law — name, year, status and research confidence.
3. Our proposed position — practical and conservative; options where a choice is needed.
4. Risks if wrong.
5. Questions for the lawyer — numbered, so answers can be returned as "2.6.4: yes, with this change".
6. Documents and actions needed.

Chapter 15 is the legal go-live checklist (item, owner, milestone).

## Limits of the research

- The Acts passed in April 2026 (when Parliament converted the interim-government ordinances) were not found on the official laws website; Act and section numbers must be checked in the Bangladesh Gazette. This affects the Personal Data Protection Act 2026, the Cyber Security Act 2026 and the National Data Management Act 2026. The Master Blueprint's draft opinion cites Acts 63, 81 and 80 of 2026; these numbers are unverified [lawyer to confirm].
- Employment, trade-licence, trademark and outward-investment rules were not researched.
