# 5. Recording Policy

> **In short**
> - Classes are recorded only if you choose. Recording never starts by itself, and an indicator shows while it is on.
> - Recording is free. Recordings are kept for 12 months and then deleted, or until your course ends if you choose.
> - Only you (and your guardian, if you are under 18) can watch your 1:1 recordings. Batch recordings are for approved class members only.
> - You can download a recording as a video made in your own browser.
> - Draft for lawyer review, 30 October 2026. Not yet in force.

## 5.1 Recording is by choice

5.1.1 **1:1 classes.** At the start of each 1:1 class you choose "Record this class" or "Don't record". You can also start recording during the class. A recording indicator is always visible while recording is on.

5.1.2 **Batch classes (up to 50 learners).** Each learner chooses "I want the recording" or "I don't need it". If at least one learner wants it, one shared recording of the whole class is made after the notice, consent and privacy checks. It is one recording for the class, not one copy per learner. In the shared recording:

- only the audio of learners who agreed is kept;
- the voices and video of learners who did not agree are left out where possible, and otherwise extra safeguards apply [lawyer to confirm];
- learners appear by nickname in chat and polls.

5.1.3 **Learners under 18.** A learner under 18 can record, or have their audio kept in a shared recording, only if the guardian has turned on the recording consent switch.

5.1.4 **Free Beta and Free Trial.** Classes are not recorded during the Free Beta or the Free Trial.

5.1.5 **Live classes with a human teacher.** Human-teacher revision classes and group classes follow the same choice and consent rules. Live human invigilation of an institution's exam is not recorded.

## 5.2 What a recording contains

5.2.1 **AI classes.** These are stored as a light replay file containing:

- the Nexa Teacher's text, the whiteboard steps and their timing;
- your own voice, if you spoke.

The Nexa Teacher's voice and avatar are re-created in your browser when you replay.

5.2.2 **Live and group classes.** These are stored as separate audio and video tracks with the whiteboard log, and played in the NexaTute player.

5.2.3 **No voice transcript.** We do not store a written transcript of your own voice. Search inside a recording uses only the Nexa Teacher's text.

5.2.4 **Class report.** A class report is attached to each recording. It shows a summary, mistakes with their minute in the class, and your improvement. If a class was not recorded, the report shows the whiteboard step and the Nexa Teacher's words as text instead of video links.

## 5.3 Who can see recordings

| Recording | Who can watch |
|---|---|
| 1:1 class | The learner, and the guardian of a learner under 18 |
| Batch class | Approved members of that class only |
| Class reports | The learner and the guardian. Teachers and institutions see an anonymised class view without names |
| Test scripts of a test an institution set | The learner, the guardian and that class's teachers |

5.3.1 NexaTute staff do not watch recordings, except a named staff member when you ask for help, or to investigate a reported safety or abuse issue. Every access is logged (to confirm).

5.3.2 Recordings are never used for advertising. They are not used to train AI unless you (or your guardian) have turned on the "use of anonymised data to improve our AI" switch, and then only with personal data removed.

## 5.4 Protected links

Each private recording opens through a signed link that is made when you press "Watch" and stays valid for 15 minutes (default, editable). A copied link does not work for anyone else. Private recordings are never stored in public caches.

## 5.5 How long recordings are kept

5.5.1 Recordings are kept for 12 months and then deleted automatically. You can choose "Keep until my course ends" for any recording.

5.5.2 Recordings count toward your plan's storage allowance. When your storage is full, you choose whether to delete old files or buy more storage.

5.5.3 If your plan ends without renewal, playback is locked until you renew. Recordings are still deleted on their normal schedule.

5.5.4 You can delete any recording yourself at any time. A guardian can delete the recordings of a learner under 18.

5.5.5 Each recording has a consent record and an access log, kept for 3 years after the consent ends.

## 5.6 Download

5.6.1 You can download any of your recordings free of charge. Your browser turns the recording into a standard MP4 video on your device; the video is not made on our servers.

5.6.2 Downloaded copies are your responsibility. Please keep them private and do not share other learners' voices or nicknames.

5.6.3 Batch recordings can be downloaded only by approved class members, for their own study.

## 5.7 Your rules as a viewer

You must not:

- record classes with another device or screen recorder;
- share recordings or clips that include other learners;
- post recordings publicly.

Breaking these rules may lead to suspension under the Terms of Use.

## 5.8 Withdrawing consent

5.8.1 You (or your guardian) can turn off recording consent at any time in Settings. This stops future recordings. Existing recordings stay until you delete them or they expire.

5.8.2 If you withdraw consent for a shared batch recording, we remove your audio from it where technically possible, or otherwise restrict it [lawyer to confirm].
