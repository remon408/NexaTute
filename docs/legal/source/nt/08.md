# 8. Data Deletion Instructions

> **In short**
> - Delete your account, or only parts of your data (such as recordings or photos), from Settings. A guardian can do this for a child.
> - Before deleting, you can download all your data in one click.
> - Deletion is completed within 30 days. Some records, such as payment records, must be kept by law, and we tell you which.
> - Draft for lawyer review, 30 October 2026. Not yet in force.

## 8.1 Before you delete

8.1.1 **Download your data first.** Go to Settings, then Privacy, then "Download my data". A download link is sent to your notifications within a few minutes. It is valid for 7 days and opens with a one-time code (default, editable).

8.1.2 **Cancel paid plans.** Turn off auto-renew, and ask for any refund within the refund window (see the Refund and Cancellation Policy). [Option A: an unused wallet balance is lost when the account is deleted, unless refunded under the Refund Policy.]

8.1.3 **Learners under 18.** Only the guardian can delete a child's account or data, from the guardian dashboard.

## 8.2 Delete your whole account

1. Sign in on nexatute.com or in the app.
2. Go to Settings, then Privacy, then "Delete my account". A guardian goes to the guardian dashboard, chooses the child's profile, then "Delete this learner's account".
3. Enter the one-time code sent to your phone or email.
4. Confirm. You receive a confirmation with a reference number.

8.2.1 **Cooling-off period.** Deletion starts after 7 days (default, editable). During that time you can sign in and cancel. After that it cannot be undone.

8.2.2 **Completion.** Deletion is completed within 30 days of your request.

## 8.3 Delete only some data

You can delete these at any time without closing your account:

| Data | Where |
|---|---|
| A class recording | Recording library, then Delete |
| Uploaded photos, scripts and files | My storage, then Delete |
| Face template | Settings, then Privacy, then turn off face verification (you then sign in with OTP checks) |
| Study Hall posts | Open the post, then Delete |
| Consent for a feature | Settings, then Consent (guardians: guardian dashboard, then Consent) |

## 8.4 Delete without signing in

If you cannot sign in, write to [to insert: DPO email] from the phone number or email on the account. Give the learner's name, and the guardian's name for a learner under 18. We confirm your identity before deleting anything and reply within 30 days.

8.5.1 If you used a NexaTute study bot on WhatsApp or Messenger, send "DELETE MY DATA" to the bot, or write to the DPO. We delete the chat data we hold. The messaging platform keeps its own copy under its own policy [to confirm the bot launch and keyword].

## 8.6 What we keep, and why

Some records must be kept even after deletion:

| Record | How long | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Payment, VAT and refund records | 7 years [lawyer to confirm] | Tax and accounting law |
| Consent records | 3 years after consent ends | To prove consent was given |
| Safety and compliance case records | 6 months after the case closes | Child safety and legal claims |
| Records under a legal hold or authority request | Until the hold ends | Legal obligation |
| Backup copies | Until each copy expires (daily copies 30 days) | Deleted data leaves each backup when that copy expires [lawyer to confirm] |
| Anonymised statistics | No limit | They no longer identify anyone (groups under 20 are hidden) |

## 8.7 Institutions

8.7.1 If a learner was enrolled by an institution, deleting the account also removes the learner from the institution's dashboard. Institution records that are not personal data, such as anonymised class averages, may remain.

8.7.2 When a learner leaves an institution without deleting the account, the institution can view that learner's records for 90 more days.

## 8.8 Automatic deletion

Even if you do nothing, we delete:

- recordings after 12 months;
- written-test scans after 90 days;
- files in your storage 30 days after your plan ends;
- the account and learning records 5 years after the account was last used.

See the Privacy Policy, section 2.7, for the full list.
