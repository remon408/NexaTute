# 7. Cookie Notice

> **In short**
> - Only essential cookies load until you choose. You can accept or refuse analytics and marketing cookies, and change your choice at any time.
> - We show no advertising on NexaTute. Marketing cookies are off by default and are never offered to learners under 18.
> - Analytics are counts only, with no session recording.
> - Draft for lawyer review, 30 October 2026. Not yet in force.

## 7.1 What cookies are

7.1.1 Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, IndexedDB and service workers, which let the app work offline and save your test answers. This notice covers all of them.

## 7.2 Your choice

7.2.1 When you first visit nexatute.com, a cookie banner lets you accept or refuse each optional category. Only essential cookies load before you choose [lawyer to confirm: the DCOG requirement for disclosure and checkbox consent].

7.2.2 You can change your choice at any time from "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Refusing optional cookies does not affect your lessons.

7.2.3 For signed-in learners under 18, the marketing category is not offered at all, and analytics are limited to counts.

## 7.3 Categories

| Category | Why we use it | Can you refuse? | Under-18 learners |
|---|---|---|---|

## 7.5 Inside the app

7.5.1 The learning app uses no third-party trackers or advertising IDs. Downloads of large in-browser models happen only on Wi-Fi or with your consent.

7.5.2 Downloaded lessons are stored on your device and lock when your plan ends.

## 7.6 Clearing cookies

You can delete cookies in your browser settings. If you delete essential cookies or local storage, you may be signed out and lose unsynced offline work.
