# 3. Children's Privacy and Guardian Consent Notice

> **In short**
> - Every learner under 18, and every PlayBoom child, needs a parent or guardian's consent before using NexaTute.
> - You, the guardian, decide each consent switch, approve every purchase and can see, download or delete your child's data at any time.
> - We never show advertising to children, never profile, track or target them, never sell their data and never use their real voice to make an AI voice.
> - You can withdraw any consent at once in the guardian dashboard.
> - Draft for lawyer review, 30 October 2026. Not yet in force.

## 3.1 Who this notice is for

3.1.1 This notice is for parents and legal guardians of learners under 18 who use NexaTute, including PlayBoom (Play class to Class III). It explains in plain words how consent works and how we protect your child. The full details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.

3.1.2 In Bangladesh a child is a person under 18 [lawyer to confirm: Children Act 2013, s.4; Personal Data Protection Act 2026]. The law requires a parent's or guardian's consent before we process a child's personal data. It also forbids tracking, monitoring for marketing, profiling and targeted advertising directed at children [lawyer to confirm the exact provisions].

## 3.2 How consent works

3.2.1 **Age check.** At sign-up we ask for the learner's date of birth. If the learner is under 18, we send a one-time code (OTP) to the guardian's phone. The account cannot be used until the guardian enters the code and gives consent.

3.2.2 **Plain summary first.** Before you consent, we show a 3–4 line summary in your language, with a link to the full text.

3.2.3 **One switch per feature.** You decide each of these separately:

| Switch | What it allows | Starting setting (default, editable) |
|---|---|---|
| Basic learning | Lessons, practice, tests, progress and reports. Needed to use NexaTute | Required |
| Face verification | A face check in the browser to make sure only your child uses the account. You can choose OTP checks instead | Off |
| Class recordings | Your child may choose to record classes; in batch classes, your child's audio is kept in the shared recording | Off |
| Study groups | Joining Study Hall groups of 2–10 learners, by nickname and text chat only | Off |
| Sharing with an institution | Your child's school or coaching centre sees progress and test results | Off, unless enrolled through the institution |
| Proctored exams | A face check and presence checks in certificate or institution exams (no video or snapshots for under-18s) | Off |
| Use of anonymised data to improve our AI | Your child's data, with personal details removed, used to improve teaching | Off |
| Sharing a "Wrapped" recap or success story | Your child may share a recap video or appear in a success story | Off |
| Personalisation features (engagement prediction, interests, learning style, offers) | Locked off for under-18s | Locked off [lawyer to confirm] |

3.2.4 **Who decides.** The learner cannot change these switches. A teacher can decide only for adult learners. An institution can rely on consent only where it has collected guardian consent on our form [lawyer to confirm].

3.2.5 **Record of consent.** We keep a record of each choice: who made it, when, and the text you saw. We keep these records for 3 years after the consent ends.

## 3.3 The guardian dashboard

3.3.1 From the guardian dashboard you can:

- see your child's progress, mastery, test results and the weekly report;
- watch your child's recordings and read class reports;
- set a daily study limit, quiet hours and a break reminder (shown after 45 minutes by default);
- choose your own app language, separate from your child's;
- change or withdraw any consent switch;
- download all of your child's data, or delete it;
- see and approve purchase requests and set a monthly spending limit;
- book a call with a human counsellor (a paid service).

3.3.2 SMS messages to guardians are free.

## 3.4 Purchase approval

3.4.1 Children cannot pay. Every purchase request from a learner under 18 goes to the guardian's phone. Payment happens only after you approve it. This covers plans, Daily Passes, Nexa Co-Teacher Passes, Doubt Passes, Crash Packs, storage, MindForge Pro and NexaTute learning items in the marketplace (learners under 18 see no other marketplace items).

3.4.2 You can set a monthly spending limit. A request above the limit cannot be approved until you raise the limit; there is no one-time override.

3.4.3 In an app-store app, purchases by a learner under 18 go through the store's billing with your approval. They never go through a payment page inside the app.

3.4.4 In PlayBoom, only the guardian can buy.

3.4.5 Some products are for adults only. Learners under 18 cannot buy recruitment tests or use the job pathway.

## 3.5 What we never do with children's data

3.5.1 We never:

- show advertising to children, on NexaTute or anywhere else;
- profile, track or monitor children for marketing, or use their learning data to choose or time sales offers;
- show children offers for other RemonLogic products (Remon Motion Studio, NexaLogicDev) or for anyone else's products;
- sell or rent children's data, or share it with advertisers or data brokers;
- use third-party trackers, advertising IDs or session recording;
- collect live location;
- read emotions from the camera or voice; the face is used only to confirm the account owner;
- keep face templates of PlayBoom children;
- send children's lessons, questions or scripts to an outside cloud AI service;
- use a child's real voice to create an AI voice, even with consent (a guardian may clone their own voice for Play Buddy stories, never the child's);
- allow private chat between children, or private chat between an adult and a child without guardian visibility;
- let children post face photos in community spaces (faces are blurred);
- publish a child's name, image or results without the guardian's consent;
- use AI to choose reminder times for children, or send reminders after 9 pm.

3.5.2 Children use nicknames in community spaces. Under-18 learners chat by text only. Leaderboards show nicknames, and only where turned on. In PlayBoom there are no links, no chat between children and no purchases by children.

## 3.6 What we do with children's data

3.6.1 We use your child's data only to teach your child and to keep them safe. For example:

- choosing the next question;
- re-planning the week;
- marking tests;
- preparing your weekly report;
- alerting a human controller and you if your child may be at risk.

3.6.2 Adaptive teaching uses answer correctness, mastery and simple interaction signals such as time stuck on a step. It does not build a personality or marketing profile.

3.6.3 Voice, images and handwriting are your child's private data. A microphone or camera indicator is always shown when they are in use.

## 3.7 Safety

3.7.1 Our safety system, Nexa Guard, filters bad language, sexual content and other harmful content. It checks images before they are posted and blocks harmful links. Serious concerns go to trained human controllers, who are on duty 24 hours a day. You receive details of any serious incident involving your child; your child's institution receives only a notice without details.

3.7.2 If your child seems to be in distress, the app shows helpline numbers for your country, and you are told.

## 3.8 How to withdraw consent

3.8.1 Open the guardian dashboard, go to Settings and then Consent, and turn off the switch. Withdrawal works at once for the future.

3.8.2 What happens to data already collected:

- turning off face verification deletes the face template;
- turning off recordings stops new recordings, and you may also delete existing ones;
- withdrawing basic learning consent closes the account (see the Data Deletion Instructions).

3.8.3 You can also write to our Data Protection Officer at the Privacy and data requests email on our Contact page [set in admin: Privacy & data requests email].

## 3.9 When your child turns 18

When your child turns 18, they take over their own account and consent choices. We tell you both 30 days before (default, editable). Your access then ends unless your child chooses to keep it [lawyer to confirm].

## 3.10 Children outside Bangladesh

US learners under 13 are on a waiting list until we have verifiable parental consent under COPPA. For learners in the EU and UK, guardian consent applies to all learners under 18, and the GDPR rights apply (see Privacy Policy 2.10). Other countries' rules are checked before we market there [lawyer to confirm].
