# 4. Refund and Cancellation Policy

> **In short**
> - Changed your mind? Within 30 days of a fresh purchase you get back 100% of the unused part.
> - Our fault? Minutes lost to an outage come back automatically within 24 hours. Technical problems, wrong charges and duplicate charges are fixed at any time.
> - Cancel any time in Settings; this stops the next renewal.
> - Paid plans carry a learning guarantee: if a learner who takes part fully does not improve, the minutes come back as credit.
> - Draft for lawyer review, 30 October 2026. Not yet in force.

## 4.1 Scope

4.1.1 This policy covers purchases of NexaTute plans, PlayBoom plans, passes, add-ons and [Option A: wallet top-ups / Option B: extra-minute packs] (see Terms of Use 1.7) made on nexatute.com or in the NexaTute apps.

4.1.2 For a learner under 18, the guardian who paid is the person entitled to the refund.

4.1.3 Institutions follow the refund terms in their institution agreement.

4.1.4 Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumers' Right Protection Act 2009, the Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines 2021 or the consumer law of your country [lawyer to confirm].

## 4.2 Refund rules

| Situation | When | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh purchase (plan, pass, add-on, top-up or pack) | Within 30 days of payment | 100% of the unused part: unused days of a plan, or unused [wallet balance / pack minutes] |
| Year Pass, commitment plan, prepaid months, Head Start, Olympiad, crash program | Within 30 days of payment | The unused part, as for a fresh purchase |
| Any purchase | After 30 days | No refund for a change of mind |
| Technical problem on our side | Any time | Full refund for the affected part |
| Platform outage, failed session or stopped 1:1 class | Automatic | Lost 1:1 minutes credited within 24 hours, and validity extended by the length of the outage |
| Batch class stopped by a server problem | Automatic | A make-up class the same day or the next day, plus the recording |
| Wrong charge | Any time | Reversed at once |
| Duplicate charge | Any time | Reversed at once |
| Shikkha Salami gift card | — | No cash refund. Valid 12 months and can be given to someone else |
| Teacher-marketplace item | Within 7 days | Refunded if not opened |

## 4.3 How the "unused part" is worked out

4.3.1 **Monthly plan.** The refund is the plan price × (days left ÷ days in the period). For example, a ৳800 plan cancelled with 20 of 30 days left gives ৳800 × 20/30 = ৳533 (rounded down to the whole Taka, default, editable).

4.3.2 **Discounts.** If you received a prepay or Year Pass discount, the days you used are charged at the normal monthly price before the refund is worked out (default, editable) [lawyer to confirm this is fair under consumer law].

4.3.3 **Daily Pass.** A Daily Pass lasts 24 hours. It is refunded in full if none of its minutes, tests or Co-Teacher topics were used. After use starts, it is refunded only for a technical problem on our side (default, editable).

4.3.4 **[Option A] Wallet.** The unused balance from top-ups made in the last 30 days can be refunded. Credits we added free (outage credits, guarantee credits, rewards, referral minutes) are not refunded as money.

4.3.5 **[Option B] Extra-minute packs.** Unused minutes of a pack bought in the last 30 days are refunded at the pack's price per minute. Minutes we added free are not refunded as money.

4.3.6 **Free products.** The Free Beta, Free Trial and free tier are free, so there is nothing to refund.

## 4.4 Automatic credits for problems on our side

4.4.1 If a platform outage, a failed session or a stopped Nexa Teacher class makes you lose 1:1 minutes, we credit them automatically within 24 hours, at your course's per-minute rate. Under Option A they go to your wallet; under Option B they are added as minutes to your course. The validity of your plan or credit is extended by the length of the outage.

4.4.2 If a timed test is interrupted by a verified NexaTute server outage, the lost time is added back to the test.

4.4.3 If you think we missed a credit, report it within 30 days (default, editable) and we will check the session log.

## 4.5 Wrong AI answers

4.5.1 If a reviewer confirms that a Nexa Teacher answer in a paid 1:1 session was wrong, we credit back the minutes spent on that explanation, with a minimum of 5 minutes (default, editable; owner to approve).

4.5.2 Repeated confirmed errors in a course that make it unusable for you count as a technical problem under 4.2. You can then ask for a refund of the affected part at any time.

## 4.6 Learning guarantee

4.6.1 **What it covers.** All paid plans. It does not cover the Free Beta, Free Trial, free tier or Daily Pass.

4.6.2 **What it measures.** Learning progress on the Mastery Map for the topics studied over 4 weeks. If the average gain is under 5 points (default, editable), the guarantee applies. It is not a grade or exam-result guarantee.

4.6.3 **What the learner must do.** In those 4 weeks, the learner must:

- study 4 weeks in a row, with at least 3 sessions a week of at least 20 active minutes;
- complete at least 80% of the reviews that fall due;
- take the start test and the end check;
- make honest attempts.

4.6.4 **For learners under 18,** the guardian must also:

- have given consent;
- not have blocked study time;
- have opened the weekly report;
- have confirmed the study schedule at least once.

4.6.5 **For institution learners,** the teacher must assign practice in the app and not override the plan. Weeks blocked by the institution extend the 4-week window. Server outages also extend it.

4.6.6 **What you get.** The 1:1 minutes used in those 4 weeks come back as credit, not cash. The credit is valid 6 months, and a learner can receive it at most once per course every 3 months (defaults, editable).

4.6.7 **How to claim.** The check runs automatically every 4 weeks, and a "Claim" button appears when the guarantee applies. A person reviews unclear cases within 7 days, shows the evidence and allows an appeal.

4.6.8 All numbers in 4.6 are set in the admin panel and changed only with two-person approval [lawyer to confirm the wording; landing-page guarantee claims follow these terms].

## 4.7 Cancellation

4.7.1 **How to cancel.** Open Settings, then Plan, then Cancel renewal. For a learner under 18, the guardian cancels from the guardian dashboard. Cancelling turns off auto-renew. The plan continues to the end of the paid period unless you also ask for a refund under 4.2.

4.7.2 **Commitment plan.** You can leave at any time without a penalty. You only lose the free 12th month.

4.7.3 **Downgrade.** A downgrade takes effect from the next renewal. There is no refund for the current period except under 4.2.

4.7.4 **Upgrade.** An upgrade takes effect at once. You pay only the difference for the days left.

4.7.5 **Account closure.** If you close your account, you can ask for a refund of any unused part within the 30-day window. If we close an account, see Terms of Use 1.16.4.

## 4.8 How to ask for a refund

4.8.1 Use "Report a problem", choose "Payment", or write to [to insert: support email]. Include the receipt number. For under-18 learners, the request must come from, or be confirmed by, the guardian.

4.8.2 We confirm the request within 72 hours (default, editable) [lawyer to confirm against DCOG].

4.8.3 We pay approved refunds within 10 working days (default, editable), back through the same payment method you used (for example bKash, Nagad or card) [lawyer to confirm: DCOG and Bangladesh Bank rules on refunds through the same channel]. Your bank or wallet provider may take a few more days to show it.

4.8.4 Large refunds may need a second approval inside NexaTute. This does not change the time limit above.

## 4.9 Purchases through Paddle and app stores

4.9.1 **Paddle.** For purchases made abroad through Paddle, Paddle is the merchant of record and processes the refund under these same rules, together with Paddle's buyer terms.

4.9.2 **App stores.** Refunds for purchases made through Google Play, the Apple App Store or the Microsoft Store are handled by the store under its own refund policy. We cannot refund them directly. If the store refunds you, the purchase is removed from your account. The 30% app-store charge is the store's fee. We return it only where the store refunds it.

## 4.10 Chargebacks

If you dispute a payment with your bank or wallet provider, please tell us first so we can fix it quickly. During a chargeback the purchase may be paused. If the chargeback is found to be wrong, we may ask you to pay again.
