# 6. AI Transparency Notice

> **In short**
> - Your teachers on NexaTute are AI. Every AI is labelled, and the PlayBoom Play Buddy always says it is an AI.
> - AI can make mistakes. Every answer has a "Report wrong answer" button, and people check reports, rechecks and appeals.
> - Our AI runs mainly on our own servers. We never read emotions from your face or voice.
> - AI never decides on its own to punish you, to accuse you of cheating or to close your account.
> - Draft for lawyer review, 30 October 2026. Not yet in force.

## 6.1 Why we publish this notice

6.1.1 We want learners and guardians to know when they are dealing with AI, what it does, and where its limits are. This notice also meets the transparency duty in Article 50 of the EU AI Act for learners in the EU, which has applied since 2 August 2026 [lawyer to confirm for other countries].

6.1.2 Each course also has a trust page. It shows the course's sources and licences, the AI models and verification level, the consent and recording settings, and the wrong-answer reports and fixes.

## 6.2 The AI on NexaTute

| AI | What it does | Human involvement |
|---|---|---|
| Nexa Teacher | Teaches 1:1 and batch classes by voice, avatar and whiteboard; asks questions; adapts the lesson | Content reviewers approve course material; wrong-answer reports are reviewed |
| Nexa Co-Teacher | Text lessons, step-by-step answers and topic summaries | Answers are checked against the verified answer store |
| Study Hall Moderator | Short help in the Study Hall; posts an "AI hint" if no one answers | Its answers are always labelled "AI hint" and are never marked verified |
| PlayBoom Play Buddy | Voice-first games, stories and questions for young children | Always says it is an AI; asks for help when unsure; guardians see the questions |
| Written-answer marking | Reads handwriting and marks answers against the board's scheme | You confirm unclear words first; a human recheck is available within 24 hours |
| Study plan and crash-program planner | Builds and re-plans the study schedule | Topics are dropped only with the learner's or guardian's agreement |
| Advice cards and reports | Explain progress and suggest next steps, with reasons and evidence | A person reviews high-impact decisions |
| Similarity and proctoring checks | Flag possible copying or rule-breaking in tests | A person always decides; you can appeal |
| "Any course" valuation | Values a course that is not in the catalogue and explains the price | The admin can review and override; the price is shown before you buy |
| Translation | Translates lessons into the six app languages | Native reviewers check translations; exam terms stay in the exam's language |

## 6.3 How the AI is built

6.3.1 **Own servers first.** NexaTute's AI runs on our own servers in the USA, using open models. Speech recognition and AI voices are self-hosted, and avatars and replay voices run in your browser. Model names and versions are listed on each course's trust page and may change as better models become available.

6.3.2 **Cloud back-up for adults only.** When our servers are overloaded, an adult plan holder's text question to the Nexa Co-Teacher may be sent to a cloud AI provider. Personal data is removed first, and the provider must keep no data and not train on it. This never happens for:

- learners under 18 or PlayBoom;
- Free Beta or free-tier users;
- live Nexa Teacher classes;
- written-answer scripts.

6.3.3 **Verified answers.** Answers that reviewers have checked are saved in a verified answer store and used first. This reduces errors over time.

6.3.4 **Quality gate.** A course is sold in a language only after it passes accuracy tests in that language. Courses that meet our accuracy threshold carry the "NexaTute Verified" badge with their measured accuracy. Courses valued live by the AI are labelled "AI-generated" until they pass the gate.

## 6.4 Limits of the AI

6.4.1 **AI can make mistakes.** Under every answer we show: "AI can make mistakes. If something looks wrong, check with your teacher or report it."

6.4.2 **Wrong-answer reports.** "Report wrong answer" sends the answer to a review queue. A confirmed error is corrected in the verified answer store, and you are told. Each week we review the explanations rated weakest. For confirmed wrong answers in paid 1:1 sessions, see Refund Policy 4.5.

6.4.3 **Uncertain answers.** Mistake analysis is shown as a "possible cause" unless it is certain. Where there is not enough data, the app says "Not enough data" instead of guessing a score.

6.4.4 **Not official.** Practice exams are not recognised by any education board. Smart Suggestion sets are for practice, not a guarantee of what will come. The Exam and Admission Hub is not an official service.

6.4.5 **Fair speaking assessment.** Speaking tests are marked on clarity, fluency, vocabulary and grammar, never on having one particular accent. We test for bias across regional accents, and a person reviews samples and every appeal.

## 6.5 What the AI does not do

6.5.1 The AI does not:

- read emotions from your face or voice. The face is used only to check that the account owner is present. "Stuck" or "didn't understand" signals come from your actions in the lesson, such as time on a step or taps, not from your face [lawyer to confirm this is not emotion recognition under the EU AI Act];
- make final decisions about cheating, suspension, account closure, refunds or appeals. A person does;
- write graded work for you, such as SOPs, essays or school assignments, to submit as your own;
- give medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice;
- profile learners under 18 for engagement or offers. These features are locked off for them;
- use adaptive (changing) tests for board-style mocks, ranks or certificates. Only placement and smart practice adapt.

## 6.6 AI-generated media

6.6.1 Short videos and recaps made with AI (for example "Wrapped" recaps, mistake videos and guardian tips) are labelled as AI-generated and carry a C2PA content label.

6.6.2 NexaTute avatars and voices are AI. Teachers' and guardians' cloned voices are made only from their own voice, with their consent. A child's real voice is never cloned.

## 6.7 Use of data to improve the AI

6.7.1 We improve teaching mainly from ratings, wrong-answer reports and reviewed answers.

6.7.2 Using a learner's data, with personal details removed, to train or tune our AI needs consent: the guardian's consent for a learner under 18. Adults can turn this off at any time in Settings.

6.7.3 We do not train AI on the data of US children under 13.

6.7.4 Written-answer scripts and recordings are never sent to outside AI services.
