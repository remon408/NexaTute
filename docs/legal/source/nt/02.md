# 2. Privacy Policy

> **In short**
> - We collect only what we need to teach: account details, learning progress, and, if you choose, recordings and face verification. We do not sell your data and we show no advertising on NexaTute.
> - For learners under 18, a guardian must consent. We do not profile, track or target advertising at children. Their learning data is used only to teach them.
> - Our servers are in the USA. We protect your data with encryption, access controls and contracts with every provider.
> - You can see, download, correct and delete your data. A guardian can do this for a child.
> - Draft for lawyer review, 30 October 2026. Not yet in force.

## 2.2 The data we collect

2.2.1 **Account data.**

- Learner: name, phone number or email, date of birth, country, language, class or course, and a nickname for community spaces.
- Guardian: name, phone number, relationship to the learner, and the OTP check.
- Institution staff: name, role and work contact details.

2.2.2 **Consent records.** Every consent choice, who made it, when, and the version of the text shown.

2.2.3 **Learning data.** This includes:

- answers, test results and marks;
- mastery levels, mistakes and the error notebook;
- the study plan, class attendance and time spent learning;
- reviews done, certificates and badges;
- the class summaries and reports made from them.

2.2.4 **Interaction logs.** Actions in a lesson, such as how long you take to answer, pauses, hints used, skips, rewinds and "I didn't understand" taps. We use these to see whether a learner is stuck and to adjust the lesson. We do not read emotions from the camera or the voice.

2.2.5 **Voice.** When you speak in a class, your voice is converted to text on our own servers so the Nexa Teacher can reply. We do not store a transcript of your own voice. Your audio is stored only if you choose to record the class (see the Recording Policy). Pronunciation scoring runs in your browser.

2.2.6 **Face verification.** With consent (the guardian's consent for a learner under 18), we check in your browser that the learner is the account owner, with a liveness check and random re-checks. The face template is encrypted and used only for this check. It is never used to read mood or attention and never sent to an outside AI service. PlayBoom does not use face verification.

2.2.7 **Images and files.** This includes:

- photos of homework and notebook pages;
- written-answer scripts and handwriting;
- notes, and files in your storage.

We remove location data from images before storing them.

2.2.8 **Community posts.** Questions, answers and comments in the Study Hall, batch chat and polls, shown under your nickname.

2.2.9 **Proctoring data.** Only for certificate exams and an institution's own exams, and only with consent:

- a face check at the start and random presence checks;
- browser focus and tab events;
- for adults only, webcam snapshots every few minutes. There is never video.

2.2.10 **Payment data.** What you bought, the amount, the date, the payment method type and the transaction reference. Card numbers and mobile wallet PINs are handled by the payment provider. We never see or store them.

2.2.11 **Support and feedback.** Messages, ticket details and, if you allow it, an automatic screenshot of the page with the time and device.

2.2.12 **Device and technical data.** IP address, device type, browser, app version, connection quality, crash reports and security logs.

2.2.13 **Cookies.** See the Cookie Notice.

2.2.14 **Optional features for adults.**

- Job pathway: a job-ready profile shared with employers only with your consent.
- Employer tests and recruitment tests.
- Sponsor information.

2.2.15 **What we do not collect.** We do not collect:

- advertising IDs or third-party trackers in the learning app;
- live location;
- passwords or OTPs of other systems (for example education-board or bank accounts);
- data from other people's devices.

## 2.4 Learning analytics: limited to teaching

2.4.1 **For every learner,** learning analytics (answer correctness, mastery, mistakes, pace and "stuck" signals from interaction logs) are used to teach that learner. For example, they choose the next question, re-plan the week, prepare the guardian report and show the learner's own dashboard. A person reviews any high-impact decision.

2.4.2 **For learners under 18,** we use learning data only for teaching and for the guardian's reports. Specifically:

- **No profiling** beyond what is needed to teach. The optional personalisation features are locked off for every learner under 18: engagement prediction, interest maps, learning-style detection, smart reminder timing and personalised offers. A guardian cannot switch them on until our lawyer confirms the law allows it [lawyer to confirm under the Personal Data Protection Act 2026].
- **No tracking.** No third-party trackers, no session recording on the website, no tracking across other websites or apps.
- **No targeted advertising.** NexaTute shows no advertising to anyone. Marketing cookies are never offered to learners under 18. We do not build marketing lead records or profiles of minors.
- **No cross-product offers.** Learners under 18 are never shown offers for Remon Motion Studio, NexaLogicDev or any other RemonLogic or third-party product, and their data is never shared with those products. Interest-based suggestions, cross-product offers and discounts are shown only to users aged 18 or over; under-18 learners can take the free Motion Studio course with guardian consent and see no offers.
- **Marketplace.** In the shared RemonLogic marketplace, learners under 18 see only NexaTute learning items, and every purchase needs the guardian's approval.
- **Purchases.** NexaTute plan and pass options may appear as neutral information, for example at the Help button. They are not chosen or timed using the child's learning analytics. Any purchase needs the guardian's approval [lawyer to confirm whether these prompts should be shown only to the guardian].
- **Reminders.** Only at a time the learner or guardian chooses, never chosen by AI, and never after 9 pm.

2.4.3 **For adults,** each optional personalisation feature has its own on/off switch, chosen at purchase or sign-up and changeable at any time in Settings. Turning a switch off does not affect your lessons.

2.4.4 **Statistics.** We publish or share only counts and anonymised statistics. Groups with fewer than 20 learners are hidden. For example, the NexaTute Learning Atlas shows topic difficulty by district.

## 2.8 How we protect data

2.8.1 Our protections include:

- encryption (TLS in transit, managed keys at rest);
- signed links valid for 15 minutes for private recordings;
- role-based access with "no access by default";
- two-person approval for data deletion and exports;
- a full action log;
- regular security tests;
- a 24/7 rota of human controllers for safety alerts.

2.8.2 No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to cause you significant harm, we will tell the data authority and you without undue delay, and within 72 hours where possible (default, editable) [lawyer to confirm the deadline in the regulations].

## 2.9 Your rights

2.9.1 You can:

- see the data we hold about you;
- download your data in a common format (one click in Settings; a link valid 7 days is sent to your notifications);
- correct wrong data (we correct it within 30 days);
- delete your data or your account (see the Data Deletion Instructions);
- withdraw any consent at any time, with immediate effect for the future;
- object to, or ask a person to review, any decision made only by automated means;
- complain to us and to the data protection authority.

2.9.2 For a learner under 18, the guardian uses these rights. Some records must be kept by law, for example payment records and consent logs. If so, we tell you which records and why.

2.9.3 **How to ask.** Use Settings, then Privacy, or write to the DPO. We check your identity with an OTP. We answer within 30 days. Requests are free.

2.9.4 **Complaints.** If you are unhappy with our answer, you may complain to the data authority set up under the Personal Data Protection Act 2026 and the National Data Management Act 2026 [lawyer to confirm the authority's name and contact].
