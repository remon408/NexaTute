This policy explains when you get money back, how much, how it is paid and how fast. Words such as "wallet", "storyboard" and "delivery speed" have the meaning given in the Terms of Service.

## 2.1 Our promise in short

2.1.1 You pay for a video only when you approve its storyboard and see its full price. Shorter videos, failed renders and late deliveries are refunded automatically. Our own defects are fixed free. Wrong or duplicate charges are reversed at once. You can cancel your package at any time in one click.

## 2.3 Package fees

2.3.1 Monthly package, new purchase. If you cancel within 30 days of a new purchase, the monthly fee is refunded in full only if no video was generated in that billing period. If a video was generated, the fee is not refunded, and the package stays active until the end of the period.

2.3.2 Monthly package, renewals and cancellations after 30 days. Cancelling stops the next renewal. The current period is not refunded, and you keep the package until its end date.

2.3.3 Annual package. You can cancel an annual package at any time. The refund is the amount you paid minus the monthly package price for each month started (including the current month), and is never less than zero (default, editable) [owner to confirm; this replaces the general "no refund after 30 days" rule for annual packages]. The package then ends at the end of the current month.

2.3.4 Upgrade and downgrade. An upgrade is charged pro rata for the rest of the period and is not refunded separately. A downgrade takes effect at the next renewal, so no refund arises.

2.3.5 Main feature removed. If we remove a main feature of your paid package during a paid period, you may cancel and receive a pro-rata refund of the unused part of the period.

2.3.6 Terms changed against you. If you cancel because of a material change to the Terms of Service before it applies to you, you receive a pro-rata refund of the unused part of a prepaid period.

2.3.7 Storage add-ons follow the rules for monthly packages.

## 2.8 Cancellation

2.8.1 You cancel a package in one click in Settings, under "Package", and receive a confirmation by email.

2.8.2 You can cancel a video order before you approve its storyboard without any charge for the video. After approval, rendering starts at once, so the order cannot be cancelled; the refund cases in 2.2 still apply.

2.8.3 When your package ends, your files stay available to download for 30 days (default, editable), and we tell you before anything is deleted.

## 2.9 Cases with no refund

2.9.1 Except where the law requires otherwise, there is no refund for a video delivered as you approved it because you changed your mind; for monthly package periods after the first 30 days (see 2.3 for annual packages); for free promotional credits, coupons and expired balances; for platform decisions (for example a YouTube claim, lost monetisation or a rejected ad); or when we close an account for a serious breach, as set out in the Terms of Service [lawyer to confirm].
