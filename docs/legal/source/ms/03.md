# 3. Acceptable Use and Content Policy

> Draft for lawyer review, not yet published. Last updated: [to insert]. This policy is part of the Terms of Service of Remon Motion Studio, provided by RemonLogic Private Limited.

Remon Motion Studio is a tool for honest creative and business videos. This policy sets out what you may not make, upload, publish or ask the AI for, and what happens if the rules are broken. It applies to every input and output, the AI Co-Director, WhatsApp and Messenger ordering, the API and content published through the Service.

## 3.1 How we apply this policy

3.1.1 Safety checks run on what you ask for and on what the system produces: prompts, scripts, captions, titles, uploads, generated images and video frames, and generated audio. A harmless prompt does not make a harmful output acceptable.

3.1.2 Clear breaches are blocked automatically. Unclear cases go to a trained person. A person, not only the AI, decides every suspension, closure and takedown.

3.1.3 We define banned categories narrowly. Ordinary criticism of governments, politicians and organisations, news reporting, education, art, satire and parody are allowed as long as they do not fall into a banned category.

3.1.4 Where the law of Bangladesh or of the country where content is published sets a stricter rule, that rule also applies.

## 3.2 Content that is never allowed

3.2.1 Child safety has zero tolerance. Any sexual or sexualised content involving a minor (anyone under 18), real or AI-generated, drawn, animated or described, is banned, including content that sexualises child characters. Uploads and outputs are checked against hash lists of known child sexual abuse material. Any match or confirmed report is blocked permanently, the account is closed at once and the case is reported to the authorities as the law requires [lawyer to confirm the Bangladeshi reporting route and the NCMEC reporting arrangement]. Content that grooms, exploits, endangers or bullies children, or shares their personal data, is also banned.

3.2.3 Ads and business videos must be true. Businesses that need a licence, registration or approval (for example recruiting agencies, clinics, financial services, real-estate developers and education providers) must give its details and documents when we ask and show the licence number where the business pack asks for it. Our system checks them automatically where it can; a check is not an endorsement, and we never label a business "verified". The business alone is responsible for its documents being genuine, valid and current (Terms of Service, 1.11.7). If a check fails or is disputed, email the Business licence verification email on our Contact page [set in admin: Business licence verification email] and a person reviews it. We may block or remove videos whose licence is false, expired or disputed. Our pre-publish claim check compares prices and offers with your saved price list and warns you about mismatches. The AI fact-checks scripts and flags claims it cannot support; you approve the final script and remain responsible for its facts.

3.2.4 Talking photos made from one picture of a real person are not offered. Voice and face rules are in the Voice, Face and Likeness Consent Policy.

## 3.3 Islamic content rules

3.3.1 Islamic content receives a 25% discount on per-minute rates. You choose the Islamic or General plan for each project. If a General project is clearly Islamic, the system explains the difference and offers a switch; it never switches without your choice.

3.3.2 Quran verses and hadith are checked against a verified text. A quotation that does not match goes to a human religious reviewer, and unverified quotations are never labelled as verified.

3.3.3 Arabic text, including Quran verses, is always added as a text overlay and is never drawn by the video model, so that it cannot be misspelt or distorted. Prayer scenes are always reviewed by a person.

3.3.4 Presenting invented religious rulings as authentic is banned.

3.3.5 Choosing the Islamic plan for content that is not Islamic in order to get the discount is a misuse: we charge the normal rate and may apply the enforcement ladder.

3.3.6 Human review takes 3 to 7 days. You can appeal a reviewer's decision (3.6).

## 3.4 Platform rules you must also follow

3.4.1 When you publish, the platform's rules apply. YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok require disclosure of realistic AI content, and YouTube and Facebook restrict monetisation of mass-produced or repetitive ("inauthentic") content. We warn you at order and before publishing. Remaking a video or changing its voice does not make content comply with these rules.

3.4.2 Faceless autopilot and bulk features need your approval of each item, show sources, never copy others' text and have a daily cap.

3.4.3 Videos made in kids-safe mode are set as "Made for kids" on YouTube automatically. You must not change this for content made for children.

3.4.4 Under-18 profiles (used under a guardian's account) always have the kids-safe strict filters on and cannot use the features listed in the Terms of Service, 1.3.3.

## 3.5 Enforcement

3.5.1 When a rule is broken, we normally follow this ladder:

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Warning | The content is blocked or removed, and you are told which rule was broken |
| 2. Temporary suspension | Creating and publishing are paused for [to insert, for example 7 days] (default, editable); you can still download your files |
| 3. Permanent closure | The account is closed and you may not open a new one |

3.5.2 We skip steps and act at once for child sexual content (immediate closure and a report to the authorities), for a complaint that a voice or face was used without consent (takedown within 24 hours and suspension during review), and for scams, fraud, terrorist content and content a court or authority orders removed.

3.5.3 We may also remove a single video, stop direct publishing, limit features, withhold the "Quality Checked" badge or charge the normal rate (3.3.5).

3.5.4 Every decision is logged with its reason, and we tell you the decision and the reason unless the law or a safety risk (for example an ongoing child-safety or fraud investigation) prevents it. We share records of breaches with police, courts or regulators when the law requires it or to protect someone from serious harm.

## 3.6 Appeals

3.6.1 You can appeal any decision within 7 days (default, editable) from the notice in the app or by email to the Disputes email on our Contact page [set in admin: Disputes email].

3.6.2 A different person from the one who made the first decision reviews the appeal and replies within [to insert, for example 5] working days (default, editable). If the appeal succeeds, we restore the content or account, remove the step from your record and refund any charge caused by the mistake.

3.6.3 Closure for child sexual abuse material can be appealed only to show that the match was an error.

## 3.7 Reporting content

3.7.1 Every video shared through the Service has a "Report" button, and anyone can report content by email, with or without an account: child-safety concerns to the Child-safety reports email on our Contact page [set in admin: Child-safety reports email], and other content to the Support email there [set in admin: Support email]. Tell us what the content is, where it is and which rule it breaks. Voice and face complaints follow the Voice, Face and Likeness Consent Policy; copyright complaints the Copyright, Takedown and Repeat-Infringer Policy. Urgent reports (child safety, non-consensual likeness, imminent violence) are handled first.
