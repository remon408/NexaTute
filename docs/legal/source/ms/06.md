6.2.3 Content Credentials can be lost if a video is re-encoded by some editing tools or apps. The invisible watermark is designed to remain. You must not remove or alter these marks in order to mislead.

6.2.4 Our public verification tool checks whether a file carries our watermark or Content Credentials. It confirms only whether a video was made with Remon Motion Studio. It cannot tell whether other content was made with AI.

## 6.3 EU AI Act and other transparency rules

6.3.1 The EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), Article 50, sets transparency duties that have applied since 2 August 2026:

- providers of AI systems that interact with people must tell them they are dealing with AI (Article 50(1)). The AI Co-Director and our support assistant say so at the start of each conversation;
- providers of systems that generate audio, images, video or text must mark outputs in a machine-readable, detectable way (Article 50(2)). Our Content Credentials and watermarks are designed to meet this;
- people who publish deepfakes, meaning realistic content resembling real people, places or events, must label them clearly and visibly (Article 50(4)). For evidently artistic, satirical or fictional work, the label may be shown in a way that does not spoil the work.

6.3.2 RemonLogic is the provider of the Motion Studio AI system. When you publish a video, you are the deployer. Our hidden marks do not meet your own duty to label deepfakes. If you publish realistic AI content of people, places or events to audiences in the EU, you must show a visible AI label. When we publish for you through the Service, we add the platform's AI label [lawyer to confirm the scope of the Article 50 duties for RemonLogic and for its users, and any duty under the EU Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content].

6.3.3 YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok require creators to disclose realistic AI-generated or altered content. When you publish yourself, use the platform's AI disclosure option.

6.3.4 Other laws may also require AI labels, for example on political or election content, in advertising and on content for children. You are responsible for following them where you publish.

## 6.4 What the AI can and cannot do

| The AI can | The AI cannot |
|---|---|
| Turn your idea, script, document or link into a script and storyboard for you to approve | Be free of mistakes: scripts may contain wrong facts, translations may miss meaning, scenes may show errors in hands, text or physics. Review everything before you publish |
| Generate scenes, characters, voices, music and video, and keep characters and locations consistent | Prove that a claim is true: fact checks only flag claims it cannot support |
| Suggest emotions, hooks, titles and trends, and check scripts for facts, bad language and policy issues | Give professional advice: on medical, legal and financial subjects the AI Co-Director gives general information only |
| Translate, dub and lip-sync into the languages that have passed our quality gate | Guarantee views, sales, monetisation, platform approval or copyright protection |
| Find and fix many technical defects (broken frames, faces, hands, flicker, caption clipping) before delivery | Decide about you: a person makes every suspension, closure and takedown decision, and unsure Islamic-content checks go to a human reviewer |

6.4.1 On-screen text, including Arabic and Bangla, is added as an overlay and is never drawn by the image or video model, so that it is spelt correctly.

## 6.5 Emotion features are creative controls, not emotion reading

6.5.1 Motion Studio offers emotion controls for voices and characters: neutral, happy, sad, tearful, angry, afraid, whisper and excited, with intensity, matched facial expressions, body language and continuity across scenes.

6.5.2 These are creative controls. They decide how a character or voice performs in your video. The AI may suggest an emotion for each line from the meaning of the script; you can change any suggestion.

6.5.3 Motion Studio does not detect, infer or record the emotions, mood, attention or personality of you, your team, your viewers or anyone else from their face, voice or behaviour. It is not an emotion-recognition system.

6.5.4 Webcam acting (performance transfer) copies your movements and expressions onto a character. It does not label or store what you are feeling.

6.5.5 The viewer panel that rates emotion quality before updates is made of people who rate our outputs, not an automated system that reads viewers.
