This Privacy Policy explains how RemonLogic Private Limited collects, uses, shares, protects and deletes personal data when you use Remon Motion Studio (remonlogicstudio.com, its apps, API and its WhatsApp and Messenger ordering channels). It applies to users, team members, people who give voice or face consent, people who view review links or our pages, and people who contact us.

## 7.1 Summary

7.1.1 We collect what we need to make your videos, take payment, keep the Service safe and meet the law. Voice and face recordings are used only with explicit consent, kept in separate encrypted storage that no staff member can play or view, and deleted within 72 hours if consent is withdrawn. We do not sell personal data, show advertising on our sites, or use your content, voice or face to train AI for other customers. Our servers are in the United States (and possibly the Netherlands). You can see, download, correct and delete your data. The Service is for adults (18+) only.

## 7.4 Voice and face data (sensitive data)

7.4.1 Voice recordings, voice models, face videos and face models can identify a person and are treated as biometric, sensitive data under the laws in 7.11 to 7.13.

7.4.2 We process them only with the explicit consent of the person whose voice or face it is, given live in the app and confirmed by OTP, to create and use the voice clone, digital twin or performance in the account that made it, and to check that the consent and cloning recordings are the same person.

7.4.3 They are stored in a separate encrypted store with keys held in our key-management system. No staff member can play, view or download them; only the cloning and rendering services can decrypt them inside the job that needs them. They are never sent to your browser, to rented overflow servers, or to outside AI services.

7.4.4 They are deleted when you delete them, when you delete your account, or within 72 hours after consent is withdrawn. Consent records are kept for 3 years after deletion for disputes and legal claims (see 7.8).

7.4.5 We never record or clone a person under 18.

7.9.2 If a personal data breach is likely to harm you, we tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.
