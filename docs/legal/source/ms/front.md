## Choices for the lawyer

1. Wallet: Option A (approved non-cash-out prepaid credit) or Option B (pay per order, refunds to the original method, no stored balance) — Terms 1.7, Refund Policy 2.5 and 2.6. The research found that Bangladesh Bank's Prepaid Instrument Guidelines 2024 appear to require approval for a closed prepaid instrument, with eligibility a new company cannot meet (5 years in business, Tk 20 crore paid-up capital) and no exemption for non-cash-out credit. Keep one option everywhere.
2. Contracting entity for international customers and for the DMCA agent: RemonLogic Private Limited or the planned US affiliate (Terms 1.1.3, Copyright 5.4.2).
3. Governing law: Bangladesh and the Dhaka courts, with or without arbitration for business customers; foreign consumers' mandatory rights (Terms 1.20.3).
4. Liability cap and indemnity against consumers (Terms 1.18.4, 1.19.1); prevailing language (Terms 1.21.2); unused balance on closure for breach or deletion (Terms 1.7.7, Data Deletion 9.6.1).
