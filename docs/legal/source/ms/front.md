## What changed in this revision

- Wallet removed: Terms 1.7 is now one clause, "Payment for orders (no stored balance)"; Refund Policy 2.5 (wallet) deleted, the old 2.6 to 2.10 renumbered 2.5 to 2.9, and the refund set-off removed (small refunds are batched monthly to the original method).
- No stored value: minute packs, gift vouchers, reseller purchases and NexaLogicDev orders are named plan codes or per-order prices, never a Taka balance and never transferable (Terms 1.7.5, 1.15.1; Refund 2.4).
- Under-18s: allowed only as a profile under a guardian's account, with forced kids-safe filters, no clone, twin, face or webcam acting, bulk messages, API or direct publishing, US under-13s blocked, and offers and interest suggestions for 18+ only (Terms 1.3; Privacy 7.14, 7.16.2; Content 3.4.4; Consent 4.1.3).

- AI Co-Director: chats kept 180 days, memory until deleted, never used for training, crisis wording with helpline information and a person (Terms 1.13; AI Notice 6.6.3; Privacy 7.8).
- Free account and Free Beta: lifetime free account (3 min a month, 30 s each, 480p, "Remix this" watermark, personal use, 1 GB, 24-month inactivity deletion, phone and device limits); Free Beta with all features within daily limits and a watermarked business test licence; clone, twin and face acting only after legal clearance (Terms 1.10; Consent 4.1.8, 4.2.4; AI Notice 6.2.1).
- Discounts: Islamic and Slow discounts always apply, one promotional discount (the largest), annual discount on the fee only, caps 45% and 30%, price floor (Terms 1.6.9).
- Late delivery: Normal and Slow late → 100% of the order's charge back to the original method automatically; Express unchanged (Terms 1.9.1; Refund 2.2.1).
- Bulk messages, regulated businesses and the marketplace: company-paid bulk sends within the package allowance under the client's own sender name (Terms 1.14.4); licence checks never shown as "verified", client alone responsible (Terms 1.11.7; Content 3.2.3); one marketplace for three products with no cloned voices or twins (Terms 1.15.2).
- Consumer rules: EU/UK 14-day withdrawal (Refund 2.7.4); auto-renewal never pre-selected, with reminders (Terms 1.6.3); 30 days' notice and active re-acceptance of changes (Terms 1.21.1; Privacy 7.17.1); international card sales limited where tax registration is missing (Terms 1.6.12).
- Legal requests, entity and contacts: AI opinion, a person decides (Privacy 7.6.5); RemonLogic Private Limited contracts with everyone until a USA company is lawfully formed through doola.com (Terms 1.1.3; Copyright 5.4.2); every contact email is an admin-set placeholder; brand-impersonation warning (Terms 1.4.5).
