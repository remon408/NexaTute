# 4. Voice, Face and Likeness Consent Policy

A person's voice and face belong to that person. This policy explains when Motion Studio lets you use a real person's voice or face, how consent is given and recorded, how consent is withdrawn, and what we do when someone complains. It applies to voice clones, digital-twin presenters, webcam acting, any real person's face in a video, stock presenters and child-character voices.

## 4.1 The core rules

4.1.1 No real person's voice or face is cloned, animated or used without that person's live, verified consent.

4.1.2 You may clone or animate your own voice and face. You may use another adult's voice or face only with that person's direct consent, given by them in the app and confirmed by a one-time password (OTP) on their own phone.

4.1.3 Everyone who gives consent must be 18 or over. Before the first voice clone or digital twin we may check age in a proportionate way [to insert method, for example an identity-document check with the image deleted after the check] [lawyer to confirm]. Under-18 profiles used under a guardian's account cannot use voice cloning, digital twins, face or webcam acting, or real people's faces (Terms of Service, 1.3.3).

4.1.4 Not allowed without extra proof:

- politicians, celebrities and religious figures, unless you upload written permission from that person (or their authorised representative) and we verify it;
- deceased persons, unless you upload written permission from their legal heirs and we verify it [lawyer to confirm who counts as heirs and what proof is needed, for example a succession certificate].

4.1.5 Never allowed:

- the voice or face of anyone under 18, even with a parent's or guardian's consent;
- cloning from WhatsApp or Messenger voice notes, phone recordings, videos found online or any other recording not made live in the app for this purpose;
- making a digital twin from a single photo or selfie.

4.1.6 A voice clone or digital twin can be used only inside the account that made it. It cannot be sold, transferred, shared with another account or listed on a marketplace.

4.1.7 A voice clone or digital twin may never be used for political speech, to ask anyone for money, for fraud, to get past voice or face security checks, or for sexual or obscene content.

4.1.8 Voice cloning, digital twins and face or webcam acting are switched on only after our legal review is complete, including in the Free Beta. They are not available on the Free Trial or the free account.

## 4.2 Voice clones (Voice Clone Studio)

4.2.1 Voice cloning is done only in the Voice Clone Studio inside the Motion Studio app, on a phone or computer. It takes about 10 to 12 minutes.

4.2.2 The steps are:

1. Microphone test with a noise meter.
2. Consent: the voice owner reads aloud, live, a consent sentence generated freshly for this session. It includes the date, the account name and the words "I consent". Pre-recorded audio is not accepted.
3. If the voice owner is not the account holder, the voice owner also confirms by OTP sent to their own phone number.
4. About 3 minutes of natural reading.
5. Emotion cards: short lines read in at least three emotions (neutral, happy, sad); more emotions can be added later.
6. Speaker check: the system compares the consent recording with the cloning recording to confirm they are the same person. If they do not match, the clone is not made.
7. Preview, and "use this voice".

4.2.3 Before cloning, noise and echo are removed from the recording. Cleaning never replaces the consent check.

4.2.4 On a paid package, the first 2 voice clones per account are included for lifetime use; further clones have a one-time fee shown in the app.

## 4.3 Digital-twin presenters

4.3.1 A digital twin is a presenter that looks and speaks like you. It is made from a video of about 2 minutes of the account holder, recorded for this purpose.

4.3.2 Digital twins are of your own face only. The same consent steps apply as for voice clones: a live consent sentence, OTP, 18+ and use only in your account.

4.3.3 Packages include a set number of digital twins; extra twins have a one-time fee shown in the app.

## 4.4 Webcam acting (performance transfer)

4.4.1 Webcam acting lets you act out a scene on your camera and transfer your facial expressions, head movement and voice performance to a character or to your own digital twin.

4.4.2 You may only use your own face and performance, after giving consent in the app. The capture is used only to drive that scene and is handled like other face data under 4.8.

4.4.3 Webcam acting is a creative control; it does not analyse or record your emotions.

## 4.5 Real people in your videos and uploads

4.5.1 Any real person's face used in a generated video (for example a business owner presenting a product) needs that person's consent and OTP, like a voice.

4.5.2 When you dub or translate an uploaded video, only voices with verified consent are cloned. Other speakers in the video receive library voices.

4.5.3 You are responsible for having the rights and permissions for people who appear in footage you upload (for example a model release for an actor in your own ad).

4.5.4 Photo-realistic likeness of public figures is blocked without proof of permission (4.1.4).

## 4.6 Stock presenters and library voices

4.6.1 Stock presenters and library voices are recorded from paid, consenting actors and voice artists who signed releases, or are fully synthetic.

4.6.2 Their releases limit how they may be used. You may not use a stock presenter or library voice (default, editable, to be aligned with the releases):

- for political campaigns or endorsements;
- for religious preaching presented as the performer's own belief [lawyer to confirm, against the actors' releases];
- to present the performer as a real doctor, lawyer, financial adviser or customer giving a testimonial;
- in sexual, dating, gambling, alcohol, tobacco or other content the Acceptable Use and Content Policy bans;
- in any way the presenter's card in the library marks as not allowed [to insert per release].

4.6.3 Stock presenters and library voices are labelled as AI-generated where the law or platform rules require.

## 4.7 Child-character voices

4.7.1 Child-character voices (boy and girl, ages 5 to 8 and 9 to 12) come only from consenting, paid adult voice artists or are fully synthetic designed voices.

4.7.2 An adult may make their own cloned voice sound child-like.

4.7.3 A real child's voice is never recorded for cloning, cloned or used, even with a parent's or guardian's consent.

4.7.4 Projects with child characters automatically use kids-safe strict filters.

## 4.8 How we protect voice and face data

4.8.1 Voice recordings, voice models, digital-twin source videos and face models are biometric-type data. They are kept in a separate encrypted store.

4.8.2 No RemonLogic staff member can listen to or view them. Only the cloning and rendering services can decrypt them, inside the job that needs them. A cloned voice model is never sent to your browser.

4.8.3 This data is never used to train models for other customers and is never sent to rented cloud servers used for overflow capacity.

## 4.9 Labels and watermarks

4.9.1 Every output carries Content Credentials (C2PA) metadata showing that it was made with AI.

4.9.2 Every output with a cloned voice carries an inaudible audio watermark identifying it as a cloned voice.

4.9.3 When you publish directly through the Service, the platform's AI-content option is ticked automatically and an "AI voice" or "Made with AI" label is added. Digital-twin outputs also carry a watermark.

4.9.4 If you publish yourself, you must disclose realistic AI voices and faces as the platform and the law require (for example the EU AI Act deepfake disclosure).

## 4.10 Withdrawing consent

4.10.1 Anyone who gave consent can withdraw it at any time: the account holder from the voice or twin settings, and a third party by using the link in their OTP message (default, editable) or by writing to the Consent and likeness complaints email on our Contact page [set in admin: Consent & likeness complaints email].

4.10.2 From the moment of withdrawal no new renders use that voice or face. The voice model, face model and recordings are deleted within 72 hours.

4.10.3 Videos already delivered before the withdrawal are the account holder's responsibility. If the person who withdrew consent asks for a published video to be removed, the account holder must remove it from where they published it within [to insert] (default, editable) [lawyer to confirm the extent of this duty under Bangladeshi law and the publicity laws of other countries].

4.10.4 Deleting the account deletes all voice clones and digital twins on the same timeline.

## 4.11 Consent records

4.11.1 For every voice clone and digital twin we keep a consent record: the consent recording, date and time, device, the speaker-check score and the OTP result.

4.11.2 Consent records are kept in an encrypted registry for 3 years after the voice or face model is deleted, so that we can answer complaints and legal claims. Then they are deleted.

4.11.3 Only our Data Protection Officer and assigned reviewers can open a consent record, and every opening is logged.

## 4.12 Complaints about misuse of a voice or face

4.12.1 If you believe your voice or face was used in Motion Studio without your consent, report it with the Report button on the video or by email to the Consent and likeness complaints email on our Contact page [set in admin: Consent & likeness complaints email]. You do not need an account.

4.12.2 Tell us who you are, where the content is, and why you believe it uses your voice or face. We may ask for proof of identity, which we use only for this complaint.

4.12.3 We take the content down within 24 hours of a complaint and suspend the account concerned while we review it.

4.12.4 We check the consent record and the speaker check. If consent is missing or false, the voice or face model is deleted, the account is closed and we keep the evidence for any legal claim. If valid consent exists, we explain this to the complainant, who may still withdraw consent under 4.10 if they gave it.

4.12.5 For videos re-uploaded elsewhere, we can provide an evidence report from our watermark and Content Credentials to support a complaint to that platform.
