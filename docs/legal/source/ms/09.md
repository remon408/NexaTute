# 9. Data Deletion Instructions

> Draft for lawyer review, not yet published. Last updated: [to insert]. Provided by RemonLogic Private Limited for Remon Motion Studio. This page is the data deletion instructions page required by Meta for apps that use Facebook Login, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp or Messenger.

You can delete your Remon Motion Studio data at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted, what we must keep, and how long it takes.

## 9.1 Ways to ask for deletion

### 9.1.1 Delete your whole account in the app

1. Log in to Remon Motion Studio.
2. Go to Settings, then Account, then "Delete account".
3. Download anything you want to keep first, using "Export all projects".
4. Confirm with the one-time password (OTP) sent to your phone or email. We confirm by email when deletion is complete.

### 9.1.2 Delete only some data

- Projects, videos and uploads: delete them in the studio. Deleted items go to Trash for 30 days, then are deleted; you can empty Trash at once.
- Voice clones and digital twins: in Settings, under "Voices and twins", choose "Delete" or "Withdraw consent". They are deleted within 72 hours.
- AI Co-Director memory: in Settings, under "AI Co-Director", view and delete what it remembers.
- Connected channels: in Settings, under "Connected accounts", choose "Disconnect". We delete the access tokens at once.

### 9.1.3 If you used Facebook Login or connected Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp or Messenger

1. In Facebook, open "Apps and websites" in Settings [to insert the current menu path at publication], find Remon Motion Studio and choose "Remove". This stops our access.
2. To delete the data we already hold, ask Facebook to send us a deletion request, or use 9.1.1 or 9.1.4. For a request through Facebook we give you a confirmation code and a page to check the status [to insert status page address].

### 9.1.4 By email or by message

Write to [to insert email] or send "Delete my data" to our official WhatsApp or Messenger account. Give the phone number or email address of your account. We confirm your identity by OTP before we delete anything. If you gave voice or face consent for someone else's account, use the withdrawal link in your OTP message or write to us; your voice or face model is deleted within 72 hours and no new videos use it.

## 9.2 What is deleted

9.2.1 When you delete your account we delete your profile and login details; all projects, scripts, storyboards, uploads and finished videos; voice clones, voice recordings, digital twins, face videos and character or style models made for you; AI Co-Director messages and memory; connected-channel tokens and the analytics we fetched for you; and your marketing preferences (keeping only a note not to contact you, if you opted out).

9.3.1 Kept records have restricted access and are used for nothing else.

## 9.4 How long deletion takes

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Access tokens of connected channels | At once |
| Voice and face models and recordings | Within 72 hours |
| Account and all other data in the live systems | Within 30 days of the request (default, editable) |
| Copies in backups | Within about 8 weeks, as backups expire |

## 9.5 What we cannot delete for you

9.5.1 Videos you published to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or elsewhere, including through our app, stay there until you delete them on that platform. Videos you downloaded or sent to others stay on their devices.

9.5.2 Data that NexaTute holds for its own services (for example if you took the free course) is deleted under NexaTute's own deletion process [to insert reference].
