## Purpose

This pack gives the lawyer a ready first draft of Clause III (Objects) of the Memorandum of Association, in English with an unofficial Bangla translation (see 01.md). The aim is one clause that covers every business the owner already runs or plans: NexaTute (AI tutoring and education), Remon Motion Studio (AI video), NexaLogicDev (AI cloud IDE / no-code app building), software and SaaS, ITES export, data processing, digital marketing, marketplaces and partner programmes.

**Why it is drafted broadly.** Under the Companies Act, 1994 the company may act only within its stated objects. Changing the objects later needs a special resolution and confirmation by the High Court Division (ss.12–13; example: Plastic Touch Industries Ltd, Company Matter 1544/2025, confirmed 14 Oct 2025 on conditions, then filing with the RJSC). Getting the objects right at incorporation avoids that court step (research note, item 11). The draft is broad, but each object is still specific enough to show a real business line.

## How a Bangladeshi MoA is usually structured [lawyer to confirm]

1. Clause I: Name ("The name of the Company is RemonLogic Private Limited").
2. Clause II: Registered office ("The registered office of the Company will be situated in Bangladesh").
3. Clause III: Objects. Usually first the main objects (the businesses the company is formed to carry on), then ancillary and incidental objects (powers that support them: borrowing, investing, owning property and IP, employing staff, opening branches), ending with a general "all other lawful things incidental or conducive" clause. Many MoAs add a statement that each sub-clause is an independent object.
4. Clause IV: Liability of members is limited.
5. Clause V: Authorised share capital, divided into shares of a stated value [to insert].
6. Subscribers' (association) clause: names, addresses, descriptions and shares taken by each subscriber, signed and witnessed.

The Articles of Association are a separate document (share transfer limits for a private company, directors, meetings, signing powers).
