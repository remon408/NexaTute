## Shared decisions (owner-approved 24 Oct 2026) — Avatar & Voice
- New platform-admin module "Avatar & Voice Studio" (X22) + user-side screens (X23).
- 3D avatars render in the learner's browser with lip-sync (no server render cost). Stock voices are self-hosted TTS in 6 languages (bn, en, hi, ur, ar, fr), male/female, styles calm / energetic / fun. Cloned voices are generated on the server GPU (same engine as Remon Motion Studio voice cloning), cached, with a daily GPU-minute cap and off-peak processing.
- Who adds what: learners only CHOOSE from the library (no clone, no photo avatar under 18); guardians may clone THEIR OWN voice so PlayBoom's Play Buddy reads stories in it (never the child's voice; other family members such as grandparents abroad each give their own live consent on their own phone with OTP); verified teachers (marketplace / institution teachers) may clone their own voice and make an avatar from their own face photo (face check with liveness, matched to their verified face) — used ONLY in their own content (notes, mock tests the AI explains); learners 18+ may clone their own voice for pronunciation practice ("hear correct English in your own voice"); admin adds official Nexa Teacher avatars and voices.
- Pricing (owner): for each of the four — Premium avatars, guardian voice clone, teacher voice and avatar, 18+ learner own-voice clone — 1 is free, the rest are bought from the marketplace.
- Claude's defaults (owner can change; shown as editable settings): Premium avatar ৳149 / $2; each extra voice clone ৳299 / $4 (USD = BDT × 0.015 rounded; Taka via aamarPay in Bangladesh, USD via Paddle abroad, tax by Paddle by customer country). Under-18 learners: 1 free Premium pick + the rest unlock with XP only — never with money (existing rule: "new looks unlock with XP, never with money for children"; PlayBoom has no child purchases). Marketplace sellers at launch = NexaTute's own items; creator-made avatars (20% commission) is an "off — later" option. Cloned voices are never sold or transferred.
- Safety (Motion Studio rules): consent = live in-app reading of a freshly generated sentence (date + account name), no pre-recorded audio; speaker verification matches consent voice to the sample; third person confirms by OTP on their own phone; no under-18 voices or faces; no politicians, celebrities or religious figures without written permission proof; no deceased persons without heirs' permission; usable only in that account; no political speech, money requests, fraud or obscene use; every output has C2PA metadata + inaudible audio watermark (AudioSeal) + an "AI voice" label; withdraw consent any time → voice model deleted within 72 hours; consent records kept 3 years after deletion; unauthorised-clone complaint → takedown within 24 hours and account suspended pending review.
- Admin approvals: avatar upload by content & programs role → review (incl. cultural/attire check for all countries) → two-person approval → live; versions + one-click rollback. Clone requests approved by the Nexa Guard controller (maker can't approve own). Price changes need two-person approval (finance lead + founder). Everything logged.
