Applies to NexaTute, Remon Motion Studio and NexaLogicDev. Not built yet. All values admin-editable, marked "(default)", two-person approval, logged.

## A. Connected accounts (sign-up + social connectors)
- Login/connect ONLY through each platform's official OAuth button; never ask for or store a password; limited-permission tokens kept encrypted in the secrets vault; "Disconnect" button and the granted permissions shown per account.
- NexaTute: sign-up with Google / Facebook / Apple; social connectors (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) only for 18+ users (e.g. sharing Project Studio videos); no auto-posting option for under-18.
- Motion Studio: client settings → "Connected accounts" (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) with direct posting.
- NexaLogicDev: connect Google and GitHub the same way.
- Admin panels: company pages/channels connected the same way (NexaTute X19 already does this — keep it).
- Direct posting per platform switches on only after that platform's app review; until then: download or the phone's Share sheet.

## B. Share options ("free marketing, no harm")
What can be shared:
- NexaTute: Project Studio final video and poster, verified badges, QR certificates, competition results, "Most Improved" scholarship, chapter milestone cards, learner portfolio page.
- Motion Studio: finished videos with a short "Made with Remon Motion Studio" end card (required on free plans, can be switched off on paid plans — default, editable).
- NexaLogicDev: built apps/websites with a "Built with NexaLogicDev" badge and a showcase page.

How:
- "Share" button on each item: download, copy link, QR code, phone Share sheet (WhatsApp, Facebook, Messenger, LinkedIn, Instagram — no API review needed); direct post for 18+ with connected accounts.
- Institutions: co-branded (school logo + NexaTute) result/achievement posts.

Marketing built in:
- Small product mark + verify link/QR (e.g. nexatute.com/v/XXXX) on every share; Open Graph preview card in 6 languages; the public verify page shows the item + a "Start free" button.
- Admin/Marketing Hub KPIs: shares, views, sign-ups from shares (counts only).

Protection:
- Certificates/badges verifiable by QR; revoked ones show "revoked"; AI-made items carry the AI label + C2PA.
- Nexa Guard checks before sharing; never other people's names, scores or ranks.
- Share texts only from admin-approved templates; no fake numbers or "guarantee" claims.
- Under-18: public share needs guardian approval; first name only, no location, face optional; no tracking on children's pages; guardian can switch the link off any time.
- Report button on every public page; takedown within 24 h.
- No money or coins for sharing (never for children).

Admin controls: shareable item types, templates, brand mark, age rules, "Made with" card per plan.
