## B. Gaps still open (max 10)

1. **Age assurance for 18+ features.** Clone, twin, face and webcam acting, bulk sends, marketplace selling and the adult Co-Director rely on a typed date of birth. With under-18 accounts now allowed (A4), a child can lie once and reach deepfake tools. This needs a proportionate check (for example an NID or passport check with the image deleted, or an adult payment-instrument check) before the first clone or twin.
2. **Nexa ID cross-product rules.** One login spans a child's NexaTute profile, Motion Studio and NexaLogicDev. Nothing defines how age and guardian control carry over, which product's consent applies, what is shared, or what deleting one product deletes. Without this, the age rules and the no-offers rule leak through the shared account.
3. **International merchant-of-record fallback.** Research item 18 says Paddle's acceptance of AI voice and avatar tools is unconfirmed, and Stripe and PayPal are ruled out. If Paddle declines, EUR, GBP and USD sales rest on aamarPay cards alone. Name a second MoR to apply to in parallel (for example FastSpring or 2Checkout/Verifone).
4. **Shared marketplace operating model.** Not yet decided: merchant of record or agent; settlement through a licensed PSP or escrow (Bangladesh Bank e-commerce circular); seller KYC and sanctions checks; VAT on the commission or the gross; withholding on local and foreign seller payouts; IP vetting and takedown for third-party goods; buyer refunds; visibility to under-18s. All are needed before three products and outside sellers share one store.
5. **Business verification (KYB) tiers.** Reseller KYC exists. Nothing checks the business before API keys, bulk sends paid by the company, white-label, hosted shoppable pages, post-paid Enterprise credit or event-triggered videos. These are the routes for scaled spam, scams and unpaid bills.
6. **App-store billing for the Motion Studio and NexaLogicDev apps.** The Capacitor app is planned for Q2 2027. Apple and Google require in-app purchase for digital content, and per-minute, per-order pricing does not map onto it. NexaTute has a +30% rule; Motion Studio and NexaLogicDev have none. Decide between web-only purchase, store IAP products, or a reader-style app.

## D. Feasibility flags (max 8)

1. **"All features" in a 20 Dec 2026 Free Beta.** Infrastructure is not ready for it: the fleet is rented from 5 Nov, the 1-week 8× A6000 test (no FP8) has not run, Cinematic 1080p Native and 4K are unbenchmarked, and live avatar streaming, API, SCORM, marketplace and autopilot are not built.
   - Alternative: every feature visible in the Beta. GPU-heavy tiers come from a tiny daily pool. Features that are not built show "coming soon in the Beta" and switch on mid-Beta as they pass their gate. The owner has already accepted that the date moves.
2. **Pay per order for automated, high-frequency and tiny charges.** API orders, autopilot, event-triggered and bulk videos, hook variants, remakes and ৳5 "shorter video" refunds each need a gateway transaction with a fee, and aamarPay partial refunds and recurring tokens are unconfirmed (Memo 2.7).
   - Alternative:
     - authorise the estimated price at "approve & pay" and capture the actual amount after render (card pre-authorisation, or bKash authorisation and capture), so most shorter-video refunds disappear;
     - a tokenised bKash agreement or card on file, with explicit consent, for API and autopilot charges within a spending cap;
     - monthly post-paid invoicing only for KYB-verified businesses (Enterprise kit pattern);
     - small refunds batched monthly.
   - Confirm each with aamarPay before building.
3. **Company-paid bulk messages capped only at 3 sends a day.** A send can go to 10,000 recipients, so cost is unbounded. WhatsApp requires the business the recipient opted in to be the sender. BTRC sender-mask rules apply. Spam reports can ban the company's own number, which also carries orders and OTPs.
   - Alternative:
     - per-package allowance of recipients a month and a cap per send;
     - beyond the allowance the client pays per message at cost;
     - send from the client's own WhatsApp Business number (embedded signup) or the client's registered SMS mask;
     - keep the company's own number for transactional messages only.
4. **USA LLC "will be opened" via doola.** Owning a foreign company from Bangladesh normally needs prior Bangladesh Bank or Government approval, which is rarely given to a new startup. Funding it or keeping proceeds abroad without approval breaches the FX Act (Memo 1.4, 5.4). Formation is easy; lawful ownership and funding are the blocker.
   - Alternative: apply to Paddle as RemonLogic Private Limited (Bangladesh is a supported seller country); register the DMCA agent in the company's name or through an agent service; ask NCMEC directly, with the paid IWF list as a fallback; form the LLC only after a written FX opinion and approval.
5. **"AI auto-verifies" regulated-business licences.** BMET recruiting-licence lists, DGHS clinic licences and RAJUK approvals have no reliable public API, and scraping may breach their terms. If the company advertises verification, the "client alone responsible, no liability" clause is weak under the CRPA 2009 (misleading service).
   - Alternative: an automatic format check plus a registry lookup where an official source exists. Human review for high-risk sectors (recruiting, health, finance, education) before export. Never show "verified" to viewers. Wording as in C7.
6. **Timeline from incorporation to children's data.** The RJSC application is filed on 1 Nov, and the NexaTute Free Beta with under-18s starts 20 Nov. Still needed first: certificate, trade licence, TIN, BIN, bank account, RemonLogic's own aamarPay account, Meta Business Verification (needs registered-entity documents), YouTube API audit, DPO, DPAs and DPIAs (Memo 15 items 11–30). Collecting children's data before the controller exists is the biggest legal hole.
   - Alternative: run the 2-week closed pilots with adults only, start the public NexaTute Beta for under-18s only once the company exists and the items marked for the Free Beta are done, or move the NexaTute Beta date.
7. **Lifetime free accounts at scale.** Every free account gets 1 GB forever, 10 Co-Director messages a day on the own LLM, 1,000 hosted-page views and 3 minutes a month. Without deleting inactive accounts and with only a trial device check, storage and LLM load grow without limit and multi-account farming is cheap.
   - Alternative: bill storage by use, not allowance. Delete free accounts inactive for 24 months, with notice. Free Co-Director messages run on a small model in the lowest priority lane and pause at degradation level L1. Phone OTP plus device check for every free account, not only the trial. Cap free accounts per device and phone.
8. **"A human decides" plus two-person approval on everything, with a very small team.** Every value change needs 2 approvals (protected settings need 3 with a 24-hour delay). Every suspension, takedown, appeal, dispute, regulated-business dispute and legal request needs a person. Add 24-hour takedowns, Islamic reviews, 24-hour English human support and a 24/7 child-safety rota for NexaTute. This cannot be staffed at launch.
   - Alternative: use the settings registry's lock levels so that only money, legal and child settings need extra approvals; batched approval sessions; per-queue SLAs by severity; an outsourced or part-time trust-and-safety on-call for nights. Measure queue load in the pilot before promising times on public pages.
