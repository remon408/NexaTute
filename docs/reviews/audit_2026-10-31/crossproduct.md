# Cross-product audit: NexaTute · Remon Motion Studio · NexaLogicDev (step 2 of docs/tasks/cross-product-audit.md)

Date: 31 Oct 2026. Prepared by Claude (Opus) as a draft for the owner. This is not legal advice: Claude is not a lawyer, and every legal point goes to the Bangladeshi lawyer. Values Claude chose are marked **(default, editable)**. Points that are not settled are marked **(to confirm)**. Every price below was recalculated with Python (USD = Taka × 0.015, rounded to the nearest dollar, an exact .5 rounds down).

**Source keys.** dec:N = `docs/decisions.md` line N (newest lines win). CPA:N = `docs/tasks/cross-product-audit.md`. BP:N = blueprint text `scratchpad/blueprint.txt` line N (v32.10). MSBP xx:N = `docs/motion-studio/source/xx.md`. GS = `docs/reviews/gap_solutions_2026-10-30.md`. R3 = `docs/reviews/review3_2026-10-30.md`. MSG = `docs/reviews/motion-studio_gaps_admin_2026-10-30.md`. IVS = `docs/tasks/internal-video-service.md`. FCL = `docs/tasks/free-class-links.md`. SHR = `docs/tasks/sharing-and-connectors.md`. UCG = `docs/tasks/unified-credit-governance.md`. mem/x:N = `docs/memory/areas/x.md` line N. CL = `CLAUDE.md`.

**The nine criteria** (the codes used in column 4): **F** user flow · **T** technical connection · **$** who pays and which book · **C** priority and capacity · **S** safety and under-18 · **P** privacy and consent · **R** storage and retention · **X** failure handling · **A** admin controls in EVERY affected product's panel. A code in the "missing" column means that criterion is missing or contradicted. Criteria not listed are defined well enough.

**Priority.** P0 = blocks a launch (NexaTute Free Beta 15 Jan / paid 5 Feb 2027; Motion Studio Free Beta 30 Jan / paid 20 Feb; NexaLogicDev Free Beta 15 Apr / paid 6 May; dec:188, dec:190), or touches children's data, money or the law before that launch. P1 = must be settled before the Free Beta of the products involved. P2 = later.
