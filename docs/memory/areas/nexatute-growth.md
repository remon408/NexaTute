## Engagement intelligence — Facebook-style behaviour prediction for learning (approved 18 Oct 2026; not yet in the doc)
- [stated] Aim: predict from behaviour like Facebook, but to make learners learn more, not stay longer; success is measured by mastery gain and regular study, not time in the app
- [stated] (1) Learner-state detection from interaction logs — answer time, pauses, repeated mistakes, recording rewinds, hints, skips → understanding / stuck / bored / tired, and the AI changes method or pace, gives a break or an easy win (scikit-learn / LightGBM); (2) "next activity" recommendation like a Facebook feed — ranks the next lesson, quiz, Short or challenge by expected learning and interest, learning daily from what the learner does (Vowpal Wabbit contextual bandit)
- [stated] (3) Smart reminder timing — learns when each learner studies and reminds then, at most 1–2 a day, none to children after 9 pm; (4) drop-out prediction — falling logins, broken streak or falling accuracy → 7 days ahead the AI Teacher sends a personal message, an easy win and a guardian tip, supporting the ≤10% churn target (LightGBM)
- [stated] (5) Interest map from questions and choices (cricket, games, cooking…) so examples use the learner's interests (pgvector); (6) personal Shorts feed ranked by weak topics and interests, a quiz after every 10 Shorts, no infinite scroll; (7) A/B test every change (buttons, reminder text, explanation type) on small groups and keep what teaches best (Unleash + ClickHouse)
- [stated] Guardrails: no camera or voice mood/emotion reading (face stays for presence only; emotion inference in education is banned under the EU AI Act); log-based "understanding/stuck" signals get a legal review before launch; no infinite scroll, autoplay or "stay a bit longer" reward loops for children; guardian daily time limit and quiet hours; every suggestion shows "why am I seeing this"
- [stated] Architecture: no GPU or new servers — runs on App-server CPU using the event data already in ClickHouse; the software register adds Vowpal Wabbit (BSD-3-Clause) and LightGBM (MIT)
