- [stated] AI Marketing Hub — Digital Marketing Automation (blueprint 3.26) must appear in the platform admin panel; being built as its own prototype X19 (24 Oct 2026)
- [stated] Event-driven journeys (approved 24 Oct 2026): NexaTute sends events (demo requested, application started/abandoned, enrollment done, institute onboarding step stuck, webinar registered/attended, inactive days, placement confirmed); each journey = trigger → wait → condition → segment → channel (push, email, SMS, WhatsApp) → stop at the goal; reports entered → messaged → goal (application/enrollment) with a 10% holdout group to measure real lift
- [stated] Journeys: demo requested but application unfinished (reminders at 24 h and 72 h with chat help, stop on completion or opt-out); institute onboarding (nudge the institution admin per stuck step, after 7 days alert the partnership manager); webinar follow-up (no-show gets the recording, attendee gets thanks + demo link; attendance → demo → enrollment reported); course recommendations (from the learner's own goals/syllabus/progress, max 1 a week, only with the "personalised offers" consent switch); inactive re-engagement (learning-first messages at 3/7/14 days, no guilt or fear wording, stop after 3); placement success stories (placement module, 18+ only, written consent, AI draft → approval → publish, views → leads reported)
- [stated] Journey rules: no marketing messages to under-18s directly — guardian only, with guardian consent; no behaviour-based targeting of minors; consent and opt-out per channel; marketing messages max 1 a day and 3 a week; quiet hours 9 pm–8 am; each journey and its message templates approved once, then sends run automatically; no grades or health details in messages; no new servers (Valkey/Redis streams, in-house CRM and notification service, ClickHouse reports; WhatsApp Business API as the paid exception)
- [stated] One shared RemonLogic Growth Platform for NexaTute, Remon Motion Studio and NexaLogicDev (approved 24 Oct 2026): AI Marketing Hub + CRM + journeys + target-client database, each product with its own brand, accounts, mailboxes, data, pipeline, owners and approvers
- [stated] Target-client database (approved 24 Oct 2026; replaces "capture the whole world's data", which would break data laws and platform terms): a worldwide database of organisations, not individuals' personal data — sources: government/open data (e.g. Bangladesh school and education-board lists, OpenStreetMap, Wikidata), organisations' own public contact pages (respecting robots.txt and site terms), official APIs, public fair/exhibitor lists, our own form/chat/webinar leads; every field records its source and lawful basis; prospects with no response for 12 months are deleted
- [stated] AI target finding: ideal customer profiles per product (NexaTute — schools, colleges, coaching centres, universities, employers, sponsors; Motion Studio — agencies, small businesses, education and Islamic content organisations; NexaLogicDev — software agencies, freelancers, SMEs with outdated sites, startups); fit score (size, type, area, language) + intent signals from public information (new branch, hiring, exam results, outdated site); weekly top-target list; a person approves each campaign before it starts
- [stated] Country rules engine: consent-first countries (e.g. Canada) get no cold email — only content, ads and inbound; EU/UK/US by their own rules to official addresses with unsubscribe; Bangladesh rules confirmed by the lawyer; never personal addresses or bought email lists; individuals such as creators and developers reached only via inbound, content or ads
- [stated] One CRM record per organisation across the three products (Nexa ID shows which products it uses); cross-selling another product only with consent; one unsubscribe stops marketing from all three products; no new servers (website reading in the night batch on CPU with the existing Scrapy, extraction by local Qwen3, PostgreSQL + pgvector for look-alike organisations, email checks only by format and domain — no probing other mail servers)
- [stated] Landing pages (24 Oct 2026): a NexaTute landing page (nexatute.com) connected to the Landing Page AI comes first and must be fully commercial and international-standard; Motion Studio and NexaLogicDev landing pages will be built later

## Channels, import/export and target clients (approved 25 Oct 2026)
- [stated] Going with a "Channels" page in the AI Marketing Hub: a Connect button per platform (OAuth with the company's official account; we never see or keep passwords), chosen page/channel and permissions shown, tokens encrypted with auto-refresh and expiry alerts, super admin connects and the security owner approves, separate accounts per product (NexaTute, Motion Studio, NexaLogicDev); YouTube (Google Cloud + YouTube Data API, audit before public API uploads), Facebook + Instagram (Meta app, Instagram Business linked to a Page, App Review), LinkedIn (Community Management API approval)
- [stated] X (Twitter) is left out for now because its API is paid; decide later after checking the price
- [stated] Going with bulk Import (Excel/CSV/PDF, AI column mapping, duplicate/format/country-rule checks, source and lawful basis required per import, preview, approval, batch ID with one-click undo; organisations only, no bought lists or personal emails), the AI target-finding screen, Export (Excel/CSV/PDF with chosen fields, OTP, log, exporter watermark, link expires in 24 h, role-limited) and a "Target clients" list (filters, weekly top list with "why this target", map, saved segments, add to campaign with approval, owner, CRM record, unsubscribed never targeted)
- [stated] Import and export are approved by the marketing admin, plus the DPO when personal data is involved
- [stated] In X19 the new parts stay as tabs inside existing sections — Channels under "Publishing & channels"; Target clients, Import, AI target finding and Export under "Target clients & CRM" — not separate menu sections (final, 25 Oct 2026)
