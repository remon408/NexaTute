## Voice-cloning consent policy (approved 21 Oct 2026)
- [stated] Only own voice, or another person's with direct consent; consent = live in-app reading of a freshly generated consent sentence (date, account name), no pre-recorded audio; speaker verification matches consent voice to the cloning sample; third party confirms by OTP on their own phone
- [stated] Not allowed: under-18 voices; politicians, celebrities or religious figures without written permission proof; deceased persons without heirs' permission
- [stated] Cloned voice usable only in that account (not transferable or sold); no political speech, money requests, fraud or obscene use; 4.15 applies
- [stated] Every output carries C2PA metadata + inaudible audio watermark (AudioSeal) and an "AI voice" label when published; speaker verification via SpeechBrain ECAPA (licences to verify)
- [stated] Consent can be withdrawn anytime → voice model deleted within 72 hours; consent records kept 3 years after deletion; unauthorised-clone complaints → takedown within 24 hours and account suspended pending review

## Content policy 4.15 additions (approved 21 Oct 2026)
- [stated] Adds: deepfakes/impersonation shown as news or evidence (stricter for election candidates); fake news mimicking real outlets; scam ads (fake investment, fake jobs/overseas job lures, fake lotteries); gambling/betting, drugs, weapons promotion; health misinformation; Quran/hadith quotes checked against a verified text, mismatches go to human review; C2PA + "Made with AI" label on outputs; definitions finalised by a Bangladeshi lawyer
