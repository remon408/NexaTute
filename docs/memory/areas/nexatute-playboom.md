## Basics (21 Oct 2026; part of NexaTute, not a separate product; name PlayBoom, not "PlayBloom"; source doc NexaTute_Live_Study_Hall_PlayBloom_Compatible.docx)
- [stated] Play class to Class III, game-based AI learning (Math, Bangla, English, AI Play Buddy voice/video, Handwriting Studio, parent dashboard); replaced Kids Mode (removed 21 Oct 2026 with its ৳400/৳700, ৳20 Daily Pass and $6/$11) as the only offer for children
- [stated] Price ৳599 per child per 30 days; annual 20% off rounded (৳5,750); USD $9/month, $86/year; 2nd child 30% off; Daily Pass ৳20; institution 25% off (20–99) / 35% (100+)
- [stated] Limits: max 75 min/day; AI Voice + Video 30 min/day combined; 10 successful handwriting scans/day; Play/Nursery/KG default 25 min/day, guardian may allow up to 3 × 25-min sessions with at least a 2-hour gap and a one-line note that under 1 hour/day is recommended at this age (guardian decides); Class I–III default 45 min/day, max 75; same price for all
- [stated] Content: Play/Nursery/KG voice-first with pictures and animation (letters, numbers, rhymes, phonics, colours, shapes; Handwriting Studio tracing first, then writing); Class I–III follow NCTB Math, Bangla, English
- [stated] Launches with NexaTute (dates in [[nexatute]] Core); uses the shared GPU system; follows NexaTute's minors rules (guardian consent, purchase approval, consent switches, 19.4), alignment delegated to Claude

## Approved additions (23 Oct 2026)
- [stated] PlayBoom additions (approved 23 Oct 2026; price, daily limits and no-ads/no-chat/no-links/no-child-purchase rules unchanged): story time (picture stories read by Play Buddy, child picks what happens next, tap a word to hear it, bedtime audio-only mode); letter & sound journey (Bangla vowels/consonants + English phonics with mouth animation, gentle in-browser pronunciation help, never "wrong"); rhymes & sing-along (own or copyright-free only, animated with Motion Studio); hands-on math (counters, number line, tangram, "grocery shop" game with real Bangladeshi taka coins/notes); world around me (animals, fruits, six seasons, body parts, hand-washing, road safety, festivals of all religions); little thinking games (matching, sorting, patterns, memory cards, mazes — not MindForge, no measuring); drawing & colouring saved only to the guardian's private gallery; feelings & manners stories (no emotion detection of the child)
- [stated] PlayBoom additions (cont.): sticker book + "my garden" that grows with learning stars (no purchases, loot boxes or streak pressure); Play Buddy dress-up unlocked by learning; 2-min movement breaks and eye rest; parents abroad or grandparents record stories/cheers the child hears (guardian-approved family only, private); "play at home today" screen-free family cards; guardian "ready for Class I" checklist (pre-reading, pre-math, fine motor) + weekly tip; calm mode for autism/speech delay (soft colours, no sudden sounds, picture routine; guardian turns on); classroom mode for kindergarten teachers on a TV/projector (institution discounts as before); offline packs downloaded on Wi-Fi; mostly browser-side, Play Buddy voice/video stays within 30 min/day, no new servers
- [stated] PlayBoom NCTB books (approved 23 Oct 2026): Class I and II follow their full NCTB textbooks by name, chapter by chapter — আমার বাংলা বই, English for Today and গণিত (lessons matched to the book's chapters so parents can follow school); Class III adds প্রাথমিক বিজ্ঞান, বাংলাদেশ ও বিশ্বপরিচয় and religion (Islam, Hindu, Buddhist or Christian — the guardian chooses); Class III also adds a religious-language book that follows the child's chosen religion, on by default and the guardian can turn it off — e.g. Islam → the Arabic book আদদুরুসুল আরাবিয়্যাহ, not only for Islam (24 Oct 2026); check the list against NCTB's official list before making content
- [stated] PlayBoom second-round additions (approved 23 Oct 2026): reading levels letter → word → sentence → story with in-browser read-aloud checks (Class I–III); dictation and conjunct letters (যুক্তবর্ণ) checked in Handwriting Studio (Class II–III); spoken "why?" box answered simply by Play Buddy (within the 30-min voice limit; guardian sees the questions); small home science with the guardian (float/sink, growing a seed, private photos); arrow-path coding puzzles from age 5; Bangladesh stories (copyright-free folk tales such as ঠাকুরমার ঝুলি, age-appropriate 21 Feb / 26 Mar / 16 Dec stories, map puzzle, rivers, doel and tiger); tabla/dhol/harmonium pads and clap-along rhythm game
- [stated] PlayBoom second-round additions (cont.): safety stories (water/drowning safety, fire and road, "if I get lost" incl. learning the guardian's phone number; age-appropriate body safety shown only if the guardian turns it on); healthy habits (2-min tooth-brushing song, vegetables, sleep routine, guardian-set bedtime lock); siblings taking turns on one phone in cooperative games (never online play with strangers); birthday greeting and festival themes; monthly private "my work" album PDF; printable worksheets for kindergartens; Bangla sign-language videos for key words, captions, high contrast; Class III "graduation" certificate into NexaTute Class 4 (MindForge from then)
- [stated] PlayBoom subjects follow each child's own country curriculum (standing rule, 23 Oct 2026)
- [stated] PlayBoom curricula (approved 23 Oct 2026): at setup the guardian picks the child's curriculum (the app suggests one from the country, guardian confirms); Bangladesh has three paths — NCTB Bangla medium (আমার বাংলা বই, English for Today, গণিত), NCTB English Version (same NCTB books in English, e.g. EV Mathematics, plus Bangla) and English medium (Cambridge Primary or Pearson Edexcel iPrimary, or "my school's own book list" that the AI maps to lessons and a content reviewer checks); other countries follow their own curriculum in line with the 6 languages (India CBSE or state boards, Pakistan national/provincial curriculum, Arab countries their ministry curriculum, France Éducation nationale), with Cambridge or IB for expat families; each country opens only after its content passes the quality gate, following the NexaTute launch order
- [stated] Bangladeshi children living abroad follow their host country's curriculum for school subjects plus a separate "learn Bangla" part to keep the mother tongue; Play/Nursery/KG stay skills-based (letters, numbers, colours) aligned with each country's pre-primary guidance, teaching language chosen by the guardian

## X5 prototype defaults (approved 24 Oct 2026 with the work report)
- [stated] Bangla-medium children see Bangla digits (১, ২, ৩) and Arabic children Arabic-Indic digits — the exception to Latin digits in the UI
- [stated] Movement break after every 2 games; one flower in "my garden" per 10 stars; one bedtime lock for the whole family (default 8 pm)
- [stated] Class III English Version uses NCTB EV books such as "EV Primary Science" and "EV Bangladesh and Global Studies" (names to check against NCTB's official list)
- [stated] Every country's curriculum must pass the quality check before children there get PlayBoom content, so no one has a bad experience or gives bad feedback (24 Oct 2026)
- [stated] Country curriculum quality check (approved 24 Oct 2026, instead of hiring a part-time teacher per country): (1) AI checks first — against the official curriculum, facts, language, age fit; (2) the platform's own verified teachers in that country act as "Teacher Reviewers" (institution teachers, free teacher-tool users, verified marketplace teachers) — every chapter needs 2 verified teachers' approval before it opens, rewarded with a free plan, a small per-chapter fee or a "Verified Reviewer" badge; (3) each country opens first as a labelled Beta for volunteer families with consent, fixes made from their feedback, then opens to everyone; (4) after launch, learners and guardians give feedback on every lesson (e.g. "doesn't match my school book") into a fix queue; no child sees content that hasn't passed the check
- [stated] Lawyer question: whether teacher/guardian approval changes NexaTute's liability for content (it is assumed liability stays with NexaTute)
