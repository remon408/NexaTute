# 14. Accounting, Cost Allocation and Unit Economics

Motion Studio keeps its own books inside the admin panel (owner requirement, 29 October 2026), on the company accounting system that RemonLogic already uses for NexaTute. This chapter sets out the accounting model, how shared costs are split fairly between products, how money from customers is recognised and reconciled, and how the cost and margin of each video minute are checked. Prices are in chapter 7; the admin pages, roles and approvals are in chapter 13.

> Accounting positions in this chapter are design rules for the system. Tax rates, the accrual or cash basis, revenue-recognition timing and the treatment of pre-launch costs are decided by the chartered accountant; legal points are checked by the lawyer.

## 14.1 Books and ledger structure

- Full double-entry books: chart of accounts (assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses; the accountant can add accounts), journal with balanced debit and credit, the month the entry is for, description, proof, made by and approved by; ledgers with running balances and sub-ledgers for creators (wallets), vendors, lenders and shareholders; trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow produced automatically.
- Most entries are made automatically from wallet top-ups, charges, refunds, package fees, payment-gateway settlements, vendor bills and payroll. Manual entries are made only by the finance role, with approval. Nothing is deleted, only reversed; closed months are locked.
- One company ledger: RemonLogic keeps one ledger, and every entry carries a product tag — Motion Studio, NexaTute, NexaLogicDev or common. Each product's "books" are the views of its tagged entries; there are not three separate legal ledgers.
- Funding-source tag: every entry also carries a funding tag (Funding A for NexaTute and Motion Studio; Funding B for NexaLogicDev), with investor reports per fund.
- Consolidated company report: the product books add up to one RemonLogic report, with internal transfers eliminated (for example NexaLogicDev credits spent on Motion Studio videos at ৳5 = 1 credit).
- Approvals follow the company approval table (default, editable): up to ৳1 lakh one approver other than the maker; ৳1–10 lakh the finance lead; over ৳10 lakh, any loan, share action or month close the finance lead and a founder; system entries batch-approved daily; nothing counts in reports until approved.
- Payroll, rent, vendors, loans and the share register use the company modules already designed for NexaTute; Motion Studio staff costs are tagged to Motion Studio, and shared staff are split by a fixed percentage (14.4).
- Export to Tally, QuickBooks and Excel; nightly encrypted export of the accounting database to the company's Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, with a monthly restore test.

## 14.2 Customer money and revenue recognition

| Event | Accounting treatment |
|---|---|
| Wallet top-up (aamarPay or Paddle) | Cash in; company liability to the creator (prepaid credit). The wallet is non-cash-out and non-transferable (lawyer check) |
| Shared Nexa ID wallet top-up | Company liability; becomes revenue of the product where it is spent, on the day it is spent |
| "Approve & pay" at storyboard | Wallet balance moves to Motion Studio as spent; whether revenue is recognised at this point or on delivery is set by the chartered accountant (to confirm). Until then it is held as Motion Studio unearned revenue |
| Settlement after render | Shorter video → difference back to the wallet (reversal entry); up to 10% longer → no extra charge |
| Failed or late render | Automatic wallet refund or credit as a reversal entry, with a receipt |
| Monthly package fee | Motion Studio revenue for the month it covers (accrual; accountant to confirm) |
| Pre-production fee | Motion Studio revenue when charged; refunded by reversal on technical failure |
| Pay-per-video and minute packs | As wallet spending; unused minute-pack balances stay a liability until used or expired under their terms |
| NexaLogicDev credits spent on Motion Studio | Internal transfer between product books; eliminated in the consolidated report |
| Reseller and marketplace commissions | Expense or contra-revenue as the accountant decides; tax withheld on payouts where required (accountant to confirm) |

Expired balances, if the terms allow expiry, are released from liability only under the rule the accountant and lawyer confirm (to confirm).

## 14.3 Payment reconciliation

- aamarPay (bKash, Nagad, cards, and international cards until Paddle is approved): every callback is verified on the server; each settlement is matched to orders by transaction ID and idempotency key and split into gross amount, gateway fee and net payout. Shortfalls are recorded as fees or chargebacks; unmatched receipts wait in a queue for the finance role.
- The aamarPay merchant account is held in the name of Vitlous Technology. Until it is moved to RemonLogic Private Limited (or an agreement between the two is in place), the money collected is recorded as received on RemonLogic's behalf; the agreement and its accounting and tax treatment are for the accountant and lawyer (to confirm).
- Paddle (international, when its API is approved): each payout is recorded as gross sale, Paddle fee (its own expense line) and buyer-country tax separately, and auto-matched to orders. The Paddle fee is a setting (package, percentage, fixed fee, effective date), changed with the finance lead's approval and logged. EUR, GBP and USD payouts follow the foreign-currency rule (14.6).
- Duplicate callbacks never double-credit; refunds and reversals are compensating entries; every charge and refund has a receipt in the creator's wallet history.
- Daily reconciliation report: orders vs gateway records vs ledger, with exceptions listed for action.

## 14.4 VAT and invoices

- The VAT rate is chosen automatically from the product tag; rates are set by the chartered accountant in settings and never hard-coded.
- Bangladesh prices include VAT; VAT 15/115 applies only to Bangladesh (aamarPay) revenue. Paddle revenue is booked at 0% Bangladesh VAT as an export of services (accountant to confirm).
- VAT (Mushak) invoices are issued for business clients; the VAT report is produced in the Mushak 9.1 return format for the chartered accountant. VAT registration (BIN) is part of the company registration checklist.
- Receipts show the VAT included for every charge.

## 14.5 Shared cost allocation

All servers run as one cluster with owner-first priority, and every product pays only for what it actually uses; no product pays for another's server just because it "owns" it (owner decision, 29 October 2026).

Meters (OpenCost, with DCGM Exporter and Kueue for GPUs):

| Cost | Meter used to split it |
|---|---|
| GPU servers | GPU-seconds per product, including per-request GPU time when products share an LLM server |
| CPU servers | CPU core-hours and RAM per product |
| Cloudflare R2 storage | GB stored per product (separate buckets and prefixes) |
| Cloudflare bandwidth | Bandwidth per product domain |
| Database | Database size and use per product |
| Emergency cloud GPU | Charged directly to the product that used it |
| Unmeterable costs (Cloudflare plan, monitoring, security tools, backups, domains, C2PA certificate) | "Common platform" pool, split by each product's total usage ratio |
| Shared staff salaries | Fixed percentage set by the admin (default, editable) |

Rules:

1. Each bill is split by its own meter (a GPU server by GPU use, a CPU server by CPU use).
2. Idle capacity is split in the same ratio as measured use (default, editable).
3. Free Trial, Free Beta and free users' costs stay with their own product.
4. Long-term bills (24-month server terms, setup fees, yearly licences) are spread monthly before allocation.
5. Foreign-currency bills are converted at one rate on one date before being split, so all products use the same rate.
6. Missing meter data: last month's ratio is used, an alert is raised and the difference is corrected next month.
7. Month-end allocation is computed automatically, posted to each product's books after two-person approval, and logged.
8. A copy of the meter data behind each month's allocation is kept with the accounting records as audit proof.
9. Allocation rules are locked for the financial year and changed only at the start of a year, with two approvals.
10. Pre-launch development cost is expensed or capitalised as the chartered accountant decides; both are supported.

Example (from the owner's decision): a $1,000 server bill used 60% by NexaTute, 30% by Motion Studio and 10% by NexaLogicDev is booked as $600, $300 and $100 in each product's expenses.

Borrowed capacity between funding budgets (for example Motion Studio renders on NexaLogicDev servers in their night lending window) is shown in the monthly report at cost per server-hour between Funding A and Funding B.

## 14.6 Budgets, alerts and controls

- Monthly budget per product and cost category (GPU, CPU, storage, bandwidth, cloud burst, SMS, WhatsApp, email, AI APIs, staff, marketing) with alerts at 80% and 100%; service budgets also have a set action at 100% (slow down or switch channel; cloud burst stops at its cap).
- Margin alert: when the cost per video-minute leaves a margin below 30% (default, editable) for any mode, tier or package, the finance lead and the product owner are alerted with the cause (rate, GPU time, retries or burst).
- Foreign-currency rule: one conversion rate and date per bill, recorded with the entry; USD bills paid at a different rate record the exchange gain or loss.

## 14.7 Reports

- Motion Studio profit and loss, and the consolidated company report with eliminations.
- Cost per video-minute by mode, tier, package and delivery tier; cost per creator; cost of free users (trial, beta) as a marketing cost of the product.
- Monthly cost trend and allocation history with the meter data attached.
- Wallet liability report: balances, ageing, spending, refunds and credits.
- Funding reports for investors per fund.
- Revenue by currency, gateway and country; VAT report.

## 14.8 Unit economics per video minute

The figures below are planning estimates on the baseline GPU fleet. They are replaced by measured results after the 1-week GPU test and the launch benchmark (chapter 10), and the admin dashboard then shows actual cost and margin per mode and tier.

### 14.8.1 Method

For each mode, tier and package the system computes, per finished output minute:

- Net rate = per-minute rate ÷ 1.15 for Bangladesh sales (VAT included in the price), or the Paddle net for international sales.
- Less payment-gateway fee and refunds (planning assumption until actuals exist: 2% fees and 1% refunds, as in the NexaTute model; default, editable).
- Less GPU cost = measured GPU-minutes per output minute × cost per GPU-minute at actual average use (the server's monthly rent ÷ minutes in the month ÷ use rate).
- Less other direct costs: safety and quality checks, re-renders and repairs, storage and delivery, cloud burst used, human review time.
- Margin = what remains ÷ net rate. Below 30% triggers the margin alert (14.6).

The monthly package fee covers storage and platform costs, and the pre-production fee covers the script, key-image and preview compute (estimated at about ৳3–10 per 2 minutes, including projects that never reach a final video).

### 14.8.2 GPU-minutes a rate can pay for (baseline fleet)

At the Master Blueprint baseline, renting one GPU-minute costs about ৳1.83 on an RTX PRO 6000, ৳1.02 on an RTX PRO 5000 and ৳0.53 on an RTX PRO 4000 at full use. The table applies the 29 October 2026 rates to the RTX PRO 6000 figure, before VAT, fees and other costs, as an upper bound: the benchmarked GPU-minutes per output minute must stay well below the 70%-use column. The numbers are computed by Claude from these sources (rate ÷ ৳1.83, and rate ÷ ৳2.61 for 70% use).

| Mode and tier (package) | Rate per minute | GPU-minutes covered, full use | At 70% average use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3D, 480p Native (Professional) | ৳25 | ≈14 | ≈10 |
| 3D, 720p Native (Starter) | ৳72 | ≈39 | ≈28 |
| Cinematic, 480p Native (Mini, Starter) | ৳41 | ≈22 | ≈16 |
| Cinematic, 720p Native (Starter) | ৳97 | ≈53 | ≈37 |
| Cinematic, 720p Native (Agency) | ৳84 | ≈46 | ≈32 |
| Cinematic, 1080p Escalating (Starter) | ৳111 | ≈61 | ≈42 |

Quick mode renders in the creator's browser and uses almost no GPU, so it carries the widest margin; Enhanced uses a key image and depth map per scene, so its GPU cost is low.

### 14.8.3 Margin check from the earlier cost study

The earlier cost check (Master Blueprint 35.1) counted the approved video optimisations (Lightning LoRA, LightX2V, FastWan, TeaCache, SageAttention, FP8, torch.compile and CUDA graphs, RIFE, A14B only for hero shots, AI-Director routing) at only half their expected gain and found:

- about 11 GPU-minutes per output minute for Cinematic 720p Native, about ৳16 at 70% GPU use;
- gross margin after VAT and gateway fees of about 65–77% across the Cinematic tiers, and about 65% for 1080p Native.

That check used the earlier Freelancer-package rate of ৳61 for Cinematic 720p Native; the 29 October 2026 rates for this tier (৳84–৳97) are higher, so the margin is recomputed by the same method after the benchmark rather than assumed. The optimisations must be live before paid packages start; if they have not passed, Cinematic 720p Native and higher tiers stay "coming soon" (chapter 7).

### 14.8.4 What changes the numbers

- The GPU test (8× RTX A6000) gives a different cost per GPU-minute and different GPU-minutes per output minute (A6000 has no FP8); the tables are recalculated from the chosen server's rent.
- Delivery mix: Express work at daytime peaks may need cloud burst, which is charged directly to Motion Studio; Normal and Slow work at night uses idle and lent capacity at lower cost.
- Two hero-shot candidates, 3 hook variants and multi-language packs raise GPU time per order; hook variants and extra languages are billed per minute, and the hero-shot candidates are included, so their cost is watched on the margin dashboard.
- Re-render and repair rates, quality-gate failures and free region repairs.
- Islamic content (−25%) and reseller discounts lower the rate; the margin alert works per rate cell, so these are covered.

> Rule: no margin, saving or capacity figure is shown to investors or customers as fact until it is measured; until then it is labelled a planning estimate.
