# 3. Product Overview, Creation Workflow and Render Modes

## 3.1 Product overview

Motion Studio takes a creator from an idea to a published video in one place. The creator can start from any of these inputs:

- a typed idea, topic or brief;
- their own script, pasted or uploaded (txt, docx, pdf, srt, fountain), used as-is or improved by the AI;
- a document or a link (product page, blog, Daraz or Shopify listing), from which the script, images and price are pulled;
- a WhatsApp or Messenger voice note with photos, with the preview and payment link returned in the chat;
- a template (product ad, Islamic reminder, greeting, news, education) or a business pack;
- a product catalogue (Shopify, WooCommerce or CSV), giving one ad per product;
- their own phone video, cleaned up by auto-edit, or a long video cut into Shorts.

Whatever the input, every project follows the same workflow, the same checks and the same billing rules. The features behind each input are described in chapters 4 and 5.

## 3.2 End-to-end creation workflow

| Step | What happens | Who acts | Charge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Idea and plan | Creator gives the input, chooses General or Islamic plan, format and language | Creator | None |
| 2. Script | AI writes or structures the script; per-line voice and emotion suggested; fact check; creator approves | AI, creator | None yet (see note below) |
| 3. Storyboard | Scene list, key images and a fast 480p preview; estimated length and full price shown | AI, creator | None |
| 4. Approve and pay | Creator approves the storyboard; the amount is taken from the wallet (bKash top-up if short) | Creator | Video price taken here |
| 5. Render | Scenes made in the browser or on the server by render mode | System | — |
| 6. Checks | Safety, quality, sync and (Islamic plan) source checks; failed scenes re-made | System, reviewers | Free |
| 7. Review | Watermarked preview for review; client review link with time-coded comments where the package allows | Creator, client | Free |
| 8. Delivery | Final file to storage or a 72-hour download link; small 360p WhatsApp copy | System | — |
| 9. Publish | Direct publishing or download; analytics in the dashboard | Creator | — |

> Nothing is charged before the creator approves the storyboard. Rendering starts only after the money is taken from the wallet.

### 3.2.1 Script and storyboard

The AI Director plans the video scene by scene and chooses the cheapest suitable path for each scene, so only hero shots use full video generation. Script and storyboard are versioned; every change is kept, and the creator approves each before the next step. The storyboard shows the estimated length and the full price for the chosen package, resolution and delivery tier. The storyboard stage includes a fixed number of free revisions (default, editable), and edited scenes' key images and previews can be re-made free up to 3 times per scene per day (default, editable).

The pre-production fee (৳20 per started 2 minutes of planned length; ৳15 for Islamic content) is unchanged in amount. Because the 29 October 2026 rule says nothing is charged before storyboard approval, the fee is expected to be taken together with the video price at "approve and pay" (to confirm). See chapter 7.

### 3.2.2 After the render

The final charge follows the real length of the finished video:

- shorter than estimated: the difference goes back to the wallet automatically;
- up to 10% longer: no extra charge (10% is a default, editable);
- failed render or missed delivery: automatic refund to the wallet (the Express late rule in 3.7 still applies).

After delivery, defects caused by Motion Studio are fixed free. Changes the client asks for are charged only for the minutes of the changed scenes. Packages from Freelancer Mini upward include one free repair of a marked region of a scene per video. Every charge and refund has a receipt in the wallet history.

## 3.3 Render modes

Motion Studio has three render modes. They differ in visual style and in where the work runs, not in delivery quality. One subscription covers all three. Quick and Enhanced use the 3D Animation rates; Cinematic uses the Cinematic rates (+35%); Islamic content gets 25% off in every mode.

| Mode | How scenes are made | Look | Highest tier | Where it runs | GPU cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quick | 2D and 3D characters and motion graphics drawn with Revideo and Three.js; 3D avatars with lip-sync; scene library | Animation, cartoon, explainer, Reels graphics; optional cinematic-look 3D style | 1080p Native (frames are drawn, not generated) | Creator's browser | None |
| Enhanced (2.5D) | An AI key image plus a depth map, animated with camera moves and parallax | Cinematic-looking still or slow scenes | 1080p Native | Key images on the server; motion and render in the browser | Low |
| Cinematic | Full AI video generation (Wan 2.2) for hero shots; other scenes by the cheaper paths | Most realistic AI video | 720p Native and 1080p Escalating; 1080p Native after its benchmark | Server | High, for hero shots only |

4K Escalating (upscaled from 1080p Native) is offered from Freelancer Starter upward at the 3D and Cinematic 4K rates; because it is made from 1080p Native, Cinematic 4K follows the Cinematic 1080p Native gate. The output ladder is 480p Native, 720p Escalating, 720p Native, 1080p Escalating, 1080p Native and 4K Escalating; base frame rate is 25 fps. Tiers, frame rates and formats are described in chapter 4; rates in chapter 7.

Two gates protect Cinematic quality before paid launch:

- If the video speed-ups (Lightning LoRA, LightX2V, FastWan) have not passed their tests before paid packages start, Cinematic 720p Native and higher stay "coming soon"; Cinematic 480p Native and 720p Escalating stay on.
- Until the Cinematic lip-sync model (Wan2.2-S2V-14B) passes its licence check and quality gate, dialogue scenes use Quick or Enhanced mode, and Cinematic uses narration and back or long shots.

## 3.4 What runs in the browser and what runs on the server

Work that a normal device can do runs in the creator's browser. This keeps prices low and leaves the GPU servers for work only they can do. The server keeps five kinds of work: large AI models, anything needing secret keys, results that must be trusted (money, safety, signatures), work that must continue after the tab closes, and the fallback for weak devices.

| Runs in the creator's browser | Runs on RemonLogic servers |
|---|---|
| Quick-mode render (Revideo, Three.js with WebGPU or WebGL2) | Script and storyboard LLM |
| 3D avatars and their lip-sync (Rhubarb Lip Sync, audio-based, works for Bangla and Urdu) | AI images (FLUX) including Enhanced key images |
| Enhanced 2.5D motion and render from server-made key images | Cinematic video, Cinematic avatars and their lip-sync |
| Captions, logos, Brand Kit and thumbnail text | Final voice generation: library, dialect and child-character voices, emotions, cloned voices and dubbing |
| Audio mixing and loudness (Web Audio) | Escalating-tier upscaling and the quality gate |
| Auto-reframe and Shorts scene detection | Safety checks on text, frames, audio and uploads |
| Final MP4 assembly for Quick and Enhanced (WebCodecs, Mediabunny) | Checks on browser-made chunks; final encode and validation |
| Timeline editor, teleprompter, uploads straight to storage | Content Credentials (C2PA) signing, storage, delivery, payments |
| Preview upscaling and product background removal (strong devices only) | Fallback render for weak or disconnected devices |

Final voice generation runs on the server, because emotional library voices, child-character and dialect voices, cloned voices and dubbing all need server models. The in-house VITS voices approved earlier for the browser stay available there for quick previews on capable devices (default, editable). The final voice track is made once at the preview stage and reused for the final render, so the voice and lip-sync stay the same wherever the video is drawn.

Anything that decides money, safety or the signed record is computed or verified on the server; the browser result is shown first, and the server result is the record.

### 3.4.1 Device tiers

At first sign-in, and after a browser update, a Device Check measures memory, WebGPU and WebAssembly support, free storage and connection speed, and sets the tier. The creator can see and change it in settings.

| Tier | Devices | What runs where |
|---|---|---|
| Full | Android 10 or later with at least 4 GB RAM, recent Chrome or Edge, laptops and desktops | All browser features |
| Light | Android with 3 GB RAM; Safari and iPhone | Browser features without heavy models; upscaling and Enhanced render may use the server |
| Server fallback | Below 3 GB RAM | Mainly on the server |

When an order starts, the studio also runs a 3-second test render. Phones, tablets and devices whose estimated render time is above the admin limit (default 6 times the video length) render on the server from the start. Server rendering costs the creator the same price; the ETA may be longer at busy times. Data Saver is on by default on mobile data, so large browser models download only on Wi-Fi or with consent.

## 3.5 Resume and fallback rules

Long renders must survive sleep, closed tabs, lost connections and server faults without losing work or charging twice.

1. **Chunked rendering.** Each scene, or each 10-second segment, is rendered and uploaded to storage as a separate resumable chunk. All files the job needs (characters, textures, voice track, music, fonts) are downloaded before the first frame, so rendering continues offline. Frames are drawn in a background worker, so rendering continues when the tab is in the background.
2. **Offline and restart.** Finished chunks wait in the browser's private storage while offline and upload when the connection returns. If the computer is switched off, reopening the studio resumes from the next unfinished chunk, because the project is stored on the server.
3. **Keep-awake warnings.** Before the render the studio says how long the computer will be busy and asks the creator to keep it on and plugged in. A banner in Bangla or English shows progress and "Please do not shut down the computer or close this tab". The screen is kept awake while the tab is visible. A website cannot stop a shutdown or power cut, so saved chunks and server takeover are the real protection.
4. **Server takeover.** If the studio's connection signal stops for 30 minutes (default, editable), or the browser render fails repeatedly, the server renders the remaining chunks from the last one backwards while a returning browser continues forwards. The first finished copy of each chunk is kept. The server uses the same renderer (Revideo in headless Chromium), so the result looks the same. Takeover chunks run in the low-priority lane and cost the creator the same price.
5. **Checks on browser-made chunks.** They are treated like uploads: safety frame checks, the audio transcribed and compared with the approved script, and one small frame per chunk rendered on the server to confirm it matches the storyboard. A chunk that fails is rendered again on the server.
6. **Server-side failures.** A failed server render resumes from where it stopped, and only failed scenes are re-made. The creator is never charged for a failure; a render that cannot be completed is refunded to the wallet automatically.
7. **Model version.** Each project stays on the model version it started with, so a model update never changes the look of a series half-way.
8. **Settings at confirmation.** Jobs already confirmed finish under the price and settings shown at confirmation; the usage ledger records the settings version.
9. **Capacity protection.** When GPU load passes the admin threshold (default 80%), 1080p Native is paused for new orders and 1080p Escalating is offered until load falls.
10. **GPU site outage.** Paid work moves to rented cloud GPUs in an emergency, and clients see a new ETA. Free Trial and Free Beta work never uses paid cloud capacity; it waits in the queue.

See chapter 10 for the scheduling and GPU pool behind these rules.

## 3.6 Islamic plan and checks in the workflow

The creator chooses General or Islamic content at the start. If a General project is clearly Islamic, the studio explains the difference and offers a switch; it never switches silently. On the Islamic plan the AI checks quotations against a verified text and gives a confidence score. When it is confident, the 25% discount applies automatically; when it is unsure, the project goes to a human reviewer and shows a separate "in review, up to 7 days" ETA (reviews complete within 3–7 days). No GPU is reserved and no charge is taken before approval. Prayer scenes are always human-reviewed, and Arabic text is always an overlay. See chapter 4 for the verification features and chapter 12 for the safety rules.

## 3.7 Delivery tiers and expected times

Paid server renders use three delivery tiers, chosen at order. Quick mode renders in the browser and has no delivery tier. Free Trial and Free Beta renders are free, run in the low-priority lane and have no delivery tier.

| Tier | Price | Promise | If late |
|---|---|---|---|
| Express | Rate + 20% | High priority on any free GPU in the shared cluster; ETA calculated and shown at order | Surcharge refunded automatically if more than 20% later than the ETA shown (ETA 40 minutes: refund after 48 minutes) |
| Normal | Rate | Within 12 hours, shown as "usually much sooner" | Automatic refund to the wallet |
| Slow | Rate − 10% | Within 24 hours, scheduled into quiet hours and the 1–7 am window | Automatic refund to the wallet |

Every order shows an expected completion time from the live queue, by length and mode, and the creator is notified by email or push when the video is ready. Planning estimates for a 1-minute video without a queue:

| Mode | Typical time (estimate) | Main time cost |
|---|---|---|
| Quick | About 3–6 minutes (longer on a weak PC) | Drawing frames in the browser and uploading chunks |
| Enhanced | About 4–8 minutes | Key images on the server, motion in the browser |
| Cinematic | About 12–15 minutes | AI video generation for hero shots |

With a normal queue the ETA for a 1-minute video is typically 15–30 minutes. Longer videos and Islamic reviews show their own ETA. These times are design targets until measured in the closed pilot and Free Beta; delivery-time KPIs are in chapter 15.

## 3.8 Delivery and publishing

When the final file passes validation it is signed with Content Credentials and stored. Creators with storage space keep it in their library; creators without space receive a 72-hour emailed download link, after which the file is deleted (chapter 11). A small 360p WhatsApp-friendly copy is made automatically.

Creators can publish or schedule directly to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram Reels and TikTok, with LinkedIn later, through each platform's official API. The AI-disclosure option is ticked automatically where the platform requires it. Until each platform approves the app, creators download and upload themselves. A pre-publish ad-policy check, analytics and one-click boost are described in chapter 5.
