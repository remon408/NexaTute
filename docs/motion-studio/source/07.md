# 7. Pricing, Billing and Payments

## 7.1 Pricing principle

Every Motion Studio charge is built from three parts:

1. **Package fee** — the monthly subscription that gives access and features (chapter 6).
2. **Pre-production fee** — a small fee for the AI script, storyboard, scene list, key images and 480p preview (7.7).
3. **Per-minute video rate** — set by the package, the render mode (3D Animation or Cinematic) and the output tier, with 25% off for verified Islamic content and an optional delivery-speed adjustment (7.6).

There is no included minute quota, no overage fee and no "quota exhausted" pause. The price is always shown and confirmed before work starts. Usage is metered from the first second of generated video using the approved seconds-to-minutes conversion, on the accepted output duration, not on GPU processing time. The price is the same wherever the video is rendered: in the creator's browser or on our servers after a browser fallback.

Monthly package fees and their USD equivalents are in 6.2.

> Why this model: creators pay only for what they make, small creators can start at ৳499 a month, and heavy GPU work is always paid for by the minute rather than hidden in a flat fee.

## 7.8 Payment at storyboard approval

The video charge is taken at one clear moment: when the creator approves the storyboard (approved 29 October 2026).

1. The storyboard shows the estimated video length and the full price (package, output tier, render mode, delivery tier).
2. The creator presses "approve & pay". The amount is taken from the wallet; if the wallet is short, the creator tops up by bKash on the spot.
3. Only then does rendering start. Nothing is charged for the video before storyboard approval.

After the final render:

- Shorter than estimated → the difference goes back to the wallet automatically.
- Up to 10% longer → no extra charge (10% = default, editable).
- Failed render or missed delivery → automatic wallet refund (Express late rule in 7.6 unchanged). A failed render resumes from where it stopped, with no charge for the failure.
- After delivery, our defects are fixed free. Changes the client asks for are charged only for the minutes of the changed scenes.
- Every charge and refund has a receipt in the wallet history.
- A watermarked preview is available for review only; it is not a payment condition.

Islamic content that is waiting for human review has no GPU reserved and no charge taken until it is approved (review within 3–7 days).

## 7.9 Remakes and changes after delivery

Remakes follow Appendix G.4 of Master Blueprint v32.10. Each level is a percentage of the original production tier's base rate.

| Level | What it covers (proposed definitions) | Charge | Limit per video |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 Basic edit | Text/script, caption, subtitle or scene-order changes with light re-render | 30% | 3 |
| R2 Partial regeneration | Selected scenes, music or voice replaced at the same tier | 50% | 2 |
| R3 Major regeneration | Most scenes regenerated at the same tier | 75% | 1 |
| R4 Full remake | Whole video regenerated at the original tier | 100% | Not set |
| R5 Multilingual remake | New language/voice version of the full video | 91% | Not set |

Rules:

- Low-compute edits (text/script changes, scene or layout reordering without heavy GPU work, paid voice cloning or new 3D assets) are free.
- Free allowances and purchased Motion Studio credits are used first; otherwise the remake rate and estimated total are shown before charging.
- R2 and R3 rates apply only when the remake uses the same production tier as the original.
- R5 = base rate × 0.91; for verified Islamic content the 25% discount is applied to that amount, then rounded to whole taka (.5 up).
- No monthly remake package and no unlimited remakes.
- Purchased Motion Studio credits are valid for 30 days, do not renew, expire unused and are not transferable unless a signed contract says so.
- Mini and above get one free region repair per video (fixing a marked area of a scene instead of regenerating it).
- Storyboard approval comes with a fixed number of free revisions (default, editable; number to confirm).
- The level definitions need commercial sign-off. How the G.4 percentages combine with the 29 October 2026 rule "client changes charged only for the changed scenes' minutes" is to confirm.

## 7.14 Spending cap, renewals and price changes

- **Spending cap.** The creator sets a monthly spending cap (for example ৳5,000). Alerts go out at 50%, 80% and 100%; at the cap every new order needs fresh approval. Because packages have no included minutes, this prevents bill shock. Pre-production fees count toward the cap.
- **Usage display.** The creator always sees the package, the per-minute rate, minutes generated this billing cycle and the estimate for the next job. The same usage is never charged twice.
- **Renewal.** Monthly fees auto-renew only where aamarPay supports tokenised recurring payment for the method. Otherwise the creator gets renewal reminders with a payment link, a 3-day grace period and the option to pay from the wallet.
- **Price changes.** When a paid rate rises or a paid limit falls, subscribers are told in the app and by email before it applies (default 7 days' notice). Price cuts apply at once. Jobs already confirmed finish at the price shown at confirmation, and the ledger records the settings version used.
