# 12. Safety, Rights, Legal and Compliance

This chapter sets out how Remon Motion Studio keeps its output safe and lawful: the content-safety system, the consent rules for voices and faces, Islamic verification, copyright and asset licences, provenance labels, privacy, the terms of service and the payment and tax position. Every legal point here is a position drafted by Claude for a Bangladeshi lawyer to check (decided 29 October 2026); none of it is legal advice. Policy categories, term lists, thresholds and review queues are admin settings, changed with two-person approval and logged (see chapter 13).

> Rule: safety checks run on what the system produces, not only on what the client asks for. A clean prompt does not make a frame, a voice line or a caption safe.

## 12.1 Principles

- Self-hosted, free and open-source safety software only. No client content is sent to a paid outside moderation service.
- Checks on input and output: prompts, scripts, uploads, generated frames, generated audio and captions are all checked (output moderation approved 29 October 2026).
- Narrow definitions, human review for unclear cases. Automatic blocking is for clear violations only.
- Consent before likeness. No real person's voice or face is cloned or animated without that person's live, verified consent.
- Honest labelling. Every output is marked as AI-made, both in its metadata and, where published directly, on the platform.
- No guarantees the system cannot keep. Motion Studio never claims a video is copyright-safe or platform-safe.

## 12.2 Content safety system

The system has four checking layers plus child-safety hash matching. Failed scenes are re-rendered safely or blocked; they never reach the client.

| Layer | What is checked | Software (licence) | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text | Prompts, AI and client scripts, captions, titles, descriptions in Bangla, Banglish and English, including disguised spellings | Qwen3Guard + Bangladesh policy layer (Qwen3 classifier and maintained term list) | Before generation and on final captions |
| Images and video | Key images and sampled frames: nudity, sexual poses or dance, obscene or insulting gestures, gore, hate symbols | Fast NSFW classifier (e.g. Falconsai ViT, Apache-2.0, licence to verify); Qwen3-VL policy check when a frame is flagged or shows people or characters | On key images and every rendered scene |
| Audio | Voice-overs, cloned voices, audio of uploads | faster-whisper transcript, then the text layer | After voice generation and on upload |
| Uploads | Client videos and photos | Image/video and audio layers, plus copyright checks (12.10) | Before any processing |
| Child safety | Known illegal images and video | Meta Hasher-Matcher-Actioner with PDQ (images) and TMK+PDQF (video), BSD, self-hosted; hash lists via NCMEC access | On uploads and outputs |

The Bangladesh policy layer covers religious hatred, communal incitement and anti-state violence. "Anti-state" is defined narrowly: incitement to violence or terrorism, calls for armed overthrow of the state, and content clearly prohibited by law. Ordinary political criticism, news and satire are allowed; unclear cases go to a person, never to automatic blocking. The lawyer finalises these definitions under the Cyber Security Act 2026.

Bad language and obscene gestures are blocked in scripts, audio, captions, images and video:

- The AI never writes bad language. If the client's own text contains it, rendering does not start; the words are highlighted and a clean alternative is suggested. Uploaded audio with bad language is blocked.
- Prompts asking for obscene gestures are blocked. Quick-mode 3D characters use only an approved animation library, so such gestures cannot be produced. Generated frames with people or characters are checked for gestures separately.
- Safety negative prompts are always on in Wan 2.2 and FLUX.

Image and video safety checks add about 5–10% to safety GPU load, which is counted in the capacity model (see chapter 10).

## 12.3 Content policy

The content policy blocks the categories below. The 21 October 2026 additions (35.3) are final owner decisions; the lawyer still reviews the wording of terms such as "anti-state", "deepfake" and the voice rights of deceased persons.

| Category | Rule |
|---|---|
| Adult and sexual content | Blocked in all modes; sexual or insulting content made from any real person's image is prohibited |
| Hate and incitement | Religious hatred, communal incitement and anti-state violence blocked (12.2) |
| Deepfakes and impersonation | A real person shown saying or doing what they did not, presented as news or evidence, is blocked; stricter checks for election candidates in election periods; photo-real public-figure likeness needs proof of permission |
| Fake news | Copying a real news outlet's logo or format is blocked |
| Scam advertising | Fake investments, fake jobs or overseas-job lures and fake lotteries are blocked |
| Restricted promotion | Gambling and betting apps, drugs and weapons are blocked |
| Health misinformation | Miracle cures and similar claims are blocked |
| Political use of cloned voices | Not allowed (12.5) |
| Licensed businesses | Business packs for licensed businesses (for example recruiting agencies and clinics) must show the licence number |

Not offered on purpose (owner decisions 29 October 2026):

- AI "customer testimonial" videos, because they are fake reviews.
- Lyric videos of other people's songs.
- Talking photos made from one picture of a person.

Scripts are fact-checked by the AI and approved by the client before the storyboard is rendered. Wrong facts that remain are the client's responsibility under the terms (12.15), but the system flags claims it cannot support.

## 12.4 Reporting, enforcement and appeals

- Every video has a Report button.
- Enforcement ladder: warning, then temporary suspension, then permanent ban. Each decision is audit-logged with the reason.
- The client can appeal any decision. Appeals and unclear cases go to the human review queue.
- Serious cases skip the ladder: child sexual content closes the account at once (12.6); an unauthorised voice-clone or face-use complaint suspends the account while it is reviewed (12.5, 12.8).
- The AI detects and recommends; a person makes every suspension, ban and takedown decision.

## 12.5 Voice-cloning consent policy

Approved 21 October 2026 (35.2) and unchanged by the Voice Clone Studio design of 29 October 2026.

| Point | Rule |
|---|---|
| Whose voice | The client's own voice, or another adult's voice with that person's direct consent |
| Consent | The voice owner reads a freshly generated consent sentence (date, account name, "I consent") live in the app; pre-recorded audio is not accepted; a third party also confirms by OTP on their own phone |
| Verification | Speaker verification (SpeechBrain ECAPA, licence to verify) matches the consent voice to the cloning sample |
| Where | In the app only, on mobile or computer; no cloning from WhatsApp or Messenger voice notes |
| Not allowed | Under-18 voices; politicians, celebrities or religious figures without written-permission proof; deceased persons without the heirs' permission |
| Use | Only inside the account that made it; not transferable or saleable; never for political speech, money requests, fraud or obscene content |
| Labels | C2PA metadata and an inaudible AudioSeal watermark on every output; "AI voice" label on direct publishing |
| Storage | Voice samples in separate encrypted storage; staff cannot listen to them |
| Withdrawal | Consent can be withdrawn at any time; the voice model is deleted within 72 hours and no new renders are made |
| Records | Consent recording, time, device and verification score kept 3 years after deletion, for disputes |
| Complaints | A claim of cloning without consent leads to takedown within 24 hours and account suspension during review |

Noise and echo are cleaned with DeepFilterNet before cloning, but cleaning never replaces the consent check. Package limits for cloning follow chapter 6.

## 12.6 Child safety and kids-safe mode

> Zero tolerance: any sexual content involving minors is blocked permanently, the account is closed and the case is reported as the law requires.

- Known illegal images and video are matched by PDQ and TMK+PDQF hashes on spare CPU cores. Hash lists come through NCMEC Electronic Service Provider registration and a hash-sharing agreement through the USA LLC (request now); the IWF list is a paid alternative.
- Kids-safe mode (all packages, including Trial and Beta) applies stricter filters for rhymes, moral stories and education cartoons and sets "Made for kids" automatically on YouTube. PlayBoom content for NexaTute can be made in this mode.
- Any project with a child character switches to kids-safe strict filters automatically.

## 12.7 Child-character voices

- Child-character voices in the library come only from consenting, paid adult voice artists or from fully synthetic designed voices (boy or girl, ages 5–8 and 9–12, in six languages; dialects later).
- An adult client's own cloned voice may be made to sound child-like.
- A real child's voice is never cloned or used, even with guardian consent.

## 12.8 Faces, digital twins and real people

The digital-twin presenter (approved 30 October 2026) follows the same rules as voice cloning:

- Own face only, from a short video (about 2 minutes) of the client.
- Live consent with OTP, 18+ only, usable only in the client's account.
- Watermark and AI labels on every output; deleted on consent withdrawal within the voice-clone time limit.
- Any real person's face used in a video needs that person's consent and OTP, like a voice.
- Photo-real likeness of public figures is blocked without proof of permission.

The one-time fee for extra digital twins is in chapter 7 and stays under review until the GPU benchmark.

## 12.9 Islamic content verification

Islamic videos earn a 25% discount on per-minute rates (see chapter 7), so verification protects both the faith content and the billing.

1. The client chooses the Islamic or General plan for each project. If a General-plan prompt is clearly Islamic, the Content Plan Mismatch Guard explains the difference and offers a switch; it never switches silently.
2. The AI checks first: is it Islamic content, are Quran and hadith quotations correct against a verified text, does it meet policy. It records a confidence score.
3. Confident result: the discount applies automatically.
4. Unsure result, or a quotation mismatch: the project goes to the human review queue. Reviewers are assigned in the admin panel; the named reviewers set up before the Free Beta are Qari Md Shahadat Hossain and Md Serajul Islam. More reviewers can be added, and a queue-length alert warns when the queue grows.
5. Review completes within 3–7 days. The client sees a separate "in review, up to 7 days" ETA. No GPU is reserved and nothing is charged before approval.
6. The audit trail keeps the AI score, reviewer, time, decision and reason. The client can appeal.

Production rules for Islamic videos (29 October 2026): Arabic text is always an overlay, never drawn by the video model; prayer scenes are always reviewed by a person; unverified quotations are never labelled as verified.

## 12.10 Copyright for uploads and takedown

Making Shorts, translations or edits from uploaded video creates copyright risk. Motion Studio has no Content ID-scale database, so these controls reduce the risk; they do not remove it.

- Ownership declaration before processing, stored with time, account and file hash.
- Connected-channel import from the client's own YouTube or Facebook channel is the default proof of ownership; direct upload is the secondary path.
- Music replacement: background music can be separated (Demucs, weights licence to verify) and replaced with library or own AI music.
- Audio fingerprint check at launch: Chromaprint (LGPL-2.1, run as a separate process) against the RemonLogic music library and a blocklist of known copyrighted sources; matches are blocked or flagged.
- Pre-publish warning that third-party content may remain and platform claims are still possible.
- Takedown and repeat-infringer policy: a valid complaint removes the video from storage and publishing and notifies the client; repeated infringement suspends and then closes the account.
- DMCA: a designated agent is registered with the US Copyright Office and named on remonlogicstudio.com (approved 30 October 2026; which entity registers it is to confirm). Counter-notices follow the same process.
- The invisible re-upload watermark (12.13) lets clients report unauthorised re-uploads of their own videos, with an evidence report for their complaint.

## 12.14 Privacy and data protection

- Laws in scope: Bangladesh Personal Data Protection Act 2026, Cyber Security Act 2026 and National Data Management Act 2026 (as named in the draft legal opinion); GDPR for EU clients; CCPA for California residents (approved 30 October 2026).
- Servers are in the USA. Transfers rely on data-processing agreements with every provider (hosting, Cloudflare, email, SMS, payment, AI APIs), encryption in transit and at rest, named-staff access with MFA and audit logs, and a 72-hour breach notice. The Privacy Policy names the countries and providers.
- Data rights: full project export and account deletion, including cloned voices, digital twins and characters, within the retention rules (see chapter 11).
- Voice samples and face videos are biometric-type data: separate encrypted storage, no staff listening or viewing, deletion on consent withdrawal.
- Payment cards are entered only on the payment provider's pages; RemonLogic never stores card numbers.
- Cookies: essential only until the visitor consents.
- No compliance or certification is claimed until counsel or an audit confirms it. SOC 2 is planned for later (see chapter 17).

## 12.16 Brands, defamation and platform rules

- Logo detection warns the client when a third-party logo or brand appears in a video.
- Brand-misuse and defamation complaints lead to takedown within 24 hours, then review.
- Pre-publish ad-policy check against Meta and Google advertising rules (all packages).
- Clients are warned, at order and before publishing, that YouTube and Facebook restrict mass-produced or repetitive "inauthentic" content and may limit monetisation; Motion Studio does not claim that remakes or voice changes avoid these rules.
- Direct publishing uses only official platform APIs and follows each platform's terms and reused-content rules.

## 12.17 Payments, wallet and tax

- Wallet: non-cash-out, non-transferable prepaid credit used only for RemonLogic services. The lawyer checks that this stays outside Bangladesh Bank e-money and payment-service rules (to confirm). Wallet top-ups are a company liability until spent (see chapter 14).
- Payment collection: aamarPay (bKash, Nagad, cards, including international cards) now; Paddle for international sales when its API is approved. The aamarPay merchant account is in Vitlous Technology's name, which the lawyer and accountant must reconcile with RemonLogic Private Limited (see chapter 18).
- Foreign card payments follow Bangladesh remittance rules and the blueprint's entity structure (Bangladesh income to RemonLogic Private Limited, international income through the USA LLC).
- VAT: Bangladesh prices are VAT-inclusive (15/115 on aamarPay sales); Mushak VAT invoices are issued to business clients; Paddle revenue is treated as a 0% export of services. The chartered accountant confirms rates and treatment.
- Registrations needed before paid launch: RJSC registration of RemonLogic Private Limited (application 1 November 2026), trade licence, TIN and VAT registration (BIN).
