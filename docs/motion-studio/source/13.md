# 13. Admin Panel

> Owner requirement (29 October 2026): the Motion Studio admin panel must be as rich as NexaTute's, and it includes accounting.

The Motion Studio admin panel follows the patterns already approved for the NexaTute platform admin panel and adapts them to a video product. It controls every user-visible value without code changes, runs the render operation day to day, handles safety, consent and rights, and holds the product's books (chapter 14).

## 13.1 Common rules for every admin page

- No access by default. Each staff member gets only the modules and actions their role allows (13.13).
- Two-person approval: every change to a user-visible value, price, rate, limit, package feature, policy, role, refund above the limit, data deletion or model switch needs a second person to approve it. The maker can never approve their own change.
- Every value shows "(default)" while it is the launch value, and every change records who, when, where, old value, new value and reason in an append-only log.
- Changes apply to new orders; confirmed, queued or rendering jobs finish under the settings and price shown at confirmation, and the job stores the settings version used.
- Price rises and limit cuts are announced in the app and by email before they apply (default 7 days' notice); price cuts apply at once.
- Any package, tier, render mode or feature can be paused with one click during an incident, with a message to users.
- Settings are versioned rows in PostgreSQL, cached in Valkey and pushed to the API pods; on/off switches and gradual rollouts use Unleash feature flags. No new servers are needed; the admin runs in its own namespace on the App cluster.
- Every admin page works on desktop, tablet and phone, in Bangla and English at launch.
- Prototype rule: the Motion Studio admin boards are split by area from the start, each board at most 1.8 MB.

## 13.3 Dashboard

- Creators: free, trial, Free Beta and paid creators by package; new sign-ups and new paid creators today; creators active in the studio right now; by country and creator type (freelancer, business, agency, Islamic creator).
- Production: videos and minutes ordered and delivered today by mode, tier and delivery tier; queue length and ETA per tier; render success rate; first-pass approval rate; remake rate; quality-gate failure rate; browser-to-server takeovers.
- Capacity: GPU busy share per pool and node, Motion Studio floor use, borrowed and lent capacity, degradation level, cloud-burst hours and spend against the cap, Free Beta admission and Cinematic pool use.
- Money: wallet top-ups, spending, refunds, package revenue and pre-production fees today and this month; cost per output minute and margin per mode and tier, with the 30% margin alert (chapter 14).
- Quality and trust: satisfaction rating, top 5 complaints of the week, open safety and Islamic review queues with their age, open takedowns, consent withdrawals pending.
- Counts with fewer than 20 people in a cell are hidden.

## 13.4 Packages & features matrix

The "Packages & features" page (approved 30 October 2026) is the single control point for what each package includes. The customer package comparison page reads from it, so the page and the real entitlements always agree.

| Control | How it works |
|---|---|
| Matrix | Rows are features (every feature in chapters 4 and 5); columns are Free Trial / Free Beta, Mini, Starter, Premium Starter, Professional, Agency and Enterprise |
| Per-cell setting | On or off, plus limits where they apply (for example digital twins 1 / 3 / 10, daily minutes, maximum video length, frame rates, output tiers, upload size) |
| Global feature switch | Turns a feature off everywhere; customers then see "coming soon" on every page that lists it |
| Defaults | Every cell starts at the approved placement and shows "(default)" until changed |
| Approval | Every change needs two-person approval; the maker cannot approve |
| Log and history | Every change is logged with reason; version history with one-click rollback, as in NexaTute |
| Checks before saving | Dependency warnings (for example switching off the review link affects agency workflows) and a live cost and margin check against the 30% margin alert (adapted from NexaTute) |
| Effect on subscribers | Changes apply to new subscribers; current subscribers are told in advance before a feature is removed from their package |
| Comparison page | Top 8 features per package and "see all features" are chosen here; the 3-question helper's questions and rules are edited here |

Safety and rights features (content safety, consent checks, AI labels and Content Credentials, the report button, data download and deletion) cannot be switched off in any package.

## 13.5 Prices and rates

- Package fees and the per-minute rate table for 3D/Animation and Cinematic, every tier and every package (chapter 7). The base rate is editable per cell; Islamic (−25%) and USD (BDT +35% at ৳122 = $1) values recalculate automatically by their rounding rules, and any cell can be overridden with approval.
- EUR and GBP price lists for Paddle, set by the same approval flow.
- Delivery tiers: Express surcharge (+20%), Normal and Slow windows, Slow discount (−10%), late-delivery refund rules.
- Pre-production fee, pay-per-video rates, minute packs, storage add-ons, extra digital twin fee (default ৳500), character and voice add-ons, white-label licence fee, reseller discount and marketplace commission.
- 4K Escalating and 1080p Native controls: on or off for the platform, per package and per render mode; Cinematic 1080p Native stays off until its benchmark passes; the load threshold for the automatic 1080p Native pause (default 80%).
- Frame rates: base 25 fps; 24/25/30/60 per package.
- Free Trial and Free Beta settings: daily minutes, maximum video length, output tiers, Cinematic pool, end date and admission cap.
- Price changes follow propose → two-person approval → effective date → version history; rate changes above the approval table's thresholds need the finance lead and a founder.

## 13.6 Wallet, receipts and refunds

- Creator wallet view: balance, every top-up, charge at storyboard approval, settlement after render, refund and credit, each with its receipt.
- Automatic rules (chapter 7) run without staff action: shorter video → difference back; failure or late delivery → refund; Express late → surcharge back.
- Manual refunds and credits: a reason is required; refunds over ৳5,000 need two approvals (default, editable); wrong or duplicate charges are reversed at once.
- Spending caps: creators' monthly caps and alert levels (50/80/100%) are visible; staff cannot raise a creator's cap.
- Wallet rules: non-cash-out, non-transferable prepaid credit (lawyer check, chapter 12); balances form a liability in the books (chapter 14).

## 13.7 Orders and render queue

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Order list | Every job with creator (masked), package, mode, tier, delivery tier, state, node, ETA, price and settings version |
| Stuck jobs | Jobs with no heartbeat or no progress past a set time are flagged with an alert; the admin can retry from the last checkpoint, move the job to another node or cancel with automatic refund |
| Retry and re-render | Retry a failed scene or the whole job; our defects are re-rendered free |
| Emergency GPU move | Move paid jobs to rented cloud GPUs when the GPU site is down; creators get a new ETA automatically; spend shown against the monthly cap |
| Capacity limits | Festival capacity limit per day and per delivery tier, early-order discount window, and the 1080p Native load pause |
| Pause | Pause a package, tier, mode or Express with a message to users |
| Browser renders | Takeover time (default 30 minutes), weak-device test limit (default 6× video length), takeovers per creator |
| Delivery | Delivery success, 72-hour link use, notification failures, publishing errors per platform |

Job moves, cancellations and manual refunds are logged. Retries and moves to another own node need no second approval (operations actions, logged only, as in NexaTute); an emergency move to cloud GPUs is allowed within the approved monthly cap, and raising the cap needs two-person approval (default, editable); refunds follow 13.6.

## 13.8 Moderation, Islamic review and takedowns

- Safety queue: items flagged by the text, image, audio or video checks, with the model score, the policy category and the frames or lines concerned. Decisions are allow, block, or ask the creator to change; the AI never punishes, a person decides. Enforcement steps (warning → temporary suspension → ban) and appeals are recorded.
- Policy settings: categories, term lists (Bangla, Banglish, English), thresholds and the kids-safe strict filter set; changes need two-person approval.
- Islamic review queue: items where the AI is unsure, plus all prayer scenes. Reviewers are named and assigned from the admin panel; the first named reviewers are Qari Md Shahadat Hossain and Md Serajul Islam. More reviewers can be added at any time. Each item shows the AI confidence score, the quotation check result and the creator's separate "in review, up to 7 days" ETA; the audit trail stores score, reviewer, time, decision and reason; the creator can appeal.
- Queue alert: when a queue's length or oldest item passes a set limit (to confirm), the moderation lead and the founder are alerted so more reviewers can be added.
- Takedowns: copyright, brand, defamation and unauthorised-clone complaints, with the 24-hour target timer, the evidence, the action taken (removal from storage and publishing), the creator notice and the repeat-infringer count. The DMCA designated-agent record and the re-upload watermark reports are kept here.
- Logo detection warnings and ad-policy check results can be reviewed here when a creator disputes them.

## 13.9 Consent registry

- Voice clones, digital twins and real-person face use: who, whose voice or face, consent sentence recording, date, device, speaker-verification score, OTP result for a third party, permitted use and status.
- Withdrawals: the 72-hour deletion timer, deletion confirmation and the record kept for 3 years.
- Complaints: link to the takedown case; account suspension while under review.
- Child-character voices: the adult voice artist's consent and payment record for every library voice.
- Access: DPO and assigned reviewers only; staff cannot play samples or view face data (chapter 11); every opening is logged.

## 13.10 Image, music and template licence library

- Every library image, music track, sound effect, template, font and famous-place photo with source, licence, photographer or author, date, attribution text and where it is used.
- Remove or replace an item: new use stops at once; existing projects are flagged for the creators affected.
- Training-data register: which photos were used to train the "Bangladesh scenes" LoRA and character packs.
- Approval: adding a new source type needs the content lead and, where the rules are unclear, the lawyer.

## 13.11 Model versions and benchmark switch

- Model registry view: every model in use with version, weight hash, licence status, placement and quality-gate result.
- Benchmark results: the quarterly benchmark and the GPU test results (GPU-minutes per output minute, quality scores, viewer-panel ratings) side by side.
- Switch: choose the active model per task (for example the video model for non-hero scenes); applies to new projects only; open projects stay pinned to their versions; two-person approval; one-click rollback.
- Quality thresholds: VMAF, sharpness and flicker gate values, minimum bitrates and the defect-check sensitivity, set from the benchmark.
- Optimisation switches: LightX2V, FastWan, Lightning LoRA and Wan2.2-S2V are switched on only after their licence check and quality gate pass.

## 13.12 Support inbox

- One Chatwoot inbox for chat, email, WhatsApp and Messenger with Motion Studio's own channels and agents; SLA timers per package; AI hand-over reason; the creator's order and wallet view beside the ticket (read-only, logged).
- Help-centre articles and AI support answers are edited and approved here; the AI answers only from approved articles.
- Weekly complaint ranking (top 5) feeds the satisfaction loop (chapter 9).

## 13.13 Roles, access and the audit log

| Role group | Roles (default, editable) |
|---|---|
| Platform | Super admin (founders), security owner, operations / SRE owner, product owner, moderation lead, Islamic reviewer, DPO, support agent, content lead, marketing (aggregates only), analyst |
| Accounting | Finance lead, accounts officer, payroll officer, founders and board, department manager (team totals only), external auditor or chartered accountant (read-only and export, time-limited), investor (investor report only) |

- Custom roles are made by ticking view, edit, approve and export per module and sub-module, with scope limits and masking of sensitive fields (creator contact details, bank and bKash numbers except the last 4 digits, salaries).
- Role changes are proposed by the super admin and approved by the security owner (Mst Sonia Sabuz), never by the requester; suspend and reactivate need the same approval; access is removed the day an employee leaves; accounts unused for 90 days are suspended; quarterly access review.
- Sign-in: 2-step login for everyone; passkeys or hardware security keys required for the super admin, money approvers and the security owner; a fresh OTP before approving money, changing roles or exporting; break-glass access is time-limited, needs a reason and is reviewed afterwards.
- Audit log: who (name and role), when, where (device, browser, IP address, approximate city) and what (before → after) for every action, including views of sensitive data and every export; it cannot be edited or deleted.

## 13.14 Nexa Co-Admin

Nexa Co-Admin is the AI assistant inside the admin panel, the same service approved for NexaTute, serving Motion Studio staff. It is always labelled: "Nexa Co-Admin is an AI. It acts only within your own permissions."

- Permissions: every request is checked against the asking admin's role, scope and masking; requests outside them are refused with a "Request access" option.
- Actions: it asks only for missing details, shows a preview of the exact change (for example a refund entry) and acts only after Confirm. Money and other two-person actions are submitted as "Waiting for second approval"; Co-Admin can never be the second approver.
- Levels per role (default, editable): super admin, finance and operations can act with confirmation (operations: non-destructive only); support, content and marketing get drafts only. "Always ask before acting" is locked on for money, people, legal and data deletion.
- Never allowed: delete or change logs; show secrets, keys or passwords; change roles or permissions; approve as the second approver; export personal data without approval; act outside the asking admin's permissions.
- Model: the own-server model first, so admin data stays on RemonLogic servers; an outside API only for non-personal questions (off by default); 50 actions a day per admin (default, editable).
- Chat apps: WhatsApp, Messenger and imo each have their own on/off switch in the admin panel. All three stay off until the lawyer and DPO check; imo stays locked off until imo business API access is confirmed. Staff link their own number with a passkey; personal data is masked; actions are confirmed through a secure link with passkey or biometrics; money still needs the second approval; role changes, personal-data export, data deletion and API keys are never done through a chat app; 15-minute session timeout and 20 actions a day per person (defaults, editable). The staff account name for Motion Studio is (to confirm).
- Activity log: every request and action (time, admin, role, request, data read, action, approval status, model, result) in an append-only log kept 7 years; export needs two people.

## 13.15 Notifications and staff chat

- Bell on every admin page with the unread count. Levels: info (in app or daily digest), important (push), urgent (push, email and SMS, even in quiet hours).
- Motion Studio events (default, editable): approvals waiting (prices, rates, packages, refunds, roles, model switches, policy changes, data deletion); stuck jobs; queue ETA above target; GPU load over 60%; cloud-burst spend at 80%; review queue too long; takedown timer near 24 hours; consent withdrawal due; failed backup; security alerts; margin below 30%; budget at 80% and 100%; a bill due.
- Control panel: recipients by role or named user, channel and level per event; approvals go to the approver from the approval rules, with a reminder after 4 hours and escalation to the next approver after 24 hours; nobody can mute their own approvals or security alerts; push text never shows secrets, amounts or personal data.
- Staff chat: the same internal chat as NexaTute (one-to-one chat, approved groups, calls per role, never recorded); every message is checked on send for passwords, keys and bank details (blocked) and for confidential or personal data (sent, with an alert to the concerned admin); full messages kept 90 days and encrypted daily summaries 6 months (lawyer to confirm).
- Systems: the in-house notification service with web push and the Android admin app, Centrifugo, PostgreSQL and LiveKit; no new servers.
