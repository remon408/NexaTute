# 16. Risk Register

This register lists the risks that apply to Remon Motion Studio. It joins the risk fixes the owner approved on 29–30 October 2026, the twelve launch weak points of the Motion Studio review (4.14) and the rows of the ecosystem risk register (Section 23) that touch Motion Studio.

How to read it:

- Impact and Likelihood use Low, Medium, High and Critical. Rows taken from Section 23 keep its ratings; ratings for the other rows are Claude's initial assessment (default, editable).
- Owner area names the team that owns the control, not a named person. Named owners are set in the admin panel when the team is hired.
- The register is reviewed monthly and after every incident; new rows and rating changes are logged.
