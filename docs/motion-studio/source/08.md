# 8. Free Trial, Closed Pilot and Free Beta

## 8.1 Launch sequence

Motion Studio reaches paying customers in steps. Each step tests the product with real users before money is taken, and the paid launch happens only when the Free Beta pass conditions are met.

| Stage | When | Who | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closed pilot | 2 weeks before the public Free Beta (dates to confirm) | 10–15 businesses | Test the full workflow, quality and payments with known users |
| Free Beta | 30 days from 20 December 2026 | Public, within an admission cap | Test with real users at scale; run the benchmarks |
| Paid launch | Date to be set | All users | Starts only after the pass conditions in 8.5 are met |
| Free Trial | After the Free Beta ends | New users | Permanent 7-day trial for new creators |

Launch dates may move if quality is not ready; the owner's rule is quality first. During the Free Beta the live website shows the approved paid prices with the note "free during the Free Beta".

## 8.2 Free Trial Package

The Free Trial is the permanent way for a new creator to try Motion Studio. During the Free Beta, the Free Beta allowances replace it; after the Free Beta ends, new users receive the normal Free Trial.

| Setting | Free Trial |
|---|---|
| Validity | 7 days |
| Cost | Free; no payment card required |
| Daily allowance | 1 minute of video per day (7 minutes in total); unused time does not carry over |
| Maximum video length | 15 seconds per video |
| Output tiers | 480p Native and 720p Escalating only |
| Render modes | Quick and Enhanced only; no Cinematic |
| Features | Freelancer Starter features, plus the features placed in "Trial/Beta" in 6.4 |
| Output | Watermarked; personal use only; no recording |

| Daily reset | 00:00 Bangladesh time (UTC+6) |

The daily allowance, maximum video length, output tiers and validity are admin-panel settings and can be changed without a code change.

## 8.3 Closed pilot

Before the public Free Beta, Motion Studio runs a 2-week closed pilot with 10–15 businesses (approved 29 October 2026). The pilot is small on purpose: problems are found and fixed with users who know they are testing, before thousands of people arrive.

What the pilot covers:

- The whole creation path: idea or script, storyboard, approval, render, delivery and changes after delivery.
- The payment path with test payments: wallet top-up, "approve & pay" at the storyboard, refunds to the wallet and receipts.
- Video and voice quality, including Bangla voices and the Voice Clone Studio.
- Support: the AI-first support flow and hand-over to a human.

Suggested mix of pilot businesses (default, editable): businesses from the business-pack sectors (real estate, restaurants, tours, coaching centres, clinics) and at least one agency. Whether pilot businesses pay for their videos or use test payments only is to confirm.

The satisfaction loop starts in the pilot and continues through the Free Beta: a one-tap rating with "what went wrong", the top 5 complaints fixed every week, and a "you said, we fixed" note in What's new.

## 8.4 Free Beta

The Free Beta is a free 30-day testing programme for Motion Studio, starting 20 December 2026. Its purpose is to test the platform with real users while protecting service quality: admission is capped, daily use is limited, and free work never uses paid cloud capacity. The name "Free Beta" is used on every screen and in all marketing.

| Setting | Free Beta policy |
|---|---|
| Duration | 30 days from 20 December 2026; end date editable; paid packages from a date to be set |
| Cost | Free; no payment card required |
| Quick and Enhanced | 1 minute of video per day |
| Cinematic | 1 minute of video per day, within the daily Cinematic pool |
| Output tiers | 480p Native and 720p Escalating only, in all modes; no 4K, 720p Native, 1080p Escalating or 1080p Native |
| Video length | Maximum 15 seconds per video (up to 4 videos a day in Quick/Enhanced and 4 in Cinematic) |
| Output | Watermarked; personal use only |
| Pre-production | Free within 3 scripts a day |
| Admission cap | About 2,000 combined concurrent users (CCU); new users wait in a queue; paid admission is unlimited |
| Cloud fallback | Off for Free Beta users |
| Priority | P10 free/trial lane; no delivery tiers |
| Admin control | Daily minutes, video length, output tiers, end date and admission cap are separate admin settings, changeable during the beta without a code change |

### 8.4.1 Admission cap

When about 2,000 combined concurrent users are online, new users wait in a queue that shows their place in line (for example "You are #123 in line"), using the platform's own queue or Cloudflare Waiting Room. Users already inside keep full service. The cap is raised only when load tests and the live dashboard show headroom, and it is always kept at or below 70% of measured capacity.

### 8.4.2 Daily Cinematic pool

Cinematic is the most GPU-heavy mode, so all Free Beta Cinematic minutes together are limited by a daily pool. The pool starts at 10–15% of the measured Cinematic render capacity and is raised only after the benchmark. 480p Native and 720p Escalating minutes count against the pool at their measured GPU time, including the upscaling pass. When the pool is used up, new Cinematic requests are queued for the next day's pool and the user is told the expected time; Quick and Enhanced stay available.

### 8.4.3 No cloud fallback

When the local GPU cluster is full, Free Beta requests wait in the queue or see the busy message. They are never sent to paid cloud capacity, so the cloud budget is kept for paying users.

### 8.4.4 Help during the beta

The Voice Clone Studio has a weekly live webinar during the Free Beta, alongside the Bangla help pages and support chat. Support is AI-first, then human (chapter 9).

## 8.5 Pass conditions for the paid launch

Paid packages start only when the Free Beta meets the pass conditions approved on 29 October 2026. If they are not met, the launch date moves.

| Condition | Target (default, editable) |
|---|---|
| Crash-free sessions | ≥ 99% |
| AI response speed | Within target |
| User satisfaction | ≥ 4 out of 5 |
| Test payments | All successful |
| Security | No open high-severity security issue |

Motion Studio also has product-specific gates from Master Blueprint v32.10 that decide which parts switch on at the paid launch:

- Video optimizations (Lightning LoRA, LightX2V, FastWan) must be live and passed; otherwise Cinematic 720p Native and higher stay "coming soon" while Cinematic 480p Native and 720p Escalating stay on.
- Cinematic 1080p Native stays off until its GPU benchmark and quality gate pass.
- Features not ready are held as "coming soon" through the admin "Packages & features" page and switched on when they pass their tests.

## 8.6 Controls against misuse of free use

- One free trial per user account, with duplicate-claim controls.
- One free trial per phone number, plus a device check (approved 29 October 2026).
- Free Trial and Free Beta outputs are watermarked and for personal use only.
- Free work never uses paid cloud GPU or cloud LLM capacity.
- Daily limits reset at 00:00 Bangladesh time and do not carry over.

## 8.7 Benchmarks to run during the beta

The Free Beta is also the measuring period for cost and quality. Results go to the admin panel, and any rate change follows the normal two-person approval.

| Benchmark | What it decides |
|---|---|
| GPU cost per output minute, by render mode and tier (Wan 2.2 GPU-minutes per output minute, including Cinematic 1080p Native) | Confirms the Cinematic +35% rates and the ৳120 / ৳162 1080p Native rates; feeds the margin alert |
| Escalating-tier upscaler (and 4K Escalating) | Quality-gate thresholds and minimum bitrates |
| Digital-twin presenter cost | Recheck of the ৳500 extra-twin fee |
| Multi-language pack (dubbing + lip-sync per language) | Price of the multi-language pack (to confirm) |
| Cinematic lip-sync (Wan2.2-S2V-14B) on Bangla and Urdu dialogue | Whether realistic Cinematic dialogue can be offered |
| Render capacity and load | Size of the daily Cinematic pool and the admission cap |
| Express ETA accuracy | The +20% surcharge and the late-refund rate |
| Bangla voices (native-speaker test) | Whether the main TTS voice or the in-house VITS fallback is used |

The GPU work starts with a 1-week test on one rented 8× RTX A6000 server; the final server choice follows the benchmark (chapter 10). For planning, Master Blueprint v32.10 estimated about 11 GPU-minutes per output minute for Cinematic 720p Native, about ৳16 at 70% GPU use, with the optimizations counted at only half their expected gain; this is an estimate that the beta must confirm.
