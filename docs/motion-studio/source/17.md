# 17. Roadmap and Release Gates

Motion Studio is built quality first. Dates are targets, not proof that a feature has shipped, and the owner has decided that launch dates move if quality is not ready (29 October 2026). New good ideas are not paused; they are added to the plan and switched on when they pass their gates.

> Launch rule: every approved feature is in the launch scope, but only features that pass their tests are visible. Anything not ready is shown as "coming soon" and switched on from the admin "Packages & features" page when it passes (see chapter 13).

Interface languages: Bangla and English at launch, Hindi and Urdu in Year 2, Arabic and French in Year 3. Scripts, voices and subtitles work in all six languages from launch, and new dubbing languages (Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, German, Turkish and others) open one by one after their quality gate.

Features that need an outside approval switch on when it arrives: direct publishing (YouTube, TikTok, Meta), one-click boost (Meta), and LinkedIn publishing after launch.

## 17.3 Paid launch conditions

The paid start date is set only after the Free Beta pass conditions are met (defaults, editable):

- At least 99% crash-free sessions.
- AI response and render speed within target.
- Satisfaction at least 4 out of 5.
- All test payments successful, including refunds to the wallet.
- No open high-severity security issue.

Cinematic tiers: if the video optimisations (Lightning LoRA, LightX2V, FastWan) have not passed before paid packages start, Cinematic 720p Native and higher stay "coming soon"; Cinematic 480p Native and 720p Escalating stay on. Cinematic 1080p Native and 4K Escalating are switched on only after their GPU cost is benchmarked.

## 17.4 Release gates

No release is announced as complete until its gate evidence is recorded in the admin panel.

| Gate | Check | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| G1 Architecture | Node placement, queues, storage paths and isolation from NexaTute | Signed-off deployment inventory |
| G2 Technical proof | Each model, voice, upscaler and encoder benchmarked; GPU-minutes per output minute by mode and tier | Dated benchmark reports with version, hardware, errors and cost |
| G3 Functional | Full journeys: sign-up, script, storyboard, approve and pay, render, delivery, refund, export, deletion | Passing functional and regression tests |
| G4 Quality | Quality gates of chapter 15 with thresholds set; native-speaker voice test; viewer panel | Gate thresholds stored; panel results |
| G5 Safety and rights | Content safety layers, child-safety hashing, consent flows, Islamic review, copyright controls, C2PA signing | Test records; lawyer sign-off on Free Beta items |
| G6 Security and licences | Semgrep, OWASP ZAP, SBOM and licence scan, no unknown licence, penetration test before paid launch | Clean scan reports; high findings fixed and retested |
| G7 Load and recovery | Staging load test alone and with NexaTute; render resume; browser fallback; cloud GPU failover; restore drill | Load report with concurrency, p95/p99, errors, GPU use and cost |
| G8 Accessibility | WCAG 2.2 AA checks on the studio | axe-core results |
| G9 Operations | Named owners, on-call rota, status page, support inbox, review queues staffed | Rota and queue owners recorded |
| G10 Rollout | Staged rollout with monitoring, rollback criteria and a one-switch rollback per optimisation | Rollback tested |

Gates G1–G9 apply before the Free Beta; G5 and G6 are repeated in full before the paid launch, and every later release repeats G3, G4, G6 and G10.
