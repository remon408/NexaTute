# 1. Executive Summary and Product Mandate

## 1.1 What Remon Motion Studio is

Remon Motion Studio is an AI video production service from RemonLogic Private Limited, Dhaka. A creator or business describes an idea, uploads a script or sends a voice note, and the studio turns it into a finished video: script, storyboard, voices, music, scenes, captions, checks, delivery and direct publishing. It runs at remonlogicstudio.com as a separate product with its own customers, prices, admin panel and accounting books, on infrastructure shared with the other RemonLogic products.

The studio makes three kinds of video under one subscription: 3D animation and motion graphics, cinematic AI video, and Islamic content with source verification. One monthly package fee gives access to the tools; every finished minute is billed at a per-minute rate that depends on render mode, output tier and package. There is no included minute quota.

RemonLogic Private Limited is the owning company. Its registration with RJSC is being applied for (application planned for 1 November 2026). International sales are planned through a USA LLC that licenses the product from RemonLogic. See chapter 7 for payment and entity details.

## 1.2 Who it serves

Motion Studio is built for Bangladesh first and to an international standard. The first customers are Bangladeshi small businesses, freelancers, content creators, agencies, Islamic content makers and educators. The same product serves international customers in USD, EUR and GBP, with English support around the clock and more dubbing languages after each passes its quality gate.

## 1.3 Why it wins

Motion Studio does not try to be the most realistic AI video tool in the world. It competes on fit for its market, honest pricing and safety.

- **Bangla first.** Bangla script, voices, captions with correct conjuncts, a Bangla pronunciation dictionary, and regional-dialect voices (Chittagonian, Sylheti, Noakhali, Barishal, Rangpur). Interface in Bangla and English at launch.
- **Islamic verification.** Quran and hadith quotations are checked against verified text, uncertain cases go to named human reviewers, and verified Islamic content gets 25% off every rate.
- **Price.** Package fees from ৳499 a month and per-minute billing, with bKash and Nagad payment, pay-per-video and minute packs for occasional users.
- **Business tools.** Order by WhatsApp or Messenger voice note, product-catalogue import, end-screen order QR, per-video sales tracking, business packs for real estate, restaurants, tours, coaching centres and clinics, and one-click boost from the client's own ad account.
- **Safety and consent.** Voice cloning and digital twins only with live consent and OTP, no real children's voices, Content Credentials (C2PA) and AI labels on every output, and content rules written for Bangladesh.

## 1.5 Launch path

The launch follows three steps, and each step must pass before the next starts.

1. **Closed pilot (2 weeks).** 10–15 known businesses use the studio on real work. The aim is to find quality and workflow problems before the public sees them.
2. **Public Free Beta (30 days from 20 December 2026).** Free use within daily limits, an admission cap and a queue. The live website shows the approved paid prices, marked "free during the Free Beta".
3. **Paid launch (date to be set).** Paid packages, delivery tiers and payments start only when the Free Beta pass conditions are met: at least 99% crash-free sessions, AI response speed within target, satisfaction at least 4 out of 5, all test payments successful and no open high-severity security issue (defaults, editable). If the conditions are not met, the date moves.

GPU capacity starts with a one-week test on one rented server with 8× RTX A6000 GPUs. The master blueprint's server list is the baseline architecture; the final server choice follows the benchmark. See chapter 10.

## 1.7 Guiding principles

These principles decide trade-offs throughout this blueprint.

1. **Customer satisfaction first.** Every video ends with a one-tap rating and a "what went wrong" option. The top five complaints are fixed each week and reported in "What's new" as "you said, we fixed". Our own defects are fixed free.
2. **Quality before dates.** Launch dates move if quality is not ready. Features that have not passed their tests are shown as "coming soon" and switched on from the admin panel later.
3. **Browser first.** Work that a normal device can do runs in the creator's browser, which keeps prices low and servers free for heavy work. Weak devices fall back to the server at the same price. See chapter 3.
4. **Free and open-source software.** Self-hosted software and model weights must be free and allowed for commercial use. Paid services are listed as deliberate exceptions. See chapter 10.
5. **Safety and consent.** No cloning, likeness or child voice without the rules in chapter 12. Every output carries Content Credentials and an AI label where platforms ask for one.
6. **Honesty.** No fake reviews, testimonials, partners, view counts or earnings promises. Samples are labelled. No scarcity wording.
7. **Admin control.** Every user-visible value is an admin setting marked "(default)", changed with two-person approval and logged. See chapter 13.

> Motion Studio's position is Bangla, Islamic verification, price, business tools and safety. It does not claim to make the most realistic video on the market.
