# 2. Market, Customers and Competitive Position

## 2.1 Market view

Video is now the main way Bangladeshi businesses and creators reach customers on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and WhatsApp. Most small sellers cannot afford a video editor for every post, and most international AI video tools are priced in dollars, work best in English, and do not understand Bangla, local dialects or Islamic content rules. Motion Studio is designed for that gap.

This blueprint does not state a market size. No measured figure exists yet for the number of paying video customers in Bangladesh, and the blueprint does not invent one. The closed pilot and the Free Beta (chapter 8) are the first sources of real demand data: sign-ups by segment, first-video completion, trial-to-paid conversion and willingness to pay.

Demand is seasonal. Peaks are expected around Ramadan and Eid, Pohela Boishakh, 21 February, 16 December, the wedding season, Black Friday and the year-end recap. Festival rush is handled with an early-order discount and a capacity limit (see chapter 7 and chapter 15).

## 2.4 Pricing position

### 2.4.1 International

International prices are the Bangladesh price plus 35%, converted at ৳122 = $1; EUR and GBP prices are also offered through the international checkout. Examples from the approved tables:

| Item | BDT | USD |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancer Mini (monthly) | ৳499 | $6 |
| Premium Agency (monthly) | ৳7,999 | $89 |
| 3D 480p Native, per minute (Mini–Premium Starter) | ৳30 | $0.33 |
| 3D 1080p Native, per minute | ৳120 | $1.33 |
| Cinematic 1080p Native, per minute | ৳162 | $1.79 |
| 4K Escalating, per minute (3D / Cinematic) | ৳142 / ৳192 | $1.57 / $2.12 |

Our position is that these USD prices are low compared with leading international AI video tools such as Runway, Google Veo and HeyGen, which are sold in USD through subscriptions and credit systems. This is a positioning statement, not a published claim: competitor prices, plans and features change often and must be checked from dated sources before any external comparison (master blueprint 16.3). No competitor figure is quoted in this blueprint for that reason.

### 2.4.2 Bangladesh

For Bangladeshi businesses the price is reasonable: a 30-second product ad at 720p Native on Freelancer Starter costs ৳36 for the video plus the ৳20 pre-production fee, on top of the ৳999 monthly fee shared across all of the month's videos. A business that posts several ads a week spreads the fee thinly. For casual users the picture is different. A monthly fee of ৳499 or more before any minute is billed can feel high to someone who makes one or two videos a month.

The response is not to cut package prices but to offer other ways in:

- **Pay-per-video without a subscription,** paid by bKash, priced a little above package rates.
- **Minute packs** of 30 and 100 minutes at 10–15% off.
- **Free Trial** (7 days) and the 30-day **Free Beta.**
- **Voice-over-only audio** (radio ads, shop announcements, IVR) priced well below video.

See chapter 7 for the full price rules.

## 2.5 Competitive position

| Dimension | International AI video tools | Local editors and free phone apps | Motion Studio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Language | English-first; Bangla weak or missing | Bangla depends on the editor | Bangla first, five regional dialects, six core languages |
| Religious content | No source checks | Depends on the person | Quran and hadith checks, human reviewers, 25% off |
| Price and payment | USD, card only | Per job; free apps need skill and time | BDT with bKash and Nagad; USD, EUR, GBP abroad |
| Business tools | General | Manual | Voice-note ordering, catalogue import, order QR, sales tracking |
| Realism | Often higher for cinematic shots | Real footage | Good, not the market's most realistic; cheapest suitable path per scene |
| Safety and labels | Varies | None | Consent with OTP, C2PA, AI labels, Bangladesh content rules |

Motion Studio wins where language, local business needs, religious accuracy and price matter more than maximum realism. It is weaker where a customer needs the most realistic cinematic footage available; there the gap is managed through hero-shot-only cinematic generation, quality checks and a quarterly benchmark of new open models (chapter 10). The positioning is Bangla, Islamic verification, price, templates and product ads, not "most realistic video".

Two risks affect the position: platform rules on AI-generated and "inauthentic" content, which clients are warned about before publishing; and platform API approvals for direct publishing (chapter 12 and chapter 16).

## 2.6 What Motion Studio deliberately does not offer

Some features are popular elsewhere but are left out on purpose, because they mislead viewers, break other people's rights or create safety risks.

| Not offered | Reason | What we offer instead |
|---|---|---|
| AI "customer testimonial" videos | They are fake reviews and mislead buyers | The business's own recorded customer clips, uploaded with the customer's permission |
| Lyric videos of other people's songs | Copyright in the song and lyrics | Own AI music and licensed, Content-ID-free music |
| Talking photo from a single picture | Easy to abuse for impersonation; no reliable consent route | Own digital-twin presenter from a short video of the creator, with live consent, OTP and 18+ check |
| Real children's voices, even with guardian consent | Child safety | Child-character voices from consenting paid adult voice artists or fully synthetic voices |
| Cloning a voice or face without the owner's live consent | Impersonation and fraud | Voice Clone Studio with live consent sentence and OTP |
| "Copyright-safe" or "most realistic" claims | Cannot be guaranteed | Copyright checks and warnings; honest samples |

The full content and rights rules are in chapter 12.
