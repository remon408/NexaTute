# 9. Client Experience, Onboarding and Support

This chapter describes how a creator meets Motion Studio for the first time, learns it, gets help and tells us what went wrong. Feature detail sits in chapters 4 and 5, package placement in chapter 6, prices in chapter 7 and the Free Trial, closed pilot and Free Beta in chapter 8.

> Design rule: a first-time creator in Bangladesh, on a mid-range Android phone and mobile data, must be able to make, approve and receive a first video without outside help, in Bangla.

## 9.1 First visit and sign-up

The landing page on remonlogicstudio.com shows a short showreel made with Motion Studio, a "write an idea" box that makes a storyboard and thumbnail in the visitor's browser, live package prices read from the admin price table, and the main button "Start Free Trial". During the Free Beta the page shows the approved paid prices with the note "free during the Free Beta". The page carries no advertising, no fake view or earnings claims and no scarcity wording.

Sign-up asks only what is needed: name, phone or email, OTP and interface language. One free trial is allowed per phone number, with a device check (chapter 8). The Device Check (chapter 10) runs in the background in a few seconds and sets the device tier, so a weak phone is never asked to do work it cannot finish.

## 9.2 Guided first video

The first session is a guided tour that ends with a finished video, not a tour of menus.

1. Choose what to make: a product ad, an Islamic reminder, an explainer, a greeting or "my own idea". Each choice opens a ready template.
2. Type the idea in Bangla, Banglish or English, or paste or upload a script (a sample script format can be downloaded in Bangla and English).
3. The AI writes the script and storyboard. The creator sees each scene with its key image, voice and length, and can edit any line.
4. Pick a voice and, if wanted, an emotion for each line (9.4).
5. See the storyboard with the estimated length and full price. On a paid package, "approve & pay" takes the amount from the wallet; nothing is charged before this point (chapter 7). On the Free Trial or Free Beta the step shows "free" and the daily allowance left.
6. Watch progress with the expected delivery time; the creator may close the tab (Quick and Enhanced chunks are saved; the server takes over after 30 minutes, chapter 10).
7. Receive the video by in-app notice, push and email, plus the small 360p WhatsApp-friendly copy, and publish or download.

Each step has one short Bangla tooltip and a "watch how" link to a tutorial clip of under one minute. The tour can be skipped and restarted from the help menu. Onboarding variants are tested with Unleash feature flags; the measure is the share of new creators who finish a first video in their first session (KPI in chapter 15).

## 9.4 Voice picker and emotion — user flow

The product must give creators a clear, easy way to choose and use voices and emotions (owner requirement, 29 October 2026). The feature detail is in chapter 4; this is the flow the creator sees.

- Voice picker: filter by language, dialect (Starter and above), gender, age group (child-character voices are marked, Mini and above) and style; every voice has a play button that reads the creator's own first line, not a fixed demo sentence.
- Simple emotion mode (every package, including Trial and Beta): the AI suggests one emotion per line from the script; the creator sees it as a coloured tag and can change it with one tap.
- Advanced emotion mode (Premium Starter and above): eight emotions (neutral, happy, sad, tearful, angry, afraid, whisper, excited) with an intensity slider per line, a preview of the line in each emotion, and the facial expression of the character matched to the line.
- "Act it yourself" (Starter and above): the creator records the line with feeling and it is converted to the character's voice. The screen states clearly that this is performance transfer, not cloning.
- Voice Clone Studio (in the app only, about 10–12 minutes): mic test with a green/yellow/red noise meter → live consent sentence and OTP → about 3 minutes of natural reading → emotion cards with example, acting tips and an instant "good" or "try again" check with the reason → at least neutral, happy and sad to create the clone, the rest later with a progress bar → preview each emotion, re-record only the weak one → "use this voice". Bangla text and voice guidance at every step and a "5 tips for a good clone" help page. Consent rules are in chapter 12.

> Emotion controls are free: they are included in the package and the video is billed only by its final minutes (owner decision, 29 October 2026).

## 9.5 Package comparison page and helper

The customer comparison page reads directly from the admin "Packages & features" matrix (chapter 13), so the page and the real entitlements can never disagree.

- Each package card shows its top 8 features first, with "see all features" to expand the full list; features switched off globally appear as "coming soon".
- A 3-question "which package is right for me?" helper recommends one package and shows why. Starting questions (default, editable): what do you mainly make (personal or social videos, business ads, client work for others, series or courses); about how many minutes of video a month; do you need team, review-link, API or white-label features.
- The helper never recommends by price pressure and always shows the next cheaper package that would also fit.
- Prices are shown in Taka, with USD, EUR and GBP for international visitors (chapter 7).

## 9.7 Support model

Support uses the shared Chatwoot inbox (one inbox for in-app chat, email, WhatsApp and Messenger), with Motion Studio's own channels and agents. The AI assistant answers first from the help centre and the creator's own order and payment records (checked against the ledger), and hands over to a person when it cannot resolve the question or the creator asks for one.

| Package | AI support | Human support | Target first human response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free Trial, Free Beta | 24 hours a day | Not included, except the cases in the rule below | — |
| Freelancer Mini | 24 hours a day | Not included, except the cases in the rule below | — |
| Freelancer Starter | 24 hours a day | Email and chat | 24 hours (default, editable) |
| Premium Starter, Premium Professional | 24 hours a day | Email, chat and phone | 4 hours (default, editable) |
| Premium Agency | 24 hours a day | Email, chat and phone, with SLA | 4 hours under SLA (default, editable) |
| Enterprise | 24 hours a day | Dedicated contact, with SLA | 1 hour under contract SLA (default, editable) |

- Rule for every package (default, editable): payment errors, wrong or double charges, consent withdrawal, impersonation or unauthorised-clone complaints, copyright or brand takedowns, account-security problems and child-safety reports always reach a person, whatever the package, because these carry legal or money obligations (24-hour takedown, chapter 12).
- Languages: AI support in Bangla and English 24 hours a day from launch. The 30 October 2026 decision asks for 24-hour English support with a human after the AI; human cover starts with the ecosystem hours (chat and WhatsApp 9 am–9 pm, phone 9 am–6 pm, Bangladesh time) and how the night-time English human shift is staffed is (to confirm).
- Contact details: support@remonlogicstudio.com is planned for the platform approvals; the public support phone and email are decided on 31 October 2026 (to confirm).
- Escalation follows the ecosystem matrix (agent → senior support → engineering → CTO/CEO); security, payment and outage incidents follow the severity path, not the normal window.
- SLA terms for Agency and Enterprise (response, resolution and credit if missed) are written into their contracts; the numbers above are starting values.

## 9.8 Weekly webinar and satisfaction loop

During the Free Beta a weekly live webinar in Bangla shows a real project from idea to delivery, covers Voice Clone Studio and answers questions; recordings go into the help centre.

Satisfaction loop (approved 29 October 2026, both products):

1. One-tap rating after every delivered video and every closed support ticket.
2. A low rating opens a short "what went wrong" choice list (voice, picture, lip-sync, text, delivery time, price, other) with an optional comment and screenshot.
3. Every week the top 5 complaints are ranked by count and impact, assigned an owner and fixed or answered.
4. Each release's What's new sheet includes a "you said, we fixed" list.
5. Satisfaction of at least 4 out of 5 is one of the Free Beta pass conditions for the paid launch (chapter 8).

## 9.10 Weak networks and low-end devices

Many creators work on mobile data. The product is built to finish the job on a weak connection.

| Problem | What Motion Studio does |
|---|---|
| Large uploads drop | Uploads go straight from the browser to storage in resumable parts and continue from the last finished part (chapter 11) |
| Tab closed or connection lost during a browser render | Finished chunks wait in the browser and upload when the connection returns; the server takes over after 30 minutes (chapter 10) |
| Weak phone | Device Check sends heavy work to the server at the same price; ETA may be longer at peak |
| Expensive mobile data | Data Saver is on by default on mobile data: browser models download only on Wi-Fi or with consent; previews use lower quality and AV1 copies where the device plays them |
| Sharing on WhatsApp | Every video gets a small 360p WhatsApp-friendly copy (every package) |
| Delivery without the app | Ready-video notice by in-app message, push and email; download links that work in any browser; personalised and bulk videos sent by WhatsApp or SMS link; no-storage delivery by a 72-hour link (chapter 11) |
| Ordering from WhatsApp | WhatsApp or Messenger voice note plus photos, with preview and payment link in the chat (Mini and above, chapter 5) |
