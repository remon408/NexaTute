# 5. Feature Catalogue — Business, Collaboration, Accessibility and International

This chapter describes the features that help businesses, agencies and creators sell, grow, work together and reach every audience. Chapter 4 covers creation, voice and quality. Which package includes each feature is set in chapter 6; prices for pay-per-video, minute packs, audio products and add-ons are in chapter 7.

Four rules apply to every feature in this chapter:

- Features are included in the package fee; generated video is billed per final minute at the package's rate (chapter 7). Where the AI writes new text, the pre-production fee applies (chapter 7).
- Nothing is charged before the creator approves the storyboard and its price; the money is then taken from the wallet (chapter 7).
- Every feature has an on/off switch and limits in the admin "Packages & features" matrix, with a global "coming soon" switch, two-person approval and a log (chapter 13).
- A listed feature is not live until it passes its acceptance test; features not ready at a release show "coming soon" (chapter 17).

## 5.1 Ordering and channels

### 5.1.1 Order by WhatsApp or Messenger voice note

Many small business owners in Bangladesh prefer to speak rather than type. They can order a video by sending a voice note, with photos if they wish, to Motion Studio's official WhatsApp or Messenger account.

- The AI transcribes the voice note, writes a brief and script, and builds a storyboard.
- A preview and a payment link are sent back in the same chat. Rendering starts only after the customer approves and pays.
- Photos sent with the order become reference or key images for their scenes and pass the content-safety checks (chapter 12).
- Voice notes are used only to understand the order. A voice is never cloned from a WhatsApp voice note; cloning happens only in the app (chapter 4).
- WhatsApp uses the WhatsApp Business Platform, which needs Meta Business Verification, a dedicated number and approved message templates; Messenger uses the Motion Studio Meta app. Both follow the approvals in chapter 12.
- An order is linked to the sender's Nexa ID account; a first-time sender is asked to sign up and verify the phone number (default, editable).

### 5.1.2 Pay-per-video and minute packs

Not every customer wants a subscription. Two options sit outside the packages:

- **Pay-per-video**: one video, paid by bKash, with no subscription. It is priced a little above package rates.
- **Minute packs**: 30-minute and 100-minute packs at 10–15% off.

Prices, validity and refund rules are in chapter 7.

### 5.1.3 WhatsApp-friendly copy

Every finished video automatically gets a small 360p copy that is quick to send on WhatsApp and to watch on slow mobile data. It counts toward storage like any other file (chapter 11).

### 5.1.4 Mobile app

An installable web app, later wrapped with Capacitor and shared with NexaTute, lets a customer order, preview, approve and publish from a phone. Rendering stays on the server or in a capable browser. The v32.10 plan dates it Q2 2027 (chapter 17).

### 5.1.5 Resellers

Motion Studio is sold through the RemonLogic Ecosystem Reseller Program as well as directly. The reseller discount applies to everything: package fees, per-minute rates and the Express surcharge. Pack sizes, discounts, KYC and wallet rules are in chapter 7.

## 5.2 Business tools

These tools turn Motion Studio from a video maker into a sales tool for small businesses, F-commerce sellers and agencies.

### 5.2.1 Video from a link

The creator pastes a link to a product page, blog post, Daraz listing or Shopify product. Motion Studio pulls the text, images and price automatically and writes a script and storyboard for approval.

- The creator confirms the page is their own or that they have permission to use it; the declaration is stored with the project (default, editable).
- Pages are read through the shared Document Intake Service and pass the same safety checks as uploads.
- Because the AI writes new text, the pre-production fee applies.

### 5.2.2 Product ad maker

A seller uploads product photos. The background is removed (rembg with BiRefNet, both MIT) and an ad video is made with the price, offer and an order button, ready for F-commerce. Templates help the seller start quickly.

### 5.2.3 Template library

Ready templates — product ads, Islamic reminders, news, education, birthday and Eid greetings — let a creator change the text and media and render. Templates are stored as Revideo scene files; no new software is needed. A template filled only with the creator's own text carries no pre-production fee.

### 5.2.4 Product-catalogue import

A shop imports its catalogue from Shopify, WooCommerce or a CSV file, and Motion Studio makes one ad video per product.

- Catalogue jobs render at night on the Slow tier, which keeps the price down and uses spare capacity.
- The creator approves the storyboards (singly or as a batch) before anything is charged.
- Prices and product names come from the imported data; the creator checks them in the storyboard.

### 5.2.5 Bulk personalised videos

One template plus a CSV file of customer names produces a separate video for each customer, with their name both written on screen and spoken.

- Made in Quick mode in the browser, so GPU cost stays low.
- Each video is sent by a WhatsApp or SMS link.
- The business confirms that it has its customers' consent to receive messages; WhatsApp opt-in rules and the Growth Platform's message rules apply (default, editable).
- Each video's minutes are billed at the package rate.

### 5.2.6 Business packs

Ready packs for common local businesses:

| Pack | What it makes |
|---|---|
| Real estate | Property photos turned into a 2.5D walkthrough |
| Restaurant | Menu videos with dishes and prices |
| Tours | Tour-package videos |
| Coaching centres | Course and admission videos |
| Clinics | Service and appointment videos |

> Licensed businesses (for example recruiting agencies and clinics) must show their licence number in the video. The pack asks for the number and places it on screen; a video from these packs is not exported without it (default, editable).

### 5.2.7 Voice-over-only audio product

Some customers need only audio: radio ads, shop announcements and IVR phone menus. They can order a voice-over without video, using the same voices, emotion controls and pronunciation dictionary as video (chapter 4). It is priced well below video (chapter 7).

### 5.2.8 End-screen order QR and "order now" button

The end screen can carry a QR code and an "order now" button that lead to the business's page, a WhatsApp order link or a bKash merchant QR. The business supplies the destination; Motion Studio does not take the customer's payment.

### 5.2.9 Per-video sales tracking

Each QR code and order link is unique to its video, so Motion Studio can show how many clicks and orders each video produced. Only counts are collected; no personal data of viewers is stored (default, editable).

### 5.2.10 Three hook variants and the winner

For an ad, Motion Studio can make three versions with different first-three-second hooks. Sales tracking shows which version brings the most clicks or orders, and the winner can then be boosted. Each variant's minutes are billed.

### 5.2.11 One-click Facebook and Instagram boost

The creator can boost a finished video on Facebook or Instagram from their own Meta ad account with one click.

- The creator pays Meta directly; RemonLogic never holds advertising money.
- It needs Meta's approval of the Motion Studio app for the advertising permission (chapter 12).

### 5.2.12 Pre-publish ad-policy check

Before an ad is published or boosted, the AI checks the script, captions and frames against Meta and Google advertising policies (for example banned claims, before-and-after images and restricted products) and explains any likely problem.

> The check is advice only. It reduces rejections but cannot guarantee that Meta or Google will approve an ad, and Motion Studio never claims that it does (default, editable).

### 5.2.13 Not offered

Two products are refused on purpose:

- AI "customer testimonial" videos — they would be fake reviews.
- Lyric videos of other people's songs — they infringe copyright.

## 5.3 Planning and growth

### 5.3.1 Monthly content calendar

The AI plans a month of posts around Bangladesh's days and seasons: 21 February, Pohela Boishakh, Ramadan and Eid, 16 December (Victory Day), the wedding season, Black Friday and the year-end recap.

- The creator reviews and approves the plan and each storyboard; nothing is charged before approval.
- Approved videos render at night on the Slow tier and are published automatically on the day through direct publishing (5.4).
- Festival rush is handled with an early-order discount and a capacity limit (chapter 7).

### 5.3.2 Trend ideas

Motion Studio suggests video and hook ideas from what is popular in Bangladesh, using only the official YouTube Data API. Trend ideas never override the safety rules: deepfakes, fake news and impersonation stay blocked (chapter 12).

### 5.3.3 Hooks, titles, hashtags and thumbnails

The AI suggests first-three-second hooks, titles, hashtags and thumbnail options (Qwen3 and FLUX.1-schnell, both already in the stack). Their results are compared later in the analytics (5.3.5). Bangla titles are overlaid as text, never drawn by the image model (chapter 4).

### 5.3.4 Long video to Shorts

A creator's own long video, uploaded or imported from their connected channel, is cut into several Reels or Shorts with captions and reframing. Moment selection uses the transcript (faster-whisper) and Qwen3; scene cuts use PySceneDetect; reframing uses MediaPipe. It carries no pre-production fee and is billed at the Quick-mode rate. Copyright controls for uploaded video apply (chapter 12).

### 5.3.5 Platform analytics with AI insights

YouTube and Facebook statistics for each published video — views, watch time, retention and engagement where the platform provides them — are pulled back into the Motion Studio dashboard.

- A basic view shows the numbers.
- The AI-insight view explains what worked (for example which hook held viewers) and suggests the next video.
- Sales tracking (5.2.9) and hook variants (5.2.10) appear in the same dashboard.

### 5.3.6 Creator challenge

A monthly creator challenge on a theme. Prizes are Motion Studio credits and a showcase on the homepage. A winning video is shown only with the creator's consent, and entries follow the normal safety rules.

### 5.3.7 Referral programme

Creators invite other creators and earn account credit. It uses the referral system shared with NexaTute. The credit amount is set in chapter 7.

### 5.3.8 NexaTute course: making videos with AI

NexaTute offers a skill course, "Making videos with AI", with a certificate. Learners who finish it get a Motion Studio trial or discount (amount to confirm). The course teaches the craft and brings trained users to Motion Studio.

### 5.3.9 Motion Studio's own marketing engine

Motion Studio markets itself with the shared RemonLogic tools, using its own brand, accounts, mailboxes and data:

- **Landing page and CMS** on remonlogicstudio.com, with a showreel made in Motion Studio, a "write an idea" box that makes a storyboard in the visitor's browser, and live prices read from the admin settings. The site shows the approved paid prices with "free during the Free Beta".
- **Drop-off funnel**: visit → sign-up → first project → first preview → first full render → download or publish → trial end → package purchase → second-month renewal, analysed by creator type, device, country and traffic source; a weekly AI analyst turns the findings into change requests.
- **AI Marketing Hub and CRM**: articles, showreels, Reels and outreach to agencies, small businesses and education and Islamic content organisations at their public business addresses; journeys for demo → application, webinar follow-up and inactive re-engagement.

> Honesty rules: no fake views, earnings promises or reviews; creator work is shown only with permission; marketplace income is shown only through real examples; our own websites never show advertising.

## 5.4 Publishing

### 5.4.1 Direct publishing

Creators connect their own accounts and publish or schedule finished videos straight from Motion Studio, with title, description, hashtags, thumbnail and captions.

| Platform | Account type | Status |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | Channel | At launch, after the API audit |
| Facebook | Page | At launch, after Meta App Review |
| Instagram Reels | Business or Creator account | At launch, same Meta app as Facebook |
| TikTok | Account | At launch, after the Content Posting API review |
| LinkedIn | Page or profile | After launch |

- Uploads use each platform's official API and follow its terms and reused-content rules.
- Connections use OAuth; Motion Studio never sees passwords. Tokens are encrypted and refreshed automatically.
- Failed uploads are retried where the API allows, and the creator is told the reason.
- Direct publishing is included in every paid package.

### 5.4.2 While approvals are pending

YouTube, TikTok and Meta approvals take time and need the live website first. Until each is approved, creators download the video and upload it themselves. TikTok posts made through an unaudited app stay private; if TikTok's branding rules exclude watermarked videos, direct posting to TikTok is for paid packages only. Approval steps are in chapter 12.

### 5.4.3 AI labels and Content Credentials

- Every export is signed with C2PA Content Credentials showing it was made with AI.
- Direct publishing ticks each platform's AI-disclosure option automatically for realistic AI content, cloned voices and digital twins.
- Cloned voices carry an inaudible watermark and an "AI voice" label.
- Kids-safe videos are marked "Made for kids" on YouTube automatically.
- Before publishing, creators are warned about platform "inauthentic content" and reused-content rules.

Legal positions on labelling are in chapter 12.

## 5.7 Accessibility

### 5.7.1 Captions

- Styled captions burned into the video, with word-by-word highlight and correct Bangla conjuncts, shaped with HarfBuzz and tested per font; Urdu and Arabic are right-to-left.
- Separate SRT and VTT caption files for platforms and players.
- Captions are made from the approved script and word timestamps, so they match the voice.

### 5.7.2 BdSL sign-language avatar corner

A signing avatar in a corner of the video translates the narration into Bangla Sign Language (BdSL) for deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers. It uses the BdSL avatar built for NexaTute and is drawn in the browser, so it adds no GPU cost. Signs are checked by a BdSL expert and a Deaf reviewer, as in NexaTute, before a sign is used (to confirm for Motion Studio).

### 5.7.3 Audio-description track

For blind and low-vision viewers, Motion Studio can add an audio-description track that describes the important visual action in the pauses between dialogue, as WCAG asks for prerecorded video. The description is written from the storyboard, so it matches what is on screen, and the creator approves it. It is delivered as a separate audio track or a separate described version.

### 5.7.4 Accessible studio

The studio interface is built to WCAG 2.2 Level AA: screen-reader labels, full keyboard use, sufficient contrast, large-font and high-contrast options, and automated accessibility tests in CI. A third-party audit with an Accessibility Conformance Report follows before any accessibility claim is published.

## 5.8 Interactive video and SCORM export

Interactive video adds buttons, chapters and quizzes to a finished video. It is for training, onboarding and courses.

- Creators add chapter markers, clickable buttons (for example "watch the next step" or "order now") and quiz questions at chosen moments.
- Interactive videos play in Motion Studio's own player and can be embedded on a website.
- **SCORM export** packages the interactive video for a company's own learning management system (LMS), so completion and quiz scores are recorded there.
- The same packages work in NexaTute, which already supports SCORM import, so companies and institutions can use Motion Studio videos as NexaTute course material.
- Any new library needed for SCORM packaging is checked for its licence and added to the software register first (to confirm).
