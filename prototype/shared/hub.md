## 4c. Past questions and centre-uploaded papers (v1.4; memory /areas/nexatute-hub.md last line)
Bangladesh board past questions (SSC/HSC/admission/BCS) are used verbatim inside lessons and NexaTute's own exams, each tagged with board + year (sample tags below); never shown as whole papers. Cambridge/Pearson past-paper questions are never published verbatim: O/A Level uses original syllabus-matched questions + links to the official sites. A school that is a Cambridge/Pearson centre may upload papers into its own private X12 area (family owns the upload screen and its confirmation block); learners of that school see them labelled `pq_school` — never mixed into NexaTute's question bank.
